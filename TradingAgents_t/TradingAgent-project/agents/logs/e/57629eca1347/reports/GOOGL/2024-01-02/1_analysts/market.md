**Analysis date: 2024-01-02 (close) | Reference price: $138.17**

---

## 1. Bottom Line

GOOGL enters 2024 in a **structurally bullish but tactically stalling** configuration. The primary uptrend is intact and well-supported (price 10.5% above a *rising* 200-day SMA, with the 50-day well above the 200-day). However, the December impulse leg has rolled into a **four-session distribution/pullback** that has taken price below its 10-day EMA, into a dense multi-month supply shelf at $138–$142, and on deteriorating volume. Momentum (MACD) has peaked and begun to roll; RSI has cooled from 65.7 to 54.9 without reaching oversold.

**Stance: constructive/trend-following bias, but not a fresh-momentum chase.** Favor buying defined pullbacks into the $136.5 and $133.5 clusters with ATR-based stops, rather than paying up into $139–$142 resistance. The highest-probability outcome over the next 2–4 weeks is continued consolidation between roughly $136.5 and $142, with the resolution — up through $142.7 or down through $133.6 — deciding the next directional leg.

## 8. Scenarios and Triggers

**A. Bullish continuation/re-break (probability ~40%).** Trigger: a daily close **above 139.72** on volume ≥ 30M (the 20-day average), ideally gapping over the October 24 pre-gap close of **138.81**. Confirmation: 10-EMA reclaim, MACD histogram re-expanding, RSI back over 60. Targets: **141.52 → 143.95**. This scenario re-rates the stock above the entire Sept–Oct supply shelf.

**B. Base-building consolidation (probability ~40%, base case).** Price chops between **136.5 and 141.5** for 1–3 weeks, letting the flat 50-SMA (133.60) catch up and resolving the volume divergence. ATR compression suggests the range boundary breaks within ~2–4 weeks. The constructive tell to monitor: successive higher lows (136.48 → then > 137) with rising VWMA.

**C. Trend-break pullback (probability ~20%).** Trigger: a daily close **below 136.48** (Jan 2 low) with expanding volume, i.e., a decisive loss of VWMA. This opens a measured move toward **135.89 (Boll mid) → 133.60 (50-SMA) → 132.43**. Note that the 50-SMA is only 3.4% below spot — even the bearish path is contained to a normal-trend pullback rather than a structural break, *unless* 132.43 fails.

**Longer-term note:** while the 200-SMA continues rising ~0.2/day, any pullback that holds above ~130 keeps the primary uptrend, the 50>200 alignment, and the 10.5% extension intact.

## 9. Actionable Plan

**Position-management view (for existing longs):** Hold. Trail stops on a **2×ATR (5.47-point) basis**; a logical protective level is **135.30–135.40** (1×ATR below the 136.5 VWMA/Jan-2-low cluster), which sits just above the Bollinger middle. A tighter tactical stop at **136.40** is acceptable for shorter holding periods but is inside ~1 ATR of noise.

**New-entry view (preferred):** do not initiate at 138.17 with RSI 54.9 and MACD rolling. Two staged entries:
- **Entry 1 — pullback buyer: $136.50–$137.00** (VWMA + Jan 2 low retest). Stop 133.40 (below 50-SMA/2×ATR). Target 1: 141.50. Target 2: 143.95. Risk 3.10–3.60, reward 4.50–7.45 → **R:R ≈ 1.4:1 to 2.1:1**.
- **Entry 2 — breakout buyer: $139.75–$140.00** on ≥30M volume. Stop 136.40 (below VWMA). Target 143.95 → R:R ≈ 1.2:1; only attractive if the volume condition is met.

**Sizing:** at ATR 2.734 (1.98% of price), a 2-ATR stop equals **3.96% of notional**. Size so that a 2-ATR loss = 0.5–1.0% of portfolio equity → roughly 13–25% of equity at risk-adjusted full weight per unit of 1% risk budget.

**Invalidation rules:** (a) Two consecutive closes below 136.48 → stand down on all long adds. (b) A daily close above 143.95 (upper band) with strong volume → upgrade to a momentum-chase regime and re-underwrite targets. (c) If MACD crosses below its signal *while* price is below the 10-EMA (138.66) → reduce gross exposure by half.
