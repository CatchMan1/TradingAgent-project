### 1. The dominant fact: an unfilled, high-volume earnings gap

The tape since late January is defined by one event. GOOGL closed at **153.51** on 2024-01-29 (intraday high 153.78, the highest print in the 2023-08-01→2024-02-20 dataset), then gapped from a **151.46** close on 2024-01-30 to a **143.62** open on 2024-01-31 and closed at **140.10** — a **−7.50%** single-session loss on **71.91M shares**, the largest volume bar in the entire dataset (roughly 2.2× the 20-session average of ~32.5M). *(The gap is consistent with an earnings-driven repricing; I am inferring the catalyst from the price/volume signature, not from a fundamentals tool.)*

What matters for a trader:

- The gap has **not** been filled. Price has not closed above 146.45 (the 2024-01-19 high, i.e., the last pre-gap consolidation top) since the break. Overhead supply is intact.
- 2024-02-02 printed a wide-range, high-volume reversal bar: **low 136.50 → close 142.38 on 62.50M shares**. That is the low of the post-gap structure and the single best-defined downside pivot from the event itself.
- From that 136.50 low the stock repaired to **149.00 on 2024-02-09** (intraday 149.44), then rolled over: **147.53 → 145.14 → 145.94 → 142.77 → 140.52 → 141.12**. Lower highs, flat-to-lower lows — a converging, descending consolidation (flag/triangle) sitting directly on the 50-day SMA.

So the stock is **not in freefall** (higher low vs. 136.50) and **not repaired** (eight sessions of lower highs). It is coiled at a decision point.

### 3. Momentum: the bearish impulse is *re-accelerating*, not decaying

This is the most important nuance in the whole dataset.

**MACD family (2024-02-20): macd 0.1286 | macds 1.0293 | macdh −0.9006**

- The MACD line crossed **below** its signal on 2024-01-31, when the histogram flipped from **+0.8241** (2024-01-30) to **−0.0771**. It has been negative for 15 straight sessions.
- Critically, the histogram **narrowed to −0.2390 on 2024-02-12** (a would-be bearish-momentum fade) and has since **re-expanded to −0.9006** — the most negative reading of the current leg, and getting worse each session (−0.3395 → −0.3489 → −0.5534 → −0.8094 → −0.9006). The attempted repair failed.
- The MACD line itself is at **0.1286 and falling** toward zero (−0.17/day). A **zero-line cross is imminent**. Historically that is a regime-change confirmation (bull momentum → bear momentum), not a mean-reversion signal. Combined with the histogram re-expansion, momentum is confirming the lower-high sequence rather than diverging from it.

**RSI (2024-02-20): 43.97**

- The round trip was violent: **78.25 on 2024-01-29 → 43.62 on 2024-01-31**. RSI has not recovered above 60 since (peak 60.31 on 2024-02-09), and has made a clean **lower-high sequence: 60.31 → 56.76 → 51.46 → 53.04 → 46.57 → 42.60 → 43.97**.
- At 43.97 RSI is **below the 50 midline but nowhere near oversold (30)**. This is the key nuance: there is **no oversold cushion**. Downside has room before RSI generates a mean-reversion bid. Conversely, upside has room before overbought resistance. RSI is a *permission slip*, not a signal, right now.
- I want to be explicit: I do **not** see a valid bullish RSI divergence. The 2024-02-16 low (close 140.52 / low 140.14) vs. the 2024-02-02 low (136.50) is a *higher* price low with a *lower* RSI (42.60 vs. whatever the 2/2 reading implied) — that is weak, not bullish, and the 2/20 bounce lifted RSI only to 43.97. Don't trade a divergence that isn't there.

- The **lower band has risen 4.51 points while the upper band is flat/slightly lower**. That is classic post-shock compression: the bands are narrowing around a falling price, and the price (141.12) sits **in the lower third of the envelope** (3.33% below the 145.98 midline, 2.40% above the 137.72 lower band).
- **The squeeze/ATR tension is the tell.** Bollinger width is contracting 23.6%, but **ATR is expanding**: 2.7004 (2024-01-30) → **3.1833 (2024-02-20), +17.9%**. Width contracting while true range expands means the market is building energy inside a tightening envelope with wide daily bars — an unstable condition that resolves in a directional expansion. Bias within a squeeze is normally set by the position relative to the middle band, and price is **below** the middle band and below the 20-day basis. That biases the resolution lower until proven otherwise.
- The 20-day middle (145.98) being **above** the 50-day (141.70) is another short-term-over-medium-term inversion — a fresh bearish signature that would need 146+ to unwind.

### 5. Volume and VWMA: distribution, not accumulation

**VWMA (2024-02-20): 143.182**, versus price **141.12** (−1.44%).

- VWMA peaked at **146.937 on 2024-01-30** and has declined for six consecutive sessions (145.772 → 145.576 → 145.214 → 144.630 → 143.952 → 143.182). It is now **below the 10 EMA (143.90)** and **above price**. Translation: the *volume-weighted average buyer since mid-January is underwater*. Every rally into 143–146 is hitting the average cost of trapped longs — that is the definition of overhead supply.
- VWMA > price > 50 SMA on a declining price is a **distribution** configuration, not accumulation.

### 7. Actionable scenarios

**Base case (highest probability): range-bound chop 139–146 with a downward skew.** The MACD histogram re-expansion, sub-50 RSI with no oversold cushion, declining VWMA above price, and light-volume bounce all point to continued base-building/erosion rather than a V-recovery. This is a "do less" regime for trend-following.

**Bullish trigger — requires two confirmations, not one:**
1. A **daily close above 143.90** (10 EMA) that also clears **145.98** (Bollinger middle) *within the same week*; and
2. **Volume ≥ ~38M** (≈116% of the 20-day average) on the breakout bar.
Then: initial target **149.00–149.44**, secondary **153.51–153.78**. Stop below **140.10** (≈1.2×ATR from a 145 entry would be tighter than the structure — prefer the structural stop at 140.10 and size accordingly). Nothing about the current tape justifies buying *before* those confirmations; a close between 141.70 and 143.90 is noise inside a flag.

**Bearish trigger (the squeeze resolution I'd lean toward):**
1. A **daily close below 139.49**, ideally accompanied by volume > 35M; or
2. A **close below the 50 SMA (141.70) for a third consecutive session** *while* the MACD line completes its zero-line cross.
Then: target **137.72** (lower band), then **136.50** (post-gap low). A break of 136.50 opens the pre-earnings-to-post-earnings air pocket toward the 200 SMA zone at 131.60. Stop above **144.00** (~1.5×ATR), which sits above the 10 EMA and below the VWMA.

**Invalidation of both:** a decisive reclaim of **146.45** (the 2024-01-19 high, pre-gap top) would mark the gap as a completed shakeout and shift the framework back to trend-following long with the 50>200 structure reasserting.

**What I would explicitly *not* do:** short the 141 area into support without a break (the 2024-02-20 failed breakdown at 139.49 shows dip-buyers are present); buy the 141 area expecting a fast gap fill (light volume, negative histogram, no RSI divergence); or treat the +7.2% premium to the 200 SMA as a "buy the dip" signal on its own — that premium was **~16%** on 2024-01-29 and has been compressing, which is mean-reversion *against* the longs, not for them.
