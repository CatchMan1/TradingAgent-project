The aggressive analyst is right that the bounce is unconfirmed and the near-term reward-to-risk is poor. The conservative analyst is right that half weight is still real risk in this volatility regime. But the correct answer is not to pick one extreme; it is to size GOOGL so the firm survives a failed retest without abandoning the structural upside.

To the aggressive analyst: you correctly point out that GOOGL is below the 10 EMA at 145.12, below VWMA at 145.42, and below the Bollinger mid at 144.94, and that the Feb 5 bounce came on 38.51M shares versus 62.50M on Feb 2 and 71.91M on Jan 31. A 35–40% standard allocation after the trim, with clear tiered exits below 142.78, 141.16/140.79, and 136.02, and a stricter rebuild sequence above 145.50, is the most sustainable middle path.
