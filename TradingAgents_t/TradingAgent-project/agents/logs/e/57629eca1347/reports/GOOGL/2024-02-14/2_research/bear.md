
Bear Analyst: **Bear case: GOOGL — Avoid/Underweight at $145.94**

Bull, I’ll grant the easy part: **GOOGL** is a high-quality franchise with a fortress balance sheet and real margin progress in FY23. But that is not the same as a good investment at today’s price. My case is not “Alphabet is broken.” My case is that **GOOGL at 145.94 already discounts a lot of good news while the freshest evidence — Q4 cash flow, capex, momentum, volume, and risk/reward — is deteriorating.** The bull is asking investors to pay for a recovery that has not been confirmed.

### 1. The Q4 cash-flow scare is not just “timing” — it is the forward run-rate

The bull waves away Q4 FCF as capex and working-capital noise. But the market discounts forward cash flow, and Q4 is the freshest read we have:

- Q4 operating cash flow: **$18.9B, −19.9% YoY**
- Q4 capex: **$11.0B, +45.1% YoY**
- Q4 free cash flow: **$7.9B vs $16.0B, −50.7% YoY**
- Q4 buybacks: **$16.2B** — more than **2x** Q4 FCF

Full-year FCF grew 15.8%, yes, but that masks the inflection: **capex is accelerating and management’s 2024 commentary points higher, not lower.** The bull says capex is “capacity for Cloud/AI.” Maybe. But investors are being asked to underwrite the return on that capex before seeing it in FCF. That is not a free option; it is a risk.

And the working-capital boost was real: accounts payable rose **46.1%**, accrued liabilities **21.9%**, receivables rose **19.1%** versus revenue **+8.7%**, and DSO moved from roughly **52 to 57 days**. That flattered FY23 OCF. Strip SBC of **$22.1B**, and FCF ex-SBC was roughly **$47.4B**, or **15.4% of revenue** — still good, but not the “22.6% FCF margin” headline the bull wants to lean on.

**Bull counter:** “Buybacks can flex.”  
Exactly — flexing buybacks means **slower EPS support**. FY23 buybacks were **88.5% of FCF**; a repeat is not automatic if capex pushes toward the mid-to-high $40Bs. That removes one of the bull’s own core supports.

### 2. Earnings quality and valuation are less attractive than they look

FY23 EPS grew **27.2%**, but that was helped by three things the bull is underweighting:

- Buybacks: share count fell **3.0%**
- A low effective tax rate: **13.9%** versus a 21% statutory rate
- Deferred tax benefit: roughly **$7.7B**

Normalizing the tax rate to 16–17% could knock roughly **3% off EPS** — small in isolation, but at **~25x FY23 EPS** and **~23x ex-cash**, a 3% EPS haircut plus an FCF miss is enough to compress the multiple. This is a company growing revenue **8.7%** with capex rising and FCF growth at risk. That is not a screaming valuation; it is a full one.

The bull also points to Cloud. I agree the Cloud inflection is encouraging — roughly **$33.1B revenue, +26%**, with its first full-year operating profit around **$1.7B**. But that is roughly a **~5% operating margin** on a business that is still small relative to Search. It is not yet large enough to offset ad-market maturity, and the mix shift toward Cloud actually caps consolidated gross-margin expansion even as Cloud improves. The bull is treating optionality as if it were already in the earnings stream.

### 3. The technical picture is not “just neutral” — it is a failed repair with distribution risk

The bull keeps saying the primary trend is intact. Fine: price is above the rising **50 SMA at 141.01** and **200 SMA at 131.07**. But that only tells us the long-term trend has not broken; it does not tell us the next 4–8 weeks are attractive.

Look at the tape:

- **2024-01-31 gap: 151.46 → 140.10 close, −7.50%, on 71.91M shares** — roughly 3x normal volume. That gap remains **unfilled**.
- The stock lost **7.25%** close-to-close from Jan 29 to Feb 2 and has retraced only about half.
- The rebound failed at **149.44** and has slipped back below the **10 EMA at 145.99**.
- The Feb 14 bounce came on **22.7M shares**, about **28% below** the 10-day average. That is not re-accumulation; it is low-conviction repair.
- MACD is still bearish-crossed: **1.246 vs 1.595 signal, histogram −0.349**, and the histogram has re-widened from **−0.239** on Feb 12. The bull says “MACD is above zero” — true, but that does not negate an open bearish crossover and deteriorating momentum.
- VWMA is **145.21**, essentially at spot. The bull calls that “balanced.” I call it overhead supply: recent holders are at break-even, not sitting on gains, and the February volume shows sellers have been at least as active as buyers.

The bull’s “higher low” at **143.69** versus **136.50** is one print, not a confirmed reversal. The stock is still below the gap origin and below the failed rally high. A close below **143.69** opens the **140.10–142.38 / 50 SMA 141.01** confluence; below that, **139.15** and then **136.50** come into play.

And the bull’s tactical math is weak: from **145.94** with a 1-ATR stop near **142.87**, the first target at **149.44** is only about **3.5 points** of upside versus **3.1 points** of risk — roughly **1.1:1** before confirmation. If you wait for the bull’s add-on trigger at **149.44** and use **143.69** as the stop, you are risking about **5.75 points** to reach **151.46–153.78**, which is not compelling either. That is not a margin of safety; that is paying for hope in the middle of a range.

### 4. The bull’s “franchise intact” challenge is the wrong hurdle

Bull, you say I have to prove the franchise, margins, Cloud, and balance sheet are impaired. No — I don’t. **A stock can underperform for quarters while the franchise remains excellent if cash conversion slows, capex rises, the tax tailwind fades, and the multiple compresses.**

The **~$95B net cash** is a cushion, not a catalyst. It is only around **5% of the market cap** and does not justify a 25x multiple on pressured FCF. The long-term trend can remain intact while GOOGL falls **3.4% to the 50 SMA near 141**, **4.7% to the lower Bollinger band near 139.15**, or **6.4% to 136.50** — all without breaking the 200-day uptrend. That is downside within a bull trend.

### Bottom line for GOOGL

At **145.94**, the risk/reward is unattractive:

- Q4 FCF fell **51%** and capex is inflecting higher
- Buybacks are already **88.5% of FCF**
- The tax rate flattered EPS and is likely to normalize
- Receivables and payables raise cash-quality questions
- The Jan 31 gap is unfilled, MACD is still bearish-crossed, and the bounce is low-volume
- The stock is pinned at the 10 EMA/VWMA cluster with the decision rails at **143.69** and **149.44**

**My stance: GOOGL — Avoid/Underweight at current price.** Existing holders should trim or tighten stops below **143.69**. New money should wait for either a deeper reset near the **141** confluence with stabilization, or a confirmed daily close above **149.44** on **>30M shares** with a MACD bullish crossover. Until then, the bear case wins on forward cash flow, earnings quality, momentum, and risk/reward.
