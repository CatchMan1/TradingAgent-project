AMZN is still above the rising 50-SMA, still far above the rising 200-SMA, and still holding the 143.55–144.64 cluster. Where I land is that the trader’s HOLD is directionally right, but the phrase “standard allocation weight” needs to be made event-aware rather than treated as a fixed 25–30% single-name target. At 145.24, with resistance at 149.10–150.88 only 2.7% to 3.9% above spot and support only about 1% below, the spot risk/reward does not justify new capital. The aggressive analyst is correct that we should not confuse a bad entry with a bad position.
