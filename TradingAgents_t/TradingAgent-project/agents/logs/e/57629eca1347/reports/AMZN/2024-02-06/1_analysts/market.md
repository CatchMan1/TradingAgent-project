It is shallow (<2% off the high) and occurring at the top of the range, which is stalling rather than reversal behaviour — but it does argue against chasing strength at 169–172.

**Bollinger(20,2): middle 157.96 / upper 169.89 / lower 146.03** (bandwidth 15.1% of the middle band).
- Price at 169.15 sits at **~97% of the band range** (%B), i.e. pressed against the upper band. On **2024-02-02 the close of 171.81 was 3.3% ABOVE that day's upper band (166.33)** — a genuine volatility breakout print, not just a touch.
- The upper band is **expanding aggressively** (163.10 on 02-01 → 166.33 → 168.28 → **169.89**), which is the classic signature of a trending, "walking-the-band" market. In this state, band touches are continuation cues, and shorting the upper band is a low-probability trade until the band flattens.
- The failure mode to watch: price **fails to reclaim 170–172 for several sessions while the upper band keeps rising** — then the band rolls over and the mid-band (currently **157.96**, rising ~1.4/day) becomes the magnet. Note the mid-band is *below* the gap top (159.76–167.33), so a full gap fill would roughly coincide with a return to the mean.

## 7. Actionable interpretation & trade construction

**Bias: bullish trend continuation, but not at market.** The evidence is overwhelming on trend (stacked averages, widening 50/200 spread, MACD expansion, VWMA confirmation, band walk) and mildly cautionary on timing (RSI 68, 10.9% above the 50 SMA, 97%B, three declining closes).

1. **Preferred entry: buy the pullback, not the print.** The 163–161 zone (10 EMA 163.11 and VWMA 160.82) is the highest-quality risk/reward area — it is above the gap top, so the breakout thesis is intact, and it gives roughly 2×ATR of room to the invalidation. Scale in two tranches (≈163 and ≈161).
2. **Breakout continuation entry:** a daily close above **172.50** on volume >50M re-asserts upside momentum with target discovery above the dataset's high; use 167.3–168.0 (gap-day low / rising upper band) as the stop reference, i.e. ~4.5 points ≈ 1.2×ATR — tight, so size accordingly.
3. **Stop discipline:** the thesis is *structurally* wrong below **159.76** (gap top). Anyone long from here should accept a stop in the **159.5–161.5** band (1.0–1.25×ATR below the 163/161 entries). A close inside the 159.76–167.33 gap converts the setup from "breakaway gap" to "exhaustion/ordinary gap," and the base case shifts to the mid-band at **157.96**.
4. **Take-profit / trim logic:** trim partial size if RSI pushes above ~75 **while the MACD histogram contracts** (currently expanding at 1.298, so no such signal yet). First objective is a test of 172.50; a measured continuation beyond that projects to the high-170s given the 7.6-point gap and 15.1% band width, but treat that as scenario, not forecast.
5. **Position sizing:** with ATR at 2.27% of price, a 1% account-risk per trade implies a position sized on a ~6–8 point stop, i.e. materially smaller than a January-volatility sizing model would have allowed (ATR was ~2.9 then, 3.8 now).
6. **Do not fade the upper band.** The 02-02 close above the upper band by 3.3% plus 117M volume is a volatility-expansion signal. Mean-reversion shorts against a walking band have poor odds until (a) the band flattens and (b) a close occurs back below the 10 EMA.

**Two-sided watch list:** *Invalidation of the near-term bull case* = close below 167.33 followed by a failure to reclaim, or a MACD histogram flip negative (currently +1.298, so monitor daily). *Confirmation of continuation* = a higher high above 172.50 accompanied by volume returning above ~55–60M, with RSI making a matching new high (which would erase the nascent negative divergence).
