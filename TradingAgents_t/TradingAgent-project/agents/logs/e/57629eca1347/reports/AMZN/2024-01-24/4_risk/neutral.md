Missing sentiment, news, and insider data argues for smaller size, not zero participation.

On the Q4 binary, the aggressive analyst is right that the easy year-over-year comparison and structural margin story create upside potential, but the conservative analyst is right that the easy comp is known and the bar is high after Q3’s 7.82% operating margin. The correct response is to separate the longer-term AMZN thesis from the pre-event trade. Keep the thesis, but do not let it justify oversized event risk.
