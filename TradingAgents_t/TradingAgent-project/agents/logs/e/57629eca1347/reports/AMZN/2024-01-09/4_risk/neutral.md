The trader’s plan should be read as a risk-reduction exercise, not as a bearish call and not as a disguised aggressive trade. At $151.37, AMZN is sitting under the $155.63 to $156.63 supply zone, MACD is still below its signal line, the rebound volume is not confirmed, and Q4 earnings arrive before the two decisive variables, AWS margin and 2024 capex guidance, can be observed. It does not support shorting AMZN, and it does not support treating the next technical trigger as a reason to go to 1.25x or 1.5x standard.

The $144 to $145 retest may offer attractive reward-to-risk if it truly holds on a reversal candle, but it is also a falling-knife setup just above structural failure. A partial rebuild is defensible; a 1.5x “loaded gun” into an unmodeled AWS and capex print is not neutral risk management.

The conservative analyst is right to protect capital and right to reject the aggressive analyst’s pre-earnings 1.25x to 1.5x adds. The conservative also correctly points out that missing data is risk: no AWS segment P&L, no Q4 actual, no insider data, no sentiment or news report. But the conservative case risks becoming too absolute. The balanced adjustment to the trader’s AMZN plan is this. Keep the action as a Sell only in the sense of a trim or rebalance. If the book is full-size or overweight, reduce to 0.5x standard. Portfolio context is not provided, so these are sizing ratios to apply to the caller’s own book, not dollar amounts.

For rebuilding, I would moderate both sides. After earnings, if the close above $156.63 holds and AWS margin and capex guidance confirm rather than contradict the bull case, then rebuilding toward standard weight is reasonable. Only after the event risk is passed, the technical breakout is holding, and portfolio-level risk allows it would I consider a modest overweight in AMZN. A MACD re-cross above the signal line is useful confirmation, but it should not be the primary trigger by itself.

AMZN should be trimmed to half-size at $151.37, no new long should be initiated at current levels, and the residual position should be held for the possibility that the long-term trend and fundamental improvement reassert themselves.
