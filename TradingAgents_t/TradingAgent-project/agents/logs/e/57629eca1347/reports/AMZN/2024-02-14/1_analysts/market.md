## 7. Why These 8 Indicators Were Selected
The set balances the four analytical dimensions without redundancy:
- **Trend (3):** `close_200_sma` (primary trend), `close_50_sma` (medium-term + golden-cross), `close_10_ema` (actionable short-term dynamic support). Different horizons, complementary roles.
- **Momentum (2):** `macd` (trend/momentum inflection, crossovers) and `rsi` (overbought/oversold + divergence). Chosen instead of a redundant second oscillator like StochRSI.
- **Volatility (2):** `boll_ub` (overbought/breakout envelope and band-walk context) and `atr` (volatility-scaled stops and position sizing — critical given the post-earnings range expansion).
- **Volume (1):** `vwma` (confirms the trend with volume weighting, validating the earnings-gap breakout).

Together they answer the trader's core questions: *Is the trend up? (yes) Is momentum still supporting it? (cooling but intact) Is it extended? (not yet) Is volume confirming? (yes on breakout, fading in consolidation) and Where is risk defined? (165.75 / 50-SMA).*
