## 4. Volatility: compressed, which cuts both ways

**ATR(14) = 2.997**, or **1.94% of price**. That is *down* from 3.50 on 2023‑11‑24 and 3.45 on 2023‑12‑18. Bollinger bands are correspondingly tight: upper 158.16, lower 145.84, a width of 12.31 on a 152.00 mid — **8.1% bandwidth**.

Two implications:

1. **Tight stops are hazardous.** A 1×ATR stop from 154.78 is 151.78 — *inside* the normal daily noise and barely below the VWMA. Any stop should be at least 1.5–2×ATR (≈4.50–6.00 points) to avoid being shaken out by routine chop. A 2×ATR stop lands at ≈148.78, which conveniently coincides with the 50 SMA at 148.67.
2. **Volatility compression precedes expansion.** With ATR at a two-month low and price coiling between 151.3 (VWMA) and 157.2 (Jan high), the next directional resolution is likely to be larger than the recent average daily range. That argues for *waiting for confirmation* rather than pre-positioning aggressively.

Price is **3.38 points below the upper band (158.16)** and **8.94 above the lower band (145.84)** — nothing close to a band-tag extreme, so neither a breakout-squeeze nor a mean-reversion signal is active today.

## 6. Actionable levels and scenarios

**Resistance**
- **157.05–157.17** — the 01‑22 high and the 01‑11 high, the literal double‑top pivot. A *close* above 157.17 on volume ≥ 55M (i.e., meaningfully above the ~45M January average) is the breakout trigger.

**Support (in order)**
- **153.90** — 01‑22 low; **153.18** — 10 EMA. First line.
- **152.00** — 20 SMA / Bollinger mid; **151.29** — VWMA. The key shelf. A close below 151.29 breaks the volume-weighted trend for the first time since late October.
- **148.67** — 50 SMA, ~2×ATR below spot. This is the trend‑defining level; a weekly close below it invalidates the medium-term uptrend.
- **145.84** — Bollinger lower band.

**Trading bias — constructive but not chasing.** Evidence supports a *buy-the-dip-within-uptrend* posture rather than a breakout chase:
- Bullish: perfect MA stack, rising 50/200, price above VWMA, MACD re-crossed positive, RSI 59 with no overbought exhaustion, ATR compression in an uptrend.
- Bearish/offsetting: rejected breakout at 157.17, MACD and RSI lower highs vs. price higher high (divergence), below-average volume on the advance, flat 10 EMA and flat VWMA slope.

**Concrete plan**
1. **Do not chase above 157.** The 01‑22 candle already tested that zone and failed. Require a daily close above 157.17 with volume > 1.2× the ~45M January average before treating it as a breakout.
2. **Preferred entry zone: 151.3–152.0** (VWMA/20 SMA confluence), scaling in only if price stabilizes there rather than slicing through.
3. **Stop discipline:** 2×ATR ≈ 6.00 points; a stop at 148.60–148.80 places risk just under the 50 SMA where it is structurally meaningful rather than arbitrary noise.
4. **Position sizing:** with ATR at 1.94% of price, a 1% portfolio risk budget supports a position roughly 50% of notional-equity equivalent per unit of account risk — i.e., size off the 6.0‑point stop, not off a fixed share count.
5. **What would change the view to bearish:** a close below 151.29 (VWMA) followed by a break of 148.67 (50 SMA), especially if accompanied by volume above 50M. A close below 145.84 (lower band) would confirm the compression resolved lower.
6. **Event risk:** a December-fiscal-year Q4 report typically lands in the first days of February — just outside this data set. Volatility expansion at ATR's two-month low into an event window is a two-sided risk; consider trimming size rather than widening stops.
