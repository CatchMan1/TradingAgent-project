What this actually tells a trader
  1. FY2023 was Apple's first annual revenue decline since FY2019 (−2.8%) — but it was a mix story, not a demand collapse: gross margin expanded 82 bp to a record 44.1%. Price/mix and Services weighting more than offset volume weakness in hardware. Quality of revenue improved while quantity shrank.
