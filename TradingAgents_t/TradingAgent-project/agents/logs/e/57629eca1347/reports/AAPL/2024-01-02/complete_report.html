1. Company Profile / Business Model Snapshot

Apple Inc. (AAPL) designs, manufactures and markets smartphones (iPhone), personal computers (Mac), tablets (iPad), wearables/home/accessories (Watch, AirPods, Vision), and monetizes a large installed base through Services (App Store, iCloud, Apple Music/TV+, advertising, AppleCare, payments).

What the FY2023 filings quantitatively reveal about the model:

  • Extremely high gross margin, low capital intensity. FY23 gross margin 44.1% on $383.3B revenue while capex was only $10.96B (2.9% of revenue). Apple outsources manufacturing, so PP&E net is a small share of the $352.6B balance sheet.
  • Massive capital return, not reinvestment, is the primary use of cash. FY23: $77.55B buybacks + $15.03B dividends = $92.6B returned, ~93% of free cash flow.
  • Balance sheet is net-cash and structurally under-levered relative to cash generation. ~$162B cash + marketable securities vs. ~$111B total debt → roughly +$51B net cash, against $110.5B annual operating cash flow.
  • Revenue is cyclical/flat at the top line but margin-expanding — FY23 revenue fell 2.8% yet gross margin rose ~80bp, and EPS still grew because of a 3.1% smaller diluted share count.
  • SBC is a real but modest cost: $10.83B in FY23 (2.8% of revenue), with $18.6B of unrecognized non-vested award cost still to be expensed.
