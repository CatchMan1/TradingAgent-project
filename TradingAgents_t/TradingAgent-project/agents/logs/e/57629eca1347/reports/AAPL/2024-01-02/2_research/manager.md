**Rationale**: Both sides did real work here, but they were answering different questions, and the question that matters for a position decision is the bear's: is AAPL at 185.64 on 2024-01-02 a good place to commit capital? On that, the bear won.

Downside magnets are 183.89, then 179.64, then the ~177 measured move — about 3.2% and 4.6% away. The first realistic upside is ~192, about 3.5% away, into sellers. (3) The fundamental arithmetic is decelerating and the multiple gives no cover. Revenue −2.8%, operating income −4.3%, opex +6.8% against gross profit −1.0% (operating margin compressed 50bp), FCF −10.6%, buybacks −13.3% to $77.55B, and EPS +0.3% manufactured entirely by a ~3.1% share-count reduction. Quality does not prevent a de-rating.

The bull's strongest counter — FQ1 FY24 is an unreported catalyst — cuts against him, not for him. As of 2024-01-02 that print does not exist in the record, so it is a binary event, not evidence. You cannot justify adding into a high-volume outside-lower-band break on the hope of a print you have not seen, at 30× trailing earnings that are not priced for a disappointing holiday quarter. The bull's constructive structure keeps this out of Sell territory; the bear's entry-level arithmetic and overhead supply keep it out of Hold.

Net: quality company, poor entry, decelerating growth arithmetic, rich multiple. Trimming and refusing new money until the tape repairs is the disciplined expression of that.
