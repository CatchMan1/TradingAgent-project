That is a rich multiple for a company with negative revenue growth, negative operating leverage, slowing buybacks, and zero organic EPS growth. The balance sheet does not protect against multiple compression. Quality does not prevent a de-rating.

There are other quiet headwinds, too: the FY23 tax rate of **14.7%** versus **16.2%** in FY22 flattered EPS; normalization is a headwind. But they all point the same way: the easy EPS story is not getting easier.
