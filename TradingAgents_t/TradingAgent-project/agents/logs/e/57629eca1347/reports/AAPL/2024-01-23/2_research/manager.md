**Rationale**: The bear won this round, and it won on the fundamentals rather than on the tape — which is why the call is a trim rather than a full exit, but it is still a clear directional call.

The decisive exchange was on earnings quality. The bull opened with "record EPS ($6.13), record gross margin (44.1%), 156% ROE." The same applies to the 156% ROE — $97.0B of net income over a $62.1B equity base that years of repurchases have shrunk is a thin cushion, not proof of superior incremental returns.
