**Recommendation**: Underweight

**Rationale**: The bull made a credible franchise-quality case: AAPL’s Q1 FY2024 showed real operating leverage, with revenue +2.1%, gross profit +9.0%, operating income +12.1%, EPS +16.0%, Q1 FCF of $37.5B, net cash of $64.6B, and continued buybacks. The rising 200-day SMA and the 180.17–181.35 support shelf also argue against treating this as a broken long-term story. But the bear won the stock-level risk/reward debate. AAPL closed below a still-rising 200-day SMA for the first time in the visible dataset, on a fifth consecutive lower close, with a clear sequence of lower highs. The bounce into February 5–9 came on declining volume, while the February 13–15 decline came on heavier volume, and price sits below the 186.18 VWMA—evidence of overhead supply and distribution rather than accumulation. MACD’s histogram is re-accelerating negative, RSI made a lower low without bullish divergence, and the support shelf has been repeatedly tested rather than decisively defended. The bear also exposed the weakest part of the bull case: annualizing the seasonally strongest December quarter to arrive at ~21x is not a conservative valuation. Using FY2023 diluted EPS of $6.13, AAPL is roughly 29.7x trailing earnings while revenue is growing only ~2%, and the margin expansion was helped by a 3.1% absolute decline in COGS. FY2023 was a down year, and flat R&D, rising SBC, and foreign-tax exposure are genuine quality watch items. The balance sheet prevents distress but is not a near-term catalyst. The bull’s proposed stop below 179.25 is also too tight relative to the 3.18 ATR. This is not a Sell call because AAPL remains a high-quality, net-cash-rich franchise with a rising long-term trend and a real support zone, but the bear’s technical, volume, and valuation arguments are stronger for the current setup. Hence Underweight, not Hold.

**Strategic Actions**: Trim AAPL to roughly 0.5x a standard allocation (a 50% reduction from a full-weight position; 0.25–0.5x for risk-controlled accounts). Do not add to the 180–182 shelf before confirmation. Use strength into the 185.7–186.2 confluence (10 EMA/VWMA) to execute further trims if the position is still above target. For the remaining core, use a daily close below 179.25 as the trigger to cut to 0.25x rather than a tight intraday stop, given the 3.18 ATR. If the mandate permits tactical hedging, sell rallies into 185.7–186.2 with targets of 180.9 and then 179.3, and define invalidation as a daily close above 188.7; otherwise express the view through trimming rather than new leverage. Rebuild toward a standard allocation only on a high-volume reclaim of 183.48 followed by a hold above 186.18, ideally with the MACD histogram turning positive; a Q2 FY2024 print showing gross margin at or above 45% and revenue re-acceleration would also justify revisiting the Underweight stance. Do not short through the 180–181 support shelf without confirmation, and do not assume 179.25 will hold on a second test.
