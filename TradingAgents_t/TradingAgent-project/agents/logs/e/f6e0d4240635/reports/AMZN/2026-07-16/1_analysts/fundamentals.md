## 1. Company Profile (context)

Amazon.com, Inc. is a global technology and e-commerce conglomerate operating across **three reporting segments** (the 10-Q confirms `NumberOfOperatingSegments = 3`): **North America**, **International**, and **Amazon Web Services (AWS)**. Revenue streams span online and physical retail, third-party seller services, advertising, subscription services, and cloud/compute infrastructure (AWS). The company has evolved into a major AI/accelerated-computing infrastructure builder — a theme that dominates the current financial picture (see capex). It also holds large non-marketable equity stakes (reflected in the jump in "equity securities without readily determinable fair value"), whose revaluation is materially affecting reported earnings.

## 7. Actionable Insights for Traders

1. **Own the operating story, discount the headline EPS.** Revenue +16.6% and operating income +29.6% with +130 bps margin expansion is a genuinely bullish core-operating signal — AWS/AI monetization and advertising efficiency appear to be firing. But **~$12B of Q1 net income is a non-cash private-stake revaluation**; do not extrapolate the $2.78 diluted EPS or the +77% net-income growth as recurring run-rates.

2. **Free cash flow turning negative is the key risk to monitor.** TTM FCF is now roughly breakeven-to-negative ($−2.5B) as capex ($151B TTM) exceeds OCF ($148.5B). If the AI-capex ramp continues without a matching acceleration in AWS/operating-cash growth, this pressures the historically "FCF-compounding" valuation narrative. **Watch the next earnings for FCF stabilization and capex guidance.**

3. **Re-leveraging is a two-sided flag.** LT debt nearly doubled in one quarter to $122.6B and $53.4B was raised in Q1. Liquidity is strong (~$144B cash+securities), so solvency is not a near-term concern — but rising interest expense (+48% YoY) and the funding of capex with debt means leverage metrics will keep deteriorating. Rising rates are now a more meaningful headwind than in the zero-debt 2023–24 era.

4. **Private-mark valuation risk is now material.** The $48.1B "equity securities without readily determinable FV" and the $12.3B upward adjustment reflect large embedded AI-ecosystem gains. In a risk-off or AI-valuation reset, these marks could reverse and hit reported net income — a source of earnings volatility not visible in the operating business.

5. **Insider signal unavailable — do not assume.** The vendor returned no point-in-time insider-transaction archive for AMZN. No insider-buying/selling inference should be drawn from this report.

6. **Net trading stance (fundamentals-only):** Constructive on **operations and margin trajectory**, cautious on **cash-flow quality, rising leverage, and earnings quality**. For momentum/quality traders the operating acceleration supports the long thesis; for value/FCF-focused traders the negative FCF and debt-funded capex are a reason to demand a margin of safety or wait for confirmation of FCF inflection. A prudent trim/hedge posture on AI-capex/FCF concerns while remaining core-long on the AWS/advertising margin story is a defensible framing.
