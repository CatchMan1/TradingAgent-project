Net: the business case is decided for the bulls, the entry case is decided for the bear, and those are not the same question. So I'm constructive on AMZN but sized and timed to the bear's asymmetry math: accumulate on the bull's defined triggers, do not chase $272.26, and treat the negative FCF and rising leverage as tripwires rather than thesis-killers.
