**Recommendation**: Underweight

**Rationale**: Honest verdict: the bear won this one, but not by a knockout — so we trim AMZN, we don't exit.

The argument that decided it is the multiple. The bull's §5 rested entirely on "21.3x forward, one turn above the S&P." The bear did the arithmetic: $262.07 ÷ 21.3 = ~$12.30, which is essentially the reported TTM EPS ($135.3B / ~10.89B shares ≈ $12.4) — the number propped up by the $62.8B equity mark-up the bull had just told the room to discard. Recompute on the bull's own adjusted figures ($1.96 in Q2, $3.68 in H1 → ~$7.3–7.8 annualized) and AMZN is 33–36x clean earnings, roughly fifteen turns above the index, with negative free cash flow. You cannot use one EPS base for the growth claim and a different one for the divisor. That doesn't make AMZN a bad business — it makes the "asymmetry" claim false, and the asymmetry was the load-bearing wall of the bull case.

Three further bear points carried real weight. First, FCF after acquisitions is −$53.6B, and the bull's "$173B capex" framing conveniently skipped $39.8B of H1 M&A and a $106.1B six-month build in non-marketable equity securities to $122.3B — 11.2% of total assets, marked by management's own models, with a $62.8B H1 revaluation that the source itself calls "bimodal, not smooth." That's a capital-allocation risk that lands in a single quarter. Second, the advertised "+$43.1B net cash" is a gross-debt-only construct; adding lease obligations per the same fundamentals report flips it to a net obligation. One caveat, stated in fairness to both sides: the $11.8B finance-lease line may already sit inside the $133B gross-debt figure, so the true figure is probably between −$40B and −$67B rather than exactly −$66.7B — but either way the cushion is far thinner than "net cash after nearly doubling debt." Third, OCF quality: $30.5B deferred tax + $25.5B AP build (payables +20.9% vs revenue +18.2%) + $10.1B SBC out of $161.4B means the "2x coverage" of the $78.1B near-term ladder is closer to 1.7x, and part of it is vendor financing that unwinds if growth decelerates.

The $650B commitment ladder is the quiet killer of the bull's catalyst. The bull needs peak capex to unlock the FCF inflection; the bear is right that the same disclosure cannot simultaneously be a demand signal (spend continues) and a fixed cost (FCF doesn't inflect). Add D&A +31.3% with $173B of capex not yet fully depreciating, plus an effective tax rate stepping 17% → 23% against a ~4.9% cash tax rate with a $41.4B deferred liability, and the +43% operating income growth is not an extrapolable run rate. Also relevant: a +2.5% marketing and −6.0% G&A tape laps in four quarters.

On the tape we scored a modest edge to the bear. The bull's contracting-volume point is legitimate (28.07M vs 32.6M 10-session vs 47.0M 21-session) and I won't pretend otherwise. But the 08-19 push to 266.40 died on 35.8M shares within 1% of the $267.41 VWMA, and 08-17 was rejected on 43.9M — supply was tested and confirmed, not absorbed. Price is six cents above the 262.01 gap roof against a $7.28 ATR; that is a coin flip, not a held line. Downside references are 250.14 (−4.6%) and the 239.82 gap floor (−8.5%) versus a first real upside reference at 272.27 (+3.9%), into a binary NVDA print on 08/26 with crowded, 13F-lagged long positioning. That's the wrong shape for a full-weight hold.

What kept us short of Sell: the operating engine is genuinely strong — revenue +19.6% accelerating from +16.6%, operating income +43.2%, margin 13.7% (+230bps), Q2 OCF +39.6%, AWS's fastest growth in 18 quarters, custom silicon at a $25B run rate, interest coverage 20.9x, and $176.1B of liquid assets with $96.5B of AFS maturing inside a year at ~par. And the technical regime has not broken: 50-SMA ($250.14) and 200-SMA ($238.39) are both rising, the golden cross has held since May 6, RSI never flushed (low 49.74), and the MACD line is still +3.16 above zero. None of the bear's own falsifiers has triggered; they are plausible, not remote. Finally, the evidence set has holes that cap conviction in either direction: 137 of 157 news records for 8/17–8/21 are missing — the exact week of the damage — and both insider and social-sentiment data are unavailable.

Net: the bull's valuation pillar is broken and the bear's cash-flow-quality/leverage/catalyst-timing points are unrefuted, so we reduce exposure — but the operating trajectory, liquidity, and intact primary trend rule out exiting. Underweight, sized modestly.
