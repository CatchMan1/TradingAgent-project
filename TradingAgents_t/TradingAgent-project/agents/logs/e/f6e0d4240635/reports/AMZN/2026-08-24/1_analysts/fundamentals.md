1. **Liquidity is genuinely fortress-grade.** Cash + AFS = **$176.1B**, and of the AFS book **$96,471M matures within one year** at a fair value of $96,471M vs $96,479M amortized cost (unrealized loss of only $67M portfolio-wide). This is essentially a cash-equivalent sleeve, not a duration bet.

5. **AP +$25.5B (+20.9%) vs revenue +18.2%** — Amazon is stretching vendor terms to part-fund the build-out. This is a *non-recurring* source of cash: if growth decelerates, AP unwinds and becomes a cash drag.

## 10. Actionable Trading Insights

**Bias: constructive on the operating business, cautious on the reported numbers. Long-operations / short-headline.**

1. **Do not chase the +242% EPS print.** Sell-side consensus and any momentum factor that ingests reported EPS will be forced to revise *down* after the market digests that $50.5B of Q2 pre-tax income was a private-equity mark. Expect the "beat" headline to decay into an "adjusted/EPS quality" debate within days of the print. **The tradeable number is operating income +43.2% YoY and operating margin 13.7%.**
2. **The 230bps of operating margin expansion is real and repeatable.** Marketing grew +2.5% and G&A *shrank* −6.0% against +19.6% revenue — textbook operating leverage. Gross margin +50bps and inventory flat while revenue +18% confirm genuine efficiency, not accounting. **This is the strongest bullish datapoint in the entire dataset.**
3. **The bear case is a cash-flow and leverage story, not a growth story.** FCF flipping from +$13.5B to −$11.6B TTM while debt doubled is the kind of regime change that re-rates a multiple *even with accelerating revenue*. Watch for: (a) any deceleration in OCF growth below +30% YoY, (b) another quarter of >$25B net debt issuance, (c) capex guided above ~28% of revenue.
4. **Key asymmetry:** The $122.3B non-marketable equity book is a hidden asset that has already delivered +$62.8B of recognized gains. If the underlying private positions continue to appreciate, AMZN keeps printing large non-operating gains — a recurring "surprise" that supports the stock. If they mark down, the reversal is equally large and lands in a single quarter. **Position sizing should account for a bimodal non-operating line, not a smooth one.**
5. **The $650B contractual obligation is the under-priced item.** If you are building a bear case, this is the number to cite — it caps downside flexibility. If you are building a bull case, it is the strongest evidence of committed forward demand (a company does not sign $650B of take-or-pay capacity without a demand signal).
6. **Valuation discipline:** Use **operating EPS (~$3.68 H1'26, ~$7.3–7.5 annualized run-rate, growing +23%)** and **TTM OCF of $161.4B** as the denominators. Using reported TTM net income of $135.3B understates the multiple; using reported P/E on a $12+ TTM EPS that is 50%+ mark-up overstates the quality of the earnings.
7. **What to check next (highest information value):**
   - Q3 2026 10-Q — the **upward/downward price adjustment** on non-marketable equity securities (the single largest swing factor in EPS).
   - **Q3 capex** vs the $54.2B Q2 print; any sign of peak capex is a major FCF inflection catalyst.
   - **Whether debt issuance continues** above ~$10B/quarter.
   - **The 10-Q equity note** for the composition of the +$38.1B AOCI move.
   - Segment-level revenue/operating income (not in this dataset) to confirm whether the +19.6% revenue acceleration is broad-based.
