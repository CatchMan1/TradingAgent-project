You cannot use one EPS base for the growth claim and a different one for the divisor. That does not make AMZN a bad business; it makes the advertised "asymmetry" false, and the asymmetry was load-bearing. All three seats converged on that point — the aggressive led with it, the conservative explicitly conceded it, and the neutral accepted it while arguing only about degree.

Three further bear points survived unrefuted and govern sizing rather than direction. Second, leverage: the advertised "+$43.1B net cash" is a gross-debt-only construct; adding lease obligations flips it to a net obligation.
