## 1. Company Profile

**AMZN (Amazon.com, Inc.)** is a global technology and e-commerce conglomerate operating three reportable segments (confirmed in the Q1 2026 10-Q: `NumberOfOperatingSegments = 3`):
- **North America** — online retail, third-party marketplace, advertising, subscriptions (Prime).
- **International** — the same commerce suite outside North America.
- **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** — cloud infrastructure and AI compute platform.

The company is currently in a **heavy capital-investment supercycle**, plowing record amounts into data centers, AI infrastructure, and logistics. This investment phase is the single most important fundamental driver of the financials below — it explains the accelerating revenue/operating-profit growth *and* the surging debt and negative free cash flow.

### ⚠️ Critical Quality-of-Earnings Insight
The headline +76.7% net-income growth is **inflated by a large non-operating gain**. - The **cleaner growth signal** is operating income: **+29.6% YoY** on +16.6% revenue — genuine operating leverage.
- Traders should anchor valuation on **operating income ($23,852M)** rather than the inflated net income ($30,255M). Excluding the ~$12.3B one-time revaluation gain, "core" net income would be materially lower.

### 🔴 Most Important Finding for Traders
**Amazon's free cash flow has compressed to roughly zero and turned negative on a trailing basis**, because capex ($151B TTM) has caught up to and exceeded operating cash flow ($148.5B TTM). Capex nearly doubled from $83B in 2024 to $132B in 2025 and continues accelerating. This is the fundamental "cost" of the AI/cloud arms race.

Implications:
- **Cash generation is being fully reinvested** — near-term FCF-based valuation multiples will look stressed.
- The gap is being plugged with **debt** (Q1'26: +$53.4B LT debt issuance; capital expenditures incurred-but-not-yet-paid rose from $16.8B in 2024 to $27.0B in 2025, indicating future cash outflows).
- Positively, operating cash flow is genuinely strong and accelerating (+$9B YoY in Q1), and D&A ($70.4B TTM) is climbing — meaning the massive capex is beginning to generate depreciation shield and (presumably) AWS revenue that justifies it.
- **Q1 2026 also included a large $15.4B acquisition** (cash paid for businesses) — a capital allocation event worth monitoring (likely AI-related, consistent with the $12.3B equity revaluation gain).

## 6. Key Risks & Watch Items

1. **Negative free cash flow / rising leverage.** Debt doubling in a quarter and negative TTM FCF are the biggest red flags. Monitor whether capex growth decelerates or continues to outstrip operating cash flow — a sustained negative-FCF + rising-debt combination compresses valuation support.
2. **Quality of earnings depends on non-operating marks.** The $12.3B equity revaluation gain is volatile and reversible. If the underlying AI investment marks down (as `EquitySecuritiesFvNiGainLoss` did swing to −$883M in Q1'26, and warrant adjustments), earnings could reverse sharply.
3. **Interest expense climbing** (+48% YoY to $800M) as debt load grows — modest now but a direction to watch.
4. **Depreciation ramp.** D&A of $70.4B TTM will rise further as the new AI infrastructure depreciates, pressuring future reported margins even if operations are healthy.
5. **Capex execution risk.** Investors are underwriting the AI capex as value-accretive; any sign AWS demand doesn't absorb the buildout would hit both earnings and sentiment.
6. **Positive offsets:** Expanding gross and operating margins, double-digit revenue growth, strong operating cash flow growth, declining SBC, and a fortress liquidity position.

## 7. Actionable Insights for Traders

- **Look past the +77% net-income headline.** The clean operational story is +16.6% revenue and +29.6% operating-income growth — still excellent, but the EPS beat is heavily flattered by a ~$12.3B one-time equity revaluation gain. Adjust models to exclude this.
- **Treat AMZN as a capex-cycle stock right now.** With FCF roughly at/below zero, traditional FCF-yield screens will look unattractive; the bull case rests on AWS/AI revenue acceleration and eventual capex normalization. Position sizing should reflect elevated execution and financing risk.
- **Watch the debt trajectory.** A second consecutive quarter of large bond issuance would confirm a structural leverage shift — a caution flag. Conversely, a capex plateau with rising operating cash flow would be a strong re-rating catalyst.
- **Monitor the acquisition.** The $15.4B Q1'26 business acquisition plus a $12.3B stake revaluation suggest major AI-related capital deployment; details on returns will matter for the narrative.
- **Fundamentals remain solid at the core** (margins expanding, cash generation growing, SBC declining, $144B liquidity) — this is a high-quality business in a heavy-investment phase, not a deteriorating one. The debate is about *return on* and *funding of* the capex, not about the health of the operating business.
