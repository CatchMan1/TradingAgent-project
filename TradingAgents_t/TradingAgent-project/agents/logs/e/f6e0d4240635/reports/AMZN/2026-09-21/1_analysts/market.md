# AMZN — Multi-Indicator Technical Review (as of close 2026-09-21)

## 1. Bottom line / regime read

AMZN is in a **long-term uptrend that is being digested by a medium-term corrective range**. The primary trend is intact (close well above a slowly rising 200-day SMA, 50-day SMA above the 200-day), but the August impulse has been unwound and the stock is now bouncing inside a well-defined consolidation. The 2026-09-21 session is technically important: **the first close back above both the 50-day SMA and the 20-day Bollinger mid-band after eight consecutive sessions below them**, on above-average volume, with RSI recovering from 39.4 to 52.7 in three sessions. However, MACD remains below its signal line, price is still inside an unfilled 9/1 gap, and the Bollinger bandwidth has compressed to a fraction of its August level — so this is a **"repair in progress," not yet a confirmed trend resumption.**

Snapshot (source of truth, 2026-09-21): O 256.40 / H 259.49 / L 253.60 / C **258.45** / V 42.98M.

- The MACD line is **still below zero and below its signal** — the trend signal is not bullish.
- The important detail is the *shape*: the MACD line bottomed at **-2.2863 on 2026-09-17** and has risen for two consecutive sessions to **-1.5409**. The histogram (-0.245) is shallow, meaning the gap to the signal line is nearly closed. A cross above the signal is plausible within 1–3 sessions if price holds above ~256.
- Perspective on magnitude: MACD peaked at **+8.43 on 2026-08-11** and has bled to -1.54. That is a full momentum reset, not a fresh breakdown impulse — bearish energy is being spent rather than accelerating.
- **Confirmation rule:** a MACD/signal bullish cross *while the line is still below zero* is a mean-reversion signal, not a trend signal. It needs price confirmation (close > 259.77, see §6) to be tradeable.

**RSI(14): 52.69** — dead-center neutral.

- Path: **72.23 on 2026-08-03** (overbought at the closing peak of 284.02) → 39.45 on 2026-09-16 → **52.69 on 2026-09-21**.
- Note what did *not* happen: RSI never reached 30 during the 13.4% drawdown from the 8/3 close to the 9/16 close. The pullback was **orderly distribution, not capitulation**. That argues for a range/consolidation resolution rather than a V-bottom.
- Also note there is **no bullish divergence** in the tool data: the 9/16 price low came with a *lower* RSI low (39.45 vs 46.33 on 9/1). Price and momentum fell together — a confirmation of the downtrend, not a reversal tell.
- At 52.69 there is ample room in both directions; no overbought/oversold constraint on either scenario.

## 4. Volatility — a textbook contraction after an earnings blow-off

**Bollinger Bands (20,2):** mid **256.12**, upper **265.86**, lower **246.39**.

- **Bandwidth = (265.86 − 246.39) / 256.12 ≈ 7.6%.** On 2026-08-19 it was (296.99 − 220.47) / ~258.7 ≈ **29.6%**. Volatility has compressed by roughly three-quarters in a month. This is a classic post-event coil.
- The **bands are no longer expanding downward**: the lower band bottomed at 246.38 (9/18) and ticked up to 246.39 (9/21) after collapsing from 296.99/220.47 extremes. A flattening lower band with a rising price is the signature of downside momentum exhausting.
- Price closed at 258.45 — **+0.91% above the mid-band (256.12)** and 2.8% below the upper band. The mid-band reclaim is the first objective of any mean-reversion long; the upper band at 265.86 coincides with the 8/19 high (266.40), making **265.9–266.4 the key overhead confluence**.
- A squeeze does not dictate direction — it dictates *that* a directional expansion is coming. Treat the 246.4 / 265.9 band edges as the resolution triggers, not as automatic reversal trades.

**ATR(14): 6.03**, or **2.33% of price**.

- ATR has collapsed from **9.98 (2026-08-05)** and 9.51 (7/31 post-earnings) to 6.03 — a 40% decline. Daily ranges are compressing (9/21's range was 5.89 points, slightly under 1 ATR).
- Practical use: 1×ATR below the 258.45 close = **~252.4**; 1.5×ATR = **~249.4**. Those are natural invalidation/stop distances for a long taken here. Conversely, a stop only 1 ATR below entry would sit right on top of the VWMA/10-EMA cluster (253.7–253.9) and is likely to be clipped by noise.

## 5. Volume — the most interesting bullish nuance, with a caveat

**VWMA: 253.75** vs 50-SMA 256.10 vs close 258.45.

- Price is **+1.85% above VWMA**, i.e., buyers are currently paying above the volume-weighted cost basis of the recent window. That is constructive.
- However, **VWMA (253.75) is below the 50-SMA (256.10)**, and VWMA has fallen steadily from 271.91 (2026-08-19) → 253.75 (2026-09-21). Translated: the heavy volume of the last month changed hands at *lower* prices than the simple average — evidence of distribution through August/September. The trend is only "confirmed" when VWMA turns up and crosses back above the 50-SMA.
- Recent volume pattern is **bullish-leaning**: the three up sessions (9/17 +0.4%... 9/18 +1.00% on **52.27M**, 9/21 +1.87% on **42.98M**) came on heavier volume than the down sessions of 9/15 (36.28M) and 9/16 (33.49M). My derived 20-session average from the CSV is ~33.2M, so 9/18 was ~1.6× and 9/21 ~1.3× average. That is accumulation-style volume, though 9/18's 52M print coincides with a monthly expiration Friday and should not be over-read.
- Context for event risk: the two largest volume days in the dataset were **179.2M on 2026-02-06** (gap from 222.69 to 202.70) and **129.1M on 2026-07-31** (gap from 235.50 to 265.00, +12.5%). AMZN's technical structure is repeatedly rewritten by earnings gaps; any position held through a report is an event trade, not a technical trade.

4. **271.9** — the 8/19 VWMA extreme/failed bounce zone; the true trend-repair target.

**Support ladder**
1. **256.10** — 50-SMA (just reclaimed; now the line in the sand).
2. **253.60–253.92** — 9/21 low, 10-EMA (253.92) and VWMA (253.75): a tight three-way confluence. Losing this cluster invalidates today's bounce.
3. **250.5–251.9** — 9/3 close 258.90... more precisely the 9/9 low 250.65 and 9/10 close 251.89 shelf.
4. **246.39** — lower Bollinger band.
5. **244.30** — 9/16 intraday low, the swing low of the correction.
6. **240.42** — 200-SMA. A close below this would be the first primary-trend breach since the spring and would flip the strategic bias.

**Scenario A — Range repair / continuation higher (base case, favored by trend hierarchy + volume)**
Trigger: **close above 259.77** with volume ≥ ~40M (above the ~33M 20-day average) and MACD crossing above its signal line. Targets 265.9 (upper band) → 266.4/267.6 (August highs). Stop on a close back below 253.6. Risk ~5.9 points vs. first target ~7.5 points — thin but acceptable; scaling toward 271.9 improves the ratio materially.

**Scenario B — Range-bound chop (highest-probability near-term given bandwidth 7.6% and RSI 52.7)**
Price oscillates **246.4–265.9**. Trade the edges, not the middle. Buying the 253.6–256.1 confluence and selling 265–266 is the mechanical play; momentum confirmation is absent, so size down.

**Scenario C — Failed reclaim / lower-low continuation**
Trigger: **close below 253.60** (which also breaks the 10-EMA, VWMA, and puts price back under the 50-SMA and mid-band in one move). Then 250.5–251.9, then 246.39, then the 244.30 swing low. A close under 244.30 opens 240.42 (200-SMA). MACD rolling back under -2.29 (the 9/17 low) would corroborate.

**Risk management notes**
- Use ATR 6.03, not fixed dollar stops: a 1.5×ATR stop from 258.45 ≈ **249.4**, which sits below the 250.5 shelf and therefore has a structural reason to hold.
- Position size should reflect **event-gap risk** (the Feb 6 and Jul 31 gaps of -8.9% and +12.5% overnight) — the ATR understates true overnight tail risk.
- The 50-SMA is flat and the 10-EMA is below it. Do not treat "price above the 50-SMA" as an independent buy signal today; it is one input among several that are only *beginning* to align.
