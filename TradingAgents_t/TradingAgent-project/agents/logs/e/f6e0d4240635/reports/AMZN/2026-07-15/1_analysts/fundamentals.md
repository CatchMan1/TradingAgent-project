## 2. Company Profile

Amazon.com, Inc. operates as a three‑segment, vertically‑integrated technology and logistics conglomerate:

1. **North America** — first‑party and third‑party e‑commerce, Fulfillment‑by‑Amazon, Prime subscription, advertising, physical stores.
2. **International** — the same commerce stack across non‑US geographies.
3. **AWS** — cloud compute, storage, database, AI/ML services (largest global cloud provider), plus the fast‑growing custom‑silicon (Trainium/Inferentia) and AI model infrastructure layer.

Adjacent high‑margin engines inside the segments: **advertising** (FY2025 spend $23.5B on marketing, FY2025 ad revenue base is embedded in "other" segment revenue), **subscriptions**, **devices**, and an increasingly material **investment portfolio of non‑marketable equity stakes** (AI/LLM and robotics partners — this is the driver of the Q1'26 non‑operating windfall discussed below).

Business model characteristics visible in the filings: negative cash‑conversion cycle (AP > AR + inventory), heavy fixed‑asset and lease intensity, and a management posture in FY2025/FY2026 of *re‑leveraging the balance sheet to fund an AI/physical‑infrastructure capex supercycle.*

### 3.2 Earnings quality — the single most important caveat for traders

**~40% of pre‑tax profit was non‑operating, and largely non‑cash:**

- `EquitySecuritiesWithoutReadilyDeterminableFairValueUpwardPriceAdjustmentAnnualAmount` = **$12,328M in one quarter** (vs just $37M in Q1'25). - Partly offset by a **−$883M** FV‑NI loss on marketable equity securities and a **−$889M** marketable‑securities unrealized loss.
- Net: non‑operating income of **$15,982M ≈ 40% of the $39,834M pre‑tax total**; operating income is only **60% of pre‑tax profit.**

**Pro‑forma "core" Q1'26 net income excluding the $12.3B mark‑up (tax‑effected at 24%):**
- ≈ **$20.9B**, or **~$1.92 diluted EPS** vs $1.59 → **+21% YoY** (still excellent, but less than a third of the +75% headline).

**Traders should model AMZN on the ~$1.92 "core" run‑rate, not the $2.78 GAAP print.** The mark‑up is non‑cash, non‑recurring, and can reverse (a symmetric downward adjustment is possible in any future quarter).

Other quality flags:
- **Tax rate jumped to 24.0%** (from 21.0%), and **deferred tax expense exploded to $12,798M** in the quarter (vs $507M) — a mechanical consequence of marking up equity securities and bonus‑depreciation timing. Cash taxes paid were only $1,323M in Q1.
- **D&A is compounding fast**: $18.9B/quarter (annualizing ~$76B) vs $14.3B a year ago — a direct drag on future operating margin as the AI asset base depreciates.

## 9. Actionable Insights for Traders

### Bull case — what the numbers actually support
1. **Revenue re‑acceleration to +16.6% with +132 bp of operating‑margin expansion** is the highest‑quality datapoint in the entire filing. Growth *and* margin simultaneously is rare at this scale.
2. **Operating income +29.6% YoY** — the core business is compounding faster than revenue.
3. **Negative cash‑conversion cycle intact (≈ −54 days CCC)** with inventory *falling* while revenue grew — a genuine efficiency win.
4. **Balance sheet still under‑levered in absolute terms**: net obligations incl. leases ≈ 0.5x EBITDA, ~$144B liquidity. Amazon *can* fund this capex cycle; the question is whether it should.
5. **Deferred revenue $25.3B with $9.4B recognized in Q1'26** — a hard‑to‑fake demand signal for Prime/AWS commitments.

### Bear case / risks — what could break the thesis
1. **Reported earnings are heavily mark‑to‑market dependent.** $12.3B of Q1'26 pre‑tax profit is a non‑cash equity mark‑up that can reverse. If you model off GAAP EPS ($2.78) you are **~45% too high** vs core (~$1.92). Expect consensus to be split on this for the Q2 print.
2. **TTM FCF is negative (−$2.5B)** and Q1 FCF was −$18.2B. *Free‑cash‑flow‑based valuation support has evaporated.* If capex guide rises again at Q2, expect multiple compression in FCF‑sensitive screens.
3. **Debt is up 78% in one quarter** ($68.8B → $122.6B). The credit profile has changed materially; interest expense +48% YoY and about to compound further. Any deterioration in the funding market is now a real AMZN risk.
4. **$569.3B of contractual obligations** with $57.1B due in 12 months — a hard forward cost commitment that caps margin expansion if AI demand disappoints.
5. **D&A inflecting** at $18.9B/quarter (+33% YoY) with capex/D&A at 2.14x — mechanical depreciation headwind for 2026–2028.
6. **Effective tax rate rising** (24.0% vs 21.0%) — the era of near‑zero cash taxes ($1.3B paid in Q1) is ending; this is a structural EPS headwind.
7. **Slight share‑count creep** and zero buyback/dividend — no capital‑return floor under the stock.
