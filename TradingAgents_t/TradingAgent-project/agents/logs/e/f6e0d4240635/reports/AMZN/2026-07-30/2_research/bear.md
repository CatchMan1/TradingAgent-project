## 4. The $650B "Moat" Is the Cycle Risk, Not the Reward

> *"$650B of committed capacity is a moat, not a liability. You don't commit $650B unless the demand signal justifies it."*

- **Telecom, 1999–2001:** carriers committed hundreds of billions to fiber and spectrum on "the internet demand is insatiable." The demand was real. The commitments were still written down.
- **The pattern is always the same:** capacity is committed years ahead; demand arrives on a curve nobody controls; the gap is absorbed by the equity.

Mine is **operating leverage in reverse** — a fixed cost base that compounds regardless of the demand curve.

Now connect it to the D&A wave you brushed off:
