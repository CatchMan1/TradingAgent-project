This is where I think the bear argument is at its weakest, because it ignores what the news flow actually showed last week:

- **In-house silicon is a structural cost advantage.** The Arm item confirms Amazon is among the cloud giants building custom AI chips. Every dollar of AWS compute migrated onto custom silicon is a margin point that a merchant-silicon competitor can't match.
- **Logistics insourcing keeps taking share.** The UPS piece frames its underperformance partly through "Amazon's impact." Amazon still funds operations with **$220.8B of non-interest-bearing supplier float against $126.3B of AR + inventory.** That negative cash conversion cycle is a permanent structural edge, and it survived an 18% revenue expansion with no degradation.
