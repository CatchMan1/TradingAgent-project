The bull answered with assertion, not arithmetic.

Net: the bear won entry quality, margin durability, valuation denominator, and which risks are unpriced; the bull won franchise quality, balance-sheet resilience, and the structural uptrend. At $254.92 the near-term asymmetry is against adding, and two hazards are genuinely under-priced — a mark reset on the $122.3B private holding (which the bull put on his own "change my mind" list) and an FTC action against advertising, precisely where incremental margin comes from. Unquantifiable is not immaterial, and the bull treated it as zero. That is not enough for Sell — the franchise is too good, the 200-SMA is a real floor, and the bear explicitly declined to short — but it is decisively enough for Underweight: trim, don't exit, don't add at this price. The bear only needed AMZN to be fully priced with more ways to disappoint, and that's the side I can defend.
