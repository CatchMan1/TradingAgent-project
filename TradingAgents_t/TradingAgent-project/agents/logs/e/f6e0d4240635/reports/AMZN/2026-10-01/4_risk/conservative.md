The aggressive seat says sizing controls noise; I say the sizing and stop structure need to be redesigned before any add is authorized. Each tranche should carry equal dollar risk, and any add should use an ATR-based trailing stop rather than a fixed level that is more than 2 ATR away from the second entry.
