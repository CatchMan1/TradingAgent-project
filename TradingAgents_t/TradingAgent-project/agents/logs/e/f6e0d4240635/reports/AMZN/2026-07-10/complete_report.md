AMZN (Amazon.com, Inc.) is a global technology and e-commerce conglomerate organized around **three operating segments** (confirmed by the 10-Q: `NumberOfOperatingSegments = 3`):

- **North America** — online and physical retail, third-party marketplace, advertising, Prime subscriptions
- **International** — the same retail/advertising business outside North America
- **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** — cloud computing, storage, database, AI/ML infrastructure and services

AMZN's fundamental model is a high-volume, low-margin retail/logistics engine that funds high-margin, high-growth profit pools: **AWS (cloud + AI) and advertising**. Advertising expense (a cost line, distinct from advertising *revenue*) rose to **$23.5B in FY2025** from $21.4B (FY2024) and $20.3B (FY2023), underscoring continued brand/marketing investment. The company is currently in a **massive AI/data-center capital-expenditure supercycle**, which is the single most important driver of the current financial picture.
