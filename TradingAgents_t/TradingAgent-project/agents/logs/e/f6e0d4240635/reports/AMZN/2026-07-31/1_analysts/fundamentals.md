## 1. What is genuinely new this week (the reason to re-underwrite AMZN today)

The June-quarter 10-Q hit the tape **today**. Three disclosures dominate:

1. **Revenue acceleration + record operating margin.** Q2-26 revenue ≈ **$200.6B (+19.6% y/y)**, operating income **$27.46B (13.7% margin)** vs 11.4% a year ago.
2. **A ~$50B non-cash revaluation of private equity holdings** (non-marketable equity, "upward price adjustment" = **$50.49B in Q2, $62.81B in H1**). The carrying value of *equity securities without readily determinable fair value* jumped from **$16.2B (12/31/25) → $122.3B (6/30/26)**. This single item explains most of the GAAP EPS "beat" — reported Q2 diluted EPS $5.75 vs ~$2.16 ex-mark-up.
3. **Capex supercycle + debt funding.** H1-26 capex **$98.4B (+72% y/y)**, TTM capex **$173.0B vs TTM operating cash flow $161.4B → TTM free cash flow has turned NEGATIVE (-$11.6B)**, from +$13.5B a year earlier. Long-term debt nearly doubled to **$133.0B**; TTM debt issuance **$81.9B LT + $20.8B ST**.

*Data note: revenue is derived as `CostsAndExpenses + OperatingIncomeLoss` (the cached feed did not expose a Revenues tag); the derivation reproduces known actuals (Q2-25 → $167.7B, H1-25 → $323.4B), so it is reliable. **Insider-transaction data was unavailable** for AMZN in this environment — no insider buying/selling inference is made.*

## 2. Company profile & structure

Amazon.com, Inc. is a diversified technology/retail conglomerate. The filing tags **3 operating segments** (`NumberOfOperatingSegments = 3`): **North America, International, and AWS**. Business mix spans: online & physical retail, third-party marketplace (1P/3P), Prime subscription, advertising, AWS cloud/AI infrastructure (compute, custom silicon, Bedrock-type AI services), logistics/fulfillment network, devices, and a very large **investment portfolio** (equity stakes without readily determinable fair value now $122.3B — the size of a mid-cap company in itself, and the reason earnings quality must be adjusted).

## 4. Earnings quality — the single most important adjustment for traders

Reported GAAP EPS is heavily inflated by fair-value mark-ups on private equity stakes:

| $M (pre-tax) | Q2-2026 | H1-2026 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Upward price adjustment, equity securities w/o RDFV | 50,486 | 62,814 |
| Equity securities FV-NI gain (marketable) | 1,300 | 454 |
| Warrant fair-value adjustment | (1,449) | (1,051) |
| FX transaction gain | 227 | 387 |

**Pro-forma "core" EPS (excluding the private-equity mark-ups only, taxed at the 22.5–23% effective rate, ~10.9B diluted shares):**

| | Reported | Core (ex private marks) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Q2-2026 diluted EPS | $5.75 | **≈ $2.16** |
| H1-2026 diluted EPS | $8.53 | **≈ $4.06** |

So roughly **60–62% of Q2 reported profit and ~52% of H1 profit is non-cash revaluation, not operating cash generation.** Concurrently, **deferred tax expense exploded to $30.5B in H1-26 (vs $0.5B H1-25)** — a direct accounting signature of book gains not yet taxable, and a reminder that **cash taxes paid were only $3.98B in H1 (vs $5.64B a year ago)**.

**Trading implication:** any screen or headline based on AMZN's GAAP P/E or "EPS surprise" is misleading this quarter. Value AMZN on **operating income / EBIT margin / EV-to-EBIT or EV-to-(EBIT+SBC-neutral cash flow)**; treat the $122.3B private-equity book (and the $62.8B of embedded unrealized gains) as a sum-of-the-parts asset with mark-to-market risk on the downside (these marks are reversible).

## 7. Segment/operating narrative implied by the numbers

- **Three segments** (North America, International, AWS) — the segment P&L detail was not in the retrieved tags, but the consolidated pattern (revenue +19.6%, D&A +31%, lease cost +21%, marketing +4%, G&A −4%) is the classic signature of **AWS/AI compute scaling faster than retail, with retail cost discipline funding it**.
- **Advertising and third-party seller services** are the highest-incremental-margin lines and the likely driver of the 226bp margin gain alongside AWS re-acceleration.
- **Deferred revenue flat** ($24.9B): subscription/Prime and AWS prepaid growth is *not* yet showing up as balance-sheet deferral — revenue is being recognized as delivered, which supports the quality of the +19.6%.

## 9. Actionable insights for traders

**Bullish evidence (high confidence, primary-source):**
1. **Revenue re-acceleration to +19.6%** with **operating margin +226bp to 13.7%** — genuine operating leverage, not accounting.
2. **Cost discipline is real**: G&A −4% y/y, marketing +4% y/y, SBC −7.6% y/y, inventory days down ~7 days, D&A +31% absorbed with room to spare.
3. **Net cash still positive (+$30.4B)** and interest coverage 21x — the capex ramp is fundable, not fragile.
4. **$122.3B of private equity stakes** (up from $16.2B) is an unrecognized SOTP asset; even after reversing $62.8B of marks, the base is ~$59B of book value not in operating earnings.
5. **Cash taxes only $4.0B H1-26** on $120.7B pretax (heavy deferrals) — tax cash-flow tailwind persists near term.

**Bearish / risk evidence:**
1. **Earnings quality**: ~52–62% of H1-26 and Q2-26 GAAP EPS is non-cash equity mark-ups. Q2-26 core EPS ≈ **$2.16** vs $5.75 reported. If marks reverse, GAAP EPS compresses violently.
2. **Negative FCF (−$11.6B TTM, −$27.0B in H1-26)** — the market has historically punished AMZN's FCF dips; the 2022 analogue is instructive.
3. **Debt +93% in six months** ($68.4B → $132.2B) with **interest expense +155% y/y**; continued issuance at this pace would erode the net-cash cushion within ~2–3 quarters at the current burn rate.
4. **AR +30% vs revenue +20%** — a 3+ day receivables stretch; if it persists next quarter it is an early warning on collections/segment mix.
5. **$650B of contractual obligations**, $78B due within twelve months — a hard floor under future capex/lease outflows that caps medium-term FCF recovery optionality.
6. **Effective tax rate reset to ~22.5–23%** from ~17% — a structural EPS headwind (~5–6% of EPS) as the deferred-tax shield from the investment gains normalizes.

**Concrete positioning ideas:**
- **Trade the GAAP/core dispersion, not the headline.** Any post-print gap driven by "$5.75 vs consensus" should be faded toward the ~$2.16 core; conversely a sell-off driven by "negative FCF" ignores that OCF grew 44% y/y in H1.
- **Watch three specific next-quarter checkpoints:** (a) does Q3 capex exceed ~$55B/quarter (bear case for FCF); (b) does AR growth decelerate back below revenue growth; (c) does gross LT debt exceed ~$150B (net-cash-to-net-debt flip risk).
- **SOTP framing:** value the operating business on EV/(EBIT + D&A-neutral) using ~$27.5B quarterly operating income (~$110B annualized run-rate, expanding), then add a separately-marked $122.3B investment book — a book that is *inherently volatile* and best hedged or haircut.
- **Insider signal: unavailable.** No authorized insider-transaction archive exists for AMZN in this environment; do not read the absence of reported insider activity as either bullish or bearish.
