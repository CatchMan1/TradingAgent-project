## 8. Why These 8 Indicators (Selection Rationale)

Chosen for **complementarity and low redundancy** across trend, momentum, volatility, and volume:
- **close_10_ema, close_50_sma, close_200_sma** — three time horizons of trend, giving both fast actionable signals and strategic confirmation (golden-cross structure), without redundant overlap.
- **macd + rsi** — MACD for trend/momentum crossover mechanics, RSI for overbought/oversold magnitude; they answer different questions (direction vs. intensity) and are not duplicative.
- **boll (middle/bands) + atr** — Bollinger for volatility regime/squeeze and dynamic benchmarks; ATR for absolute volatility and stop sizing. Bollinger measures relative/stretched-ness; ATR measures raw range — complementary.
- **vwma** — the sole volume-based confirmation, tying price to volume-weighted participation (avoids stacking multiple volume tools).

*(I deliberately did not select stochrsi alongside RSI, nor macds/macdh/guppy-style stacks alongside macd, to honor the no-redundancy mandate; the signal-line and histogram values were still consulted via the snapshot to interpret the MACD crossover.)*
