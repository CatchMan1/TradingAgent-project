## 1. Executive Summary

Amazon's Q2 2026 filing shows a company in the middle of an **extraordinary capital-deployment cycle** funded increasingly with debt, while core operating momentum is simultaneously accelerating. Three things dominate the fundamental picture:

1. **Core operations are inflecting higher.** Q2 2026 revenue reached **$200.6B (+19.6% YoY)** and operating income **$27.5B (+43.2% YoY)**, lifting operating margin to **13.7% (+226bp)** — *despite* depreciation rising 31% YoY. Incremental operating margin was ~25%.
2. **Reported EPS is heavily distorted by a non-cash mark-up on private equity holdings.** Q2 diluted EPS of **$5.75** includes roughly **$3.59/share** of pre-tax mark-to-market gains on equity securities without a readily determinable fair value (a $50.5B upward adjustment in the quarter; $62.8B in H1). That asset line ballooned from **$16.2B to $122.3B (+655%)** in six months. Core, operations-only EPS is approximately **$1.95 (+26% YoY)**.
3. **Free cash flow has turned negative.** TTM operating cash flow of **$161.4B** was outrun by **$173.0B** of capex (Q2 capex alone: **$54.2B, 27.0% of revenue**), producing **TTM FCF of –$11.6B** versus **+$7.7B in FY2025 (derived)**. The gap is being closed with debt: long-term debt rose **+93% in six months, from $68.8B to $133.0B**, plus $13.5B of finance leases.

**Bottom line for traders:** the operating business is performing better than at any point in its history on margin, and that is the durable signal. But the headline EPS, the balance-sheet expansion, and the free-cash-flow profile are all being driven by items that are either non-cash (equity marks) or forward-dated (AI/data-center capex). Position sizing should reflect a company whose earnings quality is bifurcated and whose financial leverage is rising fast.

### 4.2 What is actually driving the P&L
