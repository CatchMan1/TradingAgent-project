**Recommendation**: Overweight

**Rationale**: Here's how I read this round. Both sides agree AMZN is a high-quality franchise; the fight was over whether $251.19 is a good place to own it.

The bull won the fundamental debate decisively. Q2 revenue accelerated to $200.6B (+19.6% vs +16.6% in Q1), operating income jumped 43.2% to $27.5B, and margin expanded 226 bps to 13.69% — with marketing, G&A, and SBC all falling as a share of revenue and SBC down 7.6% even against 19.6% revenue growth. That's genuine operating leverage, not accounting. Core EPS (ex the $62.8B equity marks) grew ~28.6% in Q2 and ~25.1% in H1, TTM core ~$7.98. Crucially, the bear conceded nearly all of this — that concession matters, because the bear's case then rests on timing and capex-ROI uncertainty rather than deterioration in the business.

The bear's strongest card is the cash-flow/debt trajectory, and it's legitimate: TTM FCF is −$11.6B (from +$13.5B), H1 OCF covered only ~73% of capex, debt+finance leases rose 80.5% in six months to $146.4B, current ratio is 1.033, and there's $650B of contractual obligations. But the bear overreaches on solvency: $176.1B of cash and securities, net cash excluding operating leases, and 20.9x/24.3x interest coverage describe a company funding a deliberate build-out, not a distressed balance sheet. The "show me capex ROI" demand is fair, but "show me capex is peaking" is a bar that, if met, would actually undercut the growth case — so it can't be the deciding test.

The bear also won the near-term tape argument: price is below the 10 EMA ($252.73), 50 SMA ($255.71), VWMA, and Bollinger midline, with a lower-high sequence and MACD still negative. But the bull's structural counter holds — 200 SMA rising at $240.20, price +4.57% above it, 50 SMA +6.46% above the 200, a first higher low at $249.24, improving MACD histogram, and the technical report itself assigning only 25% odds to bearish continuation.

So the business case favors the bull, but the bear is right that buying the exact middle of the $244–262 range at $251 is poor tactical discipline and that valuation (~31.5x core TTM) plus the capex/debt trajectory argue for staging rather than diving in. Net: constructive on the name, but not a full-throated Buy at this price. That lands at Overweight — build the position gradually, respect the range, and let confirmation (or a better entry) do the work.
