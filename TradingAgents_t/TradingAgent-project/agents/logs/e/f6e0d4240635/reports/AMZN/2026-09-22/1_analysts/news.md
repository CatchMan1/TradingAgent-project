6. **No full-article content** was supplied; the wage figure, the $60B figure, and the UPS EPS figures are single-line summary claims that I cannot cross-verify.

## 4. Net assessment

- **Near term (1–5 sessions):** Mildly constructive but largely **beta-driven**. The 09-21 tape was a broad AI rally (Nasdaq +3%) and AMZN's +1.87% lagged it. Post-close wage news is a small, disclosed cost with a known effective date; the Muse block is a headline-risk item rather than an earnings item. I would not expect either to move AMZN more than low-single-digit percent on its own.
- **Medium term (1–3 months):** The **AI infrastructure commitment** (Qualcomm framework) and **logistics insourcing** (UPS read-through) are the structurally meaningful items — one raises the capex ceiling, the other lowers unit delivery cost. The wage increase is a rounding-error margin headwind unless $1.5B proves to be an annual recurring step-up across a much larger eligible population than the summary implies.
- **Key monitorables not in evidence:** actual capex/FCF guidance, any regulatory response to agent blocking, holiday-season volume data, and the 09-15→09-20 news gap.

**Bias of this report:** I lean slightly positive on fundamentals and neutral-to-cautious on sentiment, with **low confidence overall** given headline-only sourcing and 87% record attrition.
