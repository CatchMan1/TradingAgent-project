Where I part company is on the calibration. A 40–50% trim is not a conservative response to this setup — it is a half-measure that leaves the firm carrying 50–60% of a full AMZN position into a binary late-October catalyst with negative free cash flow, a 93% six-month increase in long-term debt, a reversible $122.3B Level 3 private-stake book, and a documented −9.73% two-day drop in this very dataset. If the aggressive analyst genuinely believes there is no edge at 254.98 — and I think he is right about that — then the logical conclusion is not to keep half the position. It is to cut deeper and keep a smaller core, because the whole point of a conservative posture is that you do not need to be right about the direction of an unresolved coil when you can simply reduce the amount of capital exposed to it.

So let me take the aggressive's framing head-on, because “this is not a bearish call, it's an offensive capital-allocation move” is a distinction without a difference when you look at the risk profile. What the firm actually holds after this plan is 50–60% of a full AMZN position inside a four-way moving-average coil with three confirmed rejections at 259 and a 55% probability the report itself assigns to continued coil or a retest of 246–250. The aggressive gets the diagnosis right and then stops one step short of the conclusion.

And the aggressive's central bull catalyst — capex growth decelerating or TTM FCF turning positive — is precisely the variable that has not turned yet. A conservative desk does not add weight because a supplier's stock moved 7% on an unquantified framework. It waits for the 10-Q.

If the desk wants downside protection into the print — and it should — use a defined-risk put spread sized so a −10% to −15% move stays inside the risk budget, not a naked fade of the 259 ceiling. The aggressive defends the overlay on asymmetry, but the asymmetry he describes only holds if the stop is honored, and in a gap scenario it will not be. That is the definition of a risk the conservative desk exists to eliminate.
