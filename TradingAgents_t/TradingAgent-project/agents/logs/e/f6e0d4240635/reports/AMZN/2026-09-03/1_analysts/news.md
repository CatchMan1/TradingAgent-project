Any Amazon-specific catalyst from Aug 27–Sep 1 is invisible to this analysis.

Where a summary truncates mid-sentence (e.g., the Zoox and Druckenmiller items end in "…"), I treat the missing portion as unknown rather than inferring it. Several records are third-party commentary/opinion pieces (motley-fool style) rather than corporate disclosures — they carry sentiment information, not factual news value.

Consequently, the report below covers a **single-day news flow of largely low-to-moderate materiality**, and the correct posture is "no evidence of a large idiosyncratic catalyst," not "no catalyst occurred."
