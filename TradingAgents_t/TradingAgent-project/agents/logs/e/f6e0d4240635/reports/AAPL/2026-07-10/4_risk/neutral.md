The June 26 reversal bar at 261.8M shares proved a bottom was defended; it did not prove this resistance shelf will break. RSI at 62.93 is healthy, but it is not a buy signal, and the prior RSI peak near 79.8 did not prevent a correction. But saying the breakout reward is limited to $321.20 understates what a confirmed break above $317 can do; it just means the initial add must be small and follow-through must be earned.

So my balanced adjustment to the trader’s AAPL plan is this. Keep the Hold. Do not add at $315.32. If AAPL is already at standard allocation, maintain the core. If it is already overweight, trim back toward standard into the $315–$317 shelf rather than pressing. For the breakout, wait for a daily close above $317 on volume greater than $65M, but add only a first tranche — not the full +10–20% overweight immediately. I would cap the initial add at roughly +5–10% above standard allocation, sized so a stop at $298 or a failed-breakout exit below $317 is an acceptable portfolio risk. Add the second tranche only if AAPL holds $317 on a retest or closes above $321.20 with sustained volume. If it closes back below $317, reduce the add even if $298 has not been hit; do not wait for the wide structural stop on a failed trigger. For the pullback, $298–$300 is the better zone because the 50 SMA at $297.77 and Bollinger mid at $298.10 form real confluence. But scale it: first tranche only if it holds that zone on declining volume and does not close below $295; second tranche only after stabilization or a reclaim of the 10 EMA near $306.57. Keep $295 on a closing basis as the core structural risk line, with $273.75 as the hard thesis line. If there is no position, wait for one of those triggers rather than initiating at resistance.

That gives the aggressive analyst the participation he wants, but only after evidence and with staged size. It gives the conservative analyst the risk control he wants, but without demanding so much confirmation that the opportunity is gone. AAPL can remain a constructive long and still be a poor place to add aggressively at $315.32. The moderate path is to hold the core, let $317 prove itself, add in smaller tranches on confirmation or at the $298 confluence, and keep total exposure near standard unless both price and volume confirm a genuine breakout.
