## 8. Indicator selection rationale

I selected **eight** indicators deliberately, avoiding overlap:

1. **close_200_sma** — establishes the primary trend regime (golden cross intact, +12.7% above) and provides a strategic, not tactical, floor.
2. **close_50_sma** — the pivot line of the whole setup: it flattened through August and has now inflected up; it is also the first major support on any pullback.
3. **close_10_ema** — captures the short-term turn (the 08-19 cross above the 50 SMA) and identifies the razor-thin $319.5 line that must hold.
4. **macdh** — the cleanest read on the momentum cycle: sign-flip on 08-26, peak on 09-03, first down-tick on 09-04. (The underlying MACD line and signal are cited from the same family rather than scored separately, to avoid double-counting.)
5. **rsi** — independent momentum confirmation that shows no overbought extreme and no bearish divergence, so the 09-04 drop is a reset, not a reversal.
6. **boll_ub** (with mid/lower as one band family) — quantifies the exact rejection level ($328.06) and the current 9.5% bandwidth, i.e. how much room the range actually has.
7. **atr** — sizes stops and positions in a name that produces 6–8% single-session gaps; converts every level above into a risk-adjusted trade.
8. **vwma** — the only volume-integrated trend check: $316.37 rising from a 08-19 trough confirms real accumulation beneath the base, and it sits in exactly the same cluster as the 50 SMA.

Excluded: MACD line as a separate score (redundant with macdh), MACD signal as a separate score (same family), boll middle and lower as separate scores (same band family), and any oscillator duplicating RSI. Note the snapshot also reports an `mfi` value of 0.6558; its scale is not documented in the tool output, so I have deliberately not built any conclusion on it.
