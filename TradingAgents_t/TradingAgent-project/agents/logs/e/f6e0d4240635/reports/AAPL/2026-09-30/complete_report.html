1) The growth story is not “steady-state Apple” right now — it is accelerating

  • Revenue: $364.36B, +16.1% YoY

And FQ3 was the strongest quarter in the dataset:

  • Revenue +16.4%
  • Gross margin 50.06%, +357 bps YoY

The bear will say 16% growth is cyclically elevated versus Apple’s historical norm. Fine — that’s a fair caveat.
