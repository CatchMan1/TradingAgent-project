## 7. Actionable Insights & Trading Plan
1. **Bias: bullish / trend-following.** The MA stack, positive MACD histogram, and price above VWMA all favor the long side. This is a "buy-the-dip" market, not a shorting market — until/unless the 50-SMA (~$299) breaks on volume.
2. **Don't chase the upper band.** With price tagging the upper Bollinger Band (~$323.78) and RSI at 64, prefer **staged entries on pullbacks** toward the 10-EMA (~$308) and the 50-SMA / mid-band confluence (~$299). Those are higher-reward-risk add zones.
3. **Breakout scenario:** A decisive close above **$323.78** on above-average volume would confirm continuation and could justify momentum entries — but demand volume expansion (>~70M) for validation.
4. **Risk management:** Use ATR-based stops. For swing positions, a stop roughly **2×ATR (~$16)** below entry, or a structural stop below the 10-EMA (~$308) for tighter trades, and below the 50-SMA (~$299) for wider trend positions.
5. **What would flip the view:** A close back below the VWMA (~$294) and especially a break of the 50-SMA (~$299) would neutralize the short-term bullish case; the 200-SMA (~$273) remains the strategic line in the sand.
6. **Beware whipsaw risk:** The June episode (≈14% drawdown with a 261M-share capitulation day) is a reminder this name can deliver violent, fast reversals even inside an uptrend — size positions and stops accordingly.
