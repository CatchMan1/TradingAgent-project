## 6. Capital Allocation — a clear strategic shift

This is one of the most actionable findings:

1. **Buybacks decelerated sharply:** $36.99B in H1 FY2026 vs $49.50B in H1 FY2025 (**-25%**), despite *higher* free cash flow. FY2025 full-year buybacks were $90.7B.
2. **Deleveraging accelerated:** ~$7.9B LT debt repaid (vs $4.0B) and commercial paper cut by ~$5.9B.
3. **M&A/IP signal:** the ~$12.8B gross-intangible increase suggests cash is being redirected to acquisitions.
4. **Dividend still growing:** H1 FY2026 dividends declared **$0.52/share** ($0.26/qtr) vs $0.50 → **+4%**, with cash dividends paid up only 1.7% because the share count is falling.

**Interpretation:** Management is prioritizing reinvestment (R&D +33%, possible M&A) and balance-sheet strength over buyback support. Bulls see higher future growth; bears note it removes a key historical EPS tailwind. Net buyback spend ($36.99B) still equals ~1.5% of the share base, so per-share metrics are still supported.

## 10. Actionable Insights for Traders

1. **Fundamentals are accelerating, not deteriorating.** Revenue +16.6%, EPS +21.8%, gross margin <49% rising — the most bullish signal is margin expansion (+222 bps) alongside double-digit top-line. Momentum-style longs are supported by the trajectory.
2. **The margin story is the trade.** Gross margin breaking toward ~49% on a Services-mix shift is the highest-quality signal. Watch next quarter's GM print — if it holds ≥48.5%, the operating-leverage thesis stays intact.
3. **Don't chase the OCF headline.** The +53% H1 OCF is ~half working-capital timing. Use ~20% underlying OCF growth (Q2 run-rate) for modeling free cash flow.
4. **Watch the capital-allocation pivot.** Buybacks -25% while intangibles +$12.8B and debt -$14B signals a shift toward M&A and deleveraging. If an acquisition is confirmed, expect near-term multiple volatility but potentially better long-term growth optionality; if the intangible build is IP licensing, margins could benefit.
5. **Tax-rate headwind is a modeling adjustment.** A ~17.5% vs ~15% ETR shaves ~$1.5–2B/yr off net income — trim EPS estimates accordingly; do not mistake it for operational weakness.
6. **R&D +33% is the AI tell.** Combined with falling capex, Apple is spending on people/IP rather than owned data centers — margin-accretive if it works, but a rising fixed cost if monetization lags. This is the key swing factor for FY2027 estimates.
7. **Valuation check is mandatory before action.** The toolset returns no price/multiple. Confirm P/E and EV/FCF against the ~$4.85 H1 diluted EPS run-rate and a fully net-cash balance sheet ($61.9B net cash) before sizing.
8. **Insider signal is a blank** — obtain Form 4s independently; do not treat the absence of data here as a bullish or bearish tell.
