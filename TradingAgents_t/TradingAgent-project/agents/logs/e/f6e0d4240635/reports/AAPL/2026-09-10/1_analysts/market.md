## 5. Actionable Trading Plan

- Maintain a constructive bias while AAPL holds above the **315.28–317.99** support cluster.

- A wider invalidation level is below **309.90**, the 2026-09-09 low. That is roughly 16.7 points below the current close, or about 5.1%, and is consistent with a volatility-adjusted stop rather than a tight noise-level stop.

- Upside to 330.81: about **1.3%**
- Downside to 309.90: about **5.1%**

A bearish setup requires confirmation, not anticipation:

- Rejection near 329.42–330.81

- Follow-through below 309.90

Until then, the weight of the evidence favors the long side: price is above the 50-day and 200-day SMAs, above the VWMA, MACD is positive, and RSI is above 50.
