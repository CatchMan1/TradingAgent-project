- Trailing performance implied by the raw series: **+32.5% over 12 months** (254.04 → 336.64), **+23.8% YTD** (271.86 on 2025-12-31 → 336.64), and **+6.75% over the last month** (315.34 on 09/09 → 336.64). **The nuance traders should not miss:** the *medium-term* average is decelerating far faster than the long-term one.

- 50-SMA: 321.98 (09/29) → 322.39 (10/09), a slope of only **~+0.05/day**, versus ~+0.90/day in mid-July.

That is the arithmetic signature of a pause, not of a reversal. The 50-SMA is still rising and price is still above it, but the medium-term trend has stopped accelerating. Framing matters: this is a **consolidation inside an uptrend**, and the burden of proof remains on the bears until the 50-SMA (322.39) or the September swing low (315.34) is broken on a closing basis.

Inside that ceiling, price has spent the last two weeks compressing:

So: **a flat lid and a rising floor** — a coil, though not a textbook triangle because of the 10/09 low undercut. The measured range of the last eight sessions is roughly **330.6 to 341.6 (11 points, 3.3% of price)**.

**Anatomy of the 10/09 session is genuinely constructive.** Price gapped down 2.56% to 331.70, printed a low of 330.70, then closed at 336.64 — **75% of the way up a 7.91-point range**, finishing +1.49% above the open and +1.8% above the low. A long lower-shadow, upper-half close with a net negative day is best read as **defensive absorption, not a breakout**.

## 4. Momentum: A Reset, Not a Breakdown — and Now Decelerating Bearishly

**MACD line 3.428 vs. signal 3.870 → histogram −0.442.**

- The MACD peak was **6.248 on 09/22**; it has fallen 45% to 3.428.
- The bearish crossover occurred on **09/29**, when the histogram flipped from +0.537 to −0.283.
- The histogram bottomed at **−1.069 on 10/05** and has since improved to −0.443 (10/08) and −0.442 (10/09). Bearish momentum is **no longer expanding** — it has stabilized, and the MACD line itself ticked up from 3.250 (10/06) to 3.551 (10/08) before easing to 3.428.

Critically, **MACD has remained above zero continuously since 08/31** (0.297). A negative histogram above a positive zero line is the classic signature of a *momentum reset within an uptrend* — a pullback in the rate of change — not a trend inversion. A bullish recross requires roughly a +0.44 move in the MACD line (or a further decline in the signal line), which at current daily deltas is a matter of a few strong sessions, not weeks.

There is also a **mild bearish divergence at the high**, directly visible in the data: the 09/25 close (341.07) was higher than the 09/22 close (339.75), while MACD fell (6.211 vs. 6.248). Small, but it is corroboration that the September push was losing thrust.

**RSI 56.12** — squarely neutral, with room in both directions.

- RSI peaked at **66.88 on 09/22**, then made a *lower* high of 65.67 on 09/25 as price made a higher close — the same mild divergence MACD shows.

This is a **60% compression in band width in ten sessions**, and the tightest reading in the entire 60-session lookback. Concurrently:

- **ATR has fallen** from 8.05 (09/11) to 6.52 (10/08), ticking up to **6.747** on 10/09.
- ATR of 6.747 = **2.00% of price**, and the full Bollinger width (13.54) is almost exactly **2× ATR**. Bands that are only two average true ranges wide mean realized volatility has collapsed to an unusually low level relative to the instrument's own behavior.

**Implication:** a volatility contraction of this magnitude almost always resolves in an expansion, and the direction of the first decisive close outside the bands is the signal. It does *not* tell you the direction — that is why the breakout levels below matter more than any single oscillator reading. Note also that the middle band (335.39) is rising, so the squeeze is happening with an upward-sloping spine, which modestly favors an upside resolution.

Average volume over the last 21 sessions (09/11–10/09) is approximately **38.4M**; the 10/09 session printed **37.9M**, essentially exactly average. Recent sessions (09/25–10/09) have clustered in a **25M–50M** band, well below the 09/10–09/18 activity (70.0M, 50.7M, 86.6M).

- **Declining volume during a flat-to-slightly-lower price phase is constructive** — it indicates lack of supply rather than distribution. Sellers are not pressing.
- But the 10/09 gap-down absorption occurred on *average* volume, not heavy volume. There is **no evidence of institutional accumulation** in the tape either.
- The large-volume events in the window are instructive about risk: **06/25 at 107.0M** (close 275.15, −6.1%) and **06/26 at 261.8M**, and **07/31 at 132.5M** where the stock gapped from a 333.43 close to a 304.81 open and closed at 308.91 (−7.3%). Those were genuine distribution events of 7–9% magnitude. The current calm is a different regime — but it is a reminder that when AAPL does resolve, it can gap violently through stop levels.

VWMA at 335.66 is only $0.98 below price, confirming that recent volume-weighted transacting has occurred right at the current level — the market's average cost basis is here, not lower. A sustained move above VWMA is supportive; losing it puts recent buyers underwater.

## 7. Scenario Map — Specific Triggers and Targets

**Scenario A — Bullish squeeze resolution / continuation (base case, ~55–60%).**
Trigger: **daily close above 342.16 (upper band)**, with confirmation from a close above **345.34** (09/22 high) on above-average volume (>45M). That would break the 344.5–345.4 double top and reassert the primary trend.
- First target: the measured move of the 330.6–341.6 range (11.0 points) projected from 345.4 → **~356**, which also sits close to a 2-ATR extension (336.64 + 3×6.75 ≈ 357).
- Invalidation of the breakout: a close back below **338.6** the following session.

**Scenario B — Continued coil, time-based resolution (~25–30%).**
Price oscillates **330.6–342.2** for another one to two weeks while the 50-SMA catches up (it is rising ~0.05/day, currently 322.39, and the spread to price is 4.4%). In this case, treat 335.4–335.7 as the pivot: long-side entries at the *lower* band (328.6–330.7) with tight stops, and lighten near 341.5–342.2. Range traders should size down; ATR 6.75 means a 2-ATR band inside a 13-point band leaves little room for error.

**Scenario C — Range failure / mean reversion (~15–20%).**
Trigger: **daily close below 328.62 (lower band)** and, more importantly, below **325.81 (10/01 low)**. That breaks the rising-low structure.
- Next supports: **322.39 (50-SMA)** — the key medium-term line — then **316.22/315.34 (09/08–09/09 lows)**, then a vacuum toward the August congestion at ~310.
- A close below the 50-SMA would be the first objective evidence of medium-term trend damage; a close below 315.3 would confirm it and call the 344.5–345.4 double top into play as a valid reversal pattern.

**Stops and sizing using ATR 6.75:** a swing long entered near 336.6 with a 2-ATR stop sits at **~323.1** — right at the 50-SMA, which is uncomfortable (a 4% risk to a major support). A tighter 1.5-ATR stop is **~326.5**, below the lower band and the 10/01 low. For a $100,000 account risking 1% ($1,000), a 2-ATR ($13.49/share) stop permits ~74 shares (~$24,900 notional, 24.9% of equity). Position sizing should be cut, not the stop widened, if that is too much exposure.

## 8. Indicator Selection Rationale

Eight indicators were selected to cover trend (three timeframes), momentum (level and rate of change), volatility/mean-reversion (both bands), and risk (ATR), with deliberate avoidance of duplication:

- **close_200_sma** — establishes the primary regime; at +15.8% distance it proves the long-term bid is intact.
- **close_50_sma** — the medium-term line that defines whether the current pause is benign; its flattening slope and the narrowing 50/200 spread are the most important nuance in the trend picture.
- **macd** — confirms the momentum regime; staying above zero since 08/31 distinguishes a reset from a reversal.
- **macdh** — the cleanest read on crossover timing and on the *deceleration* of bearish momentum (−1.07 → −0.44). Using the histogram instead of the signal line avoids restating the same crossover twice.
- **rsi** — independent momentum confirmation and the mild high-level divergence; the fact it never reached 30 shows sellers lack control.
- **boll_ub** — defines the upside breakout trigger (342.16) and the boundary of the double-top zone.
- **boll_lb** — defines the downside failure trigger (328.62) and, with the upper band, quantifies the squeeze (4.04% width).
- **atr** — converts every level into a risk unit; at 2.00% of price it drives both stop placement and the observation that the bands are exactly 2 ATR wide.

Deliberately excluded: **vwma** (its 335.66 value and confirmation logic are captured from the verified snapshot, but it would duplicate the 10-EMA/20-SMA pivot discussion), **boll** (middle band is redundant with the 20-SMA already implied by the bands), and **macds** (fully derivable from MACD and histogram).

## 9. Bottom Line

AAPL is a **strong primary uptrend in a short-term pause at the highs**. Every long-term measure supports the bulls: price above a rising 200-SMA by 15.8%, above the 50-SMA by 4.4%, +32.5% over twelve months, no oversold RSI print during the entire pullback, and MACD above zero. Every short-term measure counsels patience: MACD is in a bearish crossover with a −0.44 histogram, RSI has cooled to 56, the 344.5–345.4 zone has now rejected price twice, and volatility has compressed so tightly (4.04% band width, the narrowest in 60 sessions) that the next move will likely be sharp and directional.

The trade is therefore **event-driven, not opinion-driven**: let the squeeze resolve. Above **342.2**, and decisively above **345.3**, the path of least resistance is toward **356**; below **328.6** and then **325.8**, the market is telling you the double top is real and **322.4 (50-SMA)** is the next test. Between those lines, at 336.64 with all three short-term averages within 0.3% of price, there is no edge in anticipating — only in reacting, and in sizing for a 6.75-point daily true range.
