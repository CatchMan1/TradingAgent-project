That event reset the entire trend structure. What has happened since is a textbook repair: bottom on 2026-08-12 (close 302.25, low 300.57), then a methodical series of higher lows and higher highs back to within 0.3% of the record close.

## 8. Risk Management

- **ATR is 7.26 points (2.14%).** A standard 1.5×ATR stop is ~10.9 points (3.2%); a 2×ATR stop is ~14.5 points (4.3%).
- **Position sizing:** for a portfolio risking 1% per trade at a 3.2% stop, position size ≈ 31% of the risk budget-equivalent notional (1.0 / 3.2 ≈ 31%). At 2% risk, ≈ 62%.
- **Gap risk is the dominant tail risk, not intraday volatility.** The 2026-07-31 gap of −7.36% on 132.5M shares exceeded 3× the current ATR. Any stop tighter than ~1.5 ATR is vulnerable to overnight repricing. If holding through late October (historically Apple's fiscal-Q4 report window), reduce size or hedge rather than relying on a stop.
- **The 2026-09-18 session (86.6M shares, close at 60% of range)** is a reminder that large-volume absorption can appear without warning near round-number resistance.
- **Dividend:** the series shows $0.27 on 2026-08-10 (and $0.26 on 2025-11-10, 2026-02-09; $0.27 on 2026-05-11) — roughly a 0.3% annual yield, immaterial to the technical picture but relevant to ex-date price drops.

## 11. Summary Judgment

**Bias: constructive, with a tactical pause at 340. The trend is real; the entry is not yet.**

The weight of evidence — price above all three moving averages, all three rising, a widening 50/200 spread, 18 consecutive positive MACD histogram sessions, RSI at 66 with headroom, volatility compressing then expanding upward, and VWMA below price — points to a **higher-probability breakout than breakdown.** The July high is 1.65% away and the setup beneath it is objectively better than the one that created it: premium to the 50 and 200 SMAs is lower, the base is broader, and ATR is 26% lower.

Against that: the approach is occurring on **0.81× average volume**, the MACD histogram has **declined 20.5% since 2026-09-03 while price rose 3.28%**, and MFI at 74.1 is above its overbought threshold. These are the marks of a *maturing* impulse, not a failing one — but they argue for waiting for confirmation rather than paying up.

**The two clean, asymmetric trades are: buy the 328–332 shelf with a stop below 323.5, or buy a confirmed close above 342.90 with a stop at 335.40.** Buying the current 338.98 into a wall of resistance at 340.08/342.89 offers approximately 1:2 reward-to-risk measured against ATR-based risk — that is the trade to avoid.
