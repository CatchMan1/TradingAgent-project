**Recommendation**: Overweight

**Rationale**: The bull case carried the debate, but not by enough to justify a full Buy at the current price. AAPL’s fundamental engine is exceptional: 16.15% nine-month revenue growth, 22.4% diluted EPS growth to $6.88, a record 50.05% Q3 gross margin, 48.7% incremental operating margin, $110.2B of nine-month free cash flow, and $62.2B of net cash. The technical primary trend is also intact—price is 12.7% above a rising 200-day SMA, the 50-day SMA has turned up to $315.15, and the MACD remains above both zero and its signal line. The bear’s strongest point is tactical, not structural: the 2026-09-04 rejection at the $328 upper Bollinger band and the July gap shelf, closing at $319.97 near the 10 EMA, shows real near-term supply. The bear is also right that the 2:1 bull setup is less clean once AAPL’s $7.64 ATR and 6–8% gap history are considered, and that record gross margin, the higher tax rate, inventory build, and services/regulatory headlines are legitimate forward risks. But those risks are unconfirmed and largely already survived in the trailing numbers; the bear’s case is principally a demand for proof and a better entry, not evidence of deteriorating fundamentals or a broken trend. The decisive balance is therefore constructive on AAPL but staged: participate on support or confirmation rather than chase a rejected breakout.

**Strategic Actions**: Treat a standard single-name allocation as 100% and build AAPL toward roughly 125% of that standard allocation, using staged entries rather than a full position at $319.97. First tranche: add 40% of the intended overweight only in the $315.15–$317.50 confluence if AAPL holds that zone on a daily closing basis; do not add merely because price touches it intraday. Second tranche: add 30% on a volume-confirmed daily close above $330.81 with at least 50M shares. Final tranche: add the remaining 30% only after the Q4 FY2026 report confirms gross margin guidance at or above 49.5%, inventory at or below the June $11.09B level, and a stable rather than higher tax rate. If AAPL never retests support and never produces a volume-confirmed breakout, keep exposure at the standard allocation and do not chase. Risk controls: a daily close below $315.15 cancels further adds and trims the tactical tranche; a daily close below $307.01 invalidates the August base, so cut back to standard allocation or below and reassess the $300–$302.25 and $298.22 supports. Size positions off the $7.64 ATR and AAPL’s gap history, capping initial-tranche portfolio risk at 0.5–0.75% and total position risk at no more than 1%. Monitor the UK App Store collective action and BASF patent suit for any quantified liability, ruling, or injunction, but do not treat unquantified headlines as thesis-breaking events.
