**Key reads:**
- **Revenue growth has re-accelerated sharply** — Q1 FY2026 +15.7% YoY, Q2 FY2026 +16.6% YoY, versus the ~low-single-digit growth profile implied by the FY2023–FY2025 cash-flow history. H1 FY2026 revenue of **$254.9B** implies a **>$500B annualized run-rate**, a step-change in scale.

### 3.2 Liquidity, leverage and quality

- **Gross debt** (LT debt incl. current + commercial paper): **$84.70B** at 2026-03-28 vs **$98.68B** at 2025-09-27 → **−$13.98B (−14%)** in six months.
- **Cash + marketable securities**: **$146.60B** vs $132.42B → **+$14.18B**.
- **Net cash position: ≈ +$61.9B**, up from ≈ +$33.7B at FY2025 year-end — a **~$28B improvement in six months**. This is the most under-appreciated data point in the filings: Apple is simultaneously buying back stock, paying dividends, and *still* de-levering into a large net-cash build.
- **Current ratio 1.07x** (144,114 / 134,641); **quick ratio ≈1.02x** — inventory is only 1.8% of assets, so liquidity quality is high.
- **Total liabilities / total assets = 71.3%** (down from 79.5%), the lowest leverage ratio in the periods shown.
- **Return on equity is extreme and mechanical**: H1 FY2026 net income of $71.7B against average equity of ~$90B. Annualized ROE is well over 100% because buybacks have kept the equity base small — do **not** read this as operating quality alone. ROA (annualized NI / assets) ≈ **38.6%** is the cleaner profitability gauge.
- **Intangibles jumped $12.8B gross / $12.5B net in six months** — with accumulated amortization rising only $321M, this is *newly added* intangible assets, i.e., a **material acquisition or asset purchase inside H1 FY2026** that is not separately broken out in the returned cash-flow tag set. Continuous amortization of this will be a modest EPS drag going forward. **Flag for the reader: this is a genuine, un-narrated corporate action sitting in the filings.**
- **Watch item / data caveat:** the `OtherAssetsNoncurrent` tag reads $93,146M at 2025-12-27 versus $77,430M at 2026-03-28 — an implausible $15.7B one-quarter decline that most likely reflects different tag bundling across the two 10-Qs (marketable securities may be aggregated differently). Do not trade off that line without the paper filing.
- **Deferred revenue** rose to $14.70B total (+7.3% since FY-end), a mild positive leading indicator for Services.

## 5. Working Capital, Receivables & Off-Balance-Sheet Items

- **Accounts receivable fell $9.4B (−23.7%)** to $30,339M while H1 revenue grew 16.1% — DSO improved dramatically (implied DSO ≈ 25 days on Q2 revenue vs ~38 days a year ago). Collecting faster is a cash tailwind but also can indicate channel mix toward indirect/carrier or prepaid structures.
- **Nontrade receivables fell $10.0B (−30%)** to $23,172M — this is the vendor/component-prepayment line. A sharp decline alongside rising inventory (+$1.0B) suggests **procurement prepayments unwound**; combined with a $12.5B cut in accounts payable, Apple has **released a large amount of supplier-funded working capital** ($22.5B of AR + nontrade + AP). That flow reverses at some point and is a **medium-term cash-flow headwind to model**.
- **Inventory is rising** ($5.72B → $6.75B, +18%) into a period of strong revenue — reads as healthy build-ahead, but worth monitoring for a demand-air-pocket signal at the Q3 print.
- **Lease and debt maturity schedule (FY2025 10-K, as of 2025-09-27):** LT debt principal due 12M: $12,393M; Yr2 $10,078M; Yr3 $9,300M; Yr4 $5,235M; Yr5 $4,972M; beyond Yr5 **$49,303M**. Operating lease payments total $14,725M (12M $1,967M; Yr2 $1,988M; Yr3 $1,848M; Yr4 $1,585M; Yr5 $1,381M; beyond $5,956M). Finance lease payments $1,579M. **Apple has already prepaid part of the near-term maturity wall** (current LT debt down from $12.35B to $8.31B), which is why the net-cash build is so large.
- **Hedged fair-value liabilities** fell $2.2B to $10.40B — modestly lower derivative/hedging exposure.

## 7. Synthesis: What the Fundamentals Actually Say

**Bull case (evidence-based)**
1. **Revenue re-acceleration to +16% YoY for two consecutive quarters** (H1 revenue $254.9B), a step-change from the FY2023–FY2025 profile.
2. **Gross margin at a dataset high of 49.3%** in Q2 FY2026, up 220 bps YoY — mix and pricing power intact.
3. **Operating margin at 34.0% H1** despite a 32.6% R&D surge — the company is funding an investment cycle out of operating leverage, not sacrificing profitability.
4. **H1 FCF of $78.3B** (+33% normalized), capex at just 1.7% of revenue — exceptional cash conversion.
5. **Net cash rebuilt to ~$62B** (+$28B in six months); gross debt −14%. Balance sheet is strengthening, not deteriorating, while still returning $44.7B.
6. **Equity up 44% in six months** ($73.7B → $106.5B), AOCI improving.

**Bear case / risks to monitor**
1. **Tax rate structurally higher at 17.5%** (vs 15.0–15.5%) — a permanent ~2.5pt drag on EPS growth relative to pre-tax growth.
2. **Buyback pace cut 25%** and share count shrinking only 0.71%/half — removes a key historical EPS tailwind. Consensus models that assume a ~2–3%/yr float shrink are too high.
3. **Working-capital release is not repeatable**: $22.5B of AR/nontrade-receivable/payable unwind flattered H1 CFO; expect reversal.
4. **Cash-tax benefit is non-recurring**: +$11.3B YoY benefit to CFO will not repeat.
5. **$12.8B of new intangible assets** appeared with no discrete acquisition line — an unexplained M&A/asset purchase that carries integration and future amortization risk.
6. **R&D +32.6% with no visible revenue attribution yet** — if the step-up does not convert into product-cycle revenue, margins compress.
7. **No June-quarter filing yet** as of 2026-07-22 despite Apple's pattern of late-July Q3 reports — event risk is concentrated in the coming days.
8. **Data gap:** no insider-transaction archive, and no price data in the tool set, so **valuation multiples (P/E, EV/FCF, FCF yield) cannot be computed from what was returned** — any multiple quoted to you from another source should be checked against these TTM-adjacent figures (H1 EPS $4.85, H1 FCF $78.3B, net cash $61.9B).

**Actionable positioning read:** The filings describe a company that is **growing revenue mid-teens, expanding gross margin, aggressively funding R&D, and rebuilding net cash while moderating buybacks.** That combination is fundamentally constructive, but the *EPS* optics are deliberately being held back (tax rate up, R&D up, buyback slower). Traders should distinguish between **business momentum (strong/accelerating)** and **per-share EPS leverage (decelerating vs. prior years)**. The most likely mispricing is on the cash-flow/de-levering story, not on the EPS beat/miss.

**Next catalysts to watch (evidence-anchored):**
- **Imminent Q3 FY2026 10-Q** — verify (a) gross margin holds ≥48%, (b) R&D growth, (c) whether buyback pace re-accelerates, (d) the working-capital reversal, (e) disclosure of the intangible-asset transaction.
- Q4/September FY2026 close for the full-year FCF and capital-return picture.

**Bottom line for traders:** AAPL's filings through 2026-03-28 show **accelerating top-line growth (+16%), record gross margin (49.3%), and a $62B net-cash position rebuilt by $28B in six months**, funded entirely from operations while buybacks were deliberately slowed 25%. The fundamentals are unambiguously stronger than the FY2023–FY2025 profile. The offsets are structural and important: a **permanently higher 17.5% tax rate**, **non-repeatable cash-tax and working-capital benefits inflating H1 cash flow**, **no EPS help from buybacks at the current pace**, and an **undisclosed ~$12.8B intangible-asset purchase**. With no June-quarter 10-Q in hand as of 2026-07-22 and no insider or price data available, the near-term trade is an event-risk trade into the Q3 report, while the medium-term fundamental case rests on whether the R&D step-up converts into durable revenue and whether gross margin can hold near 49%.
