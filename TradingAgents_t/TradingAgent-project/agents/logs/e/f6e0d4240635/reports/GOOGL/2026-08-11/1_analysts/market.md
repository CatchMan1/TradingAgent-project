### 7. Why These Eight Indicators

I selected eight, deliberately covering trend, momentum, volatility and volume without redundancy:

- **close_200_sma** and **close_50_sma** — the strategic/medium-term trend pair. Essential here because they are giving *opposite* signals (200 rising, 50 rolling over), which is the entire crux of this setup. The 50/200 spread is the only clean way to frame the "golden cross compression" clock.
- **close_10_ema** — the fast average that price lost on 8/11; gives the earliest read on whether the pullback is a dip or a trend change, and forms the 353–355 resistance cluster with the 50SMA.
- **macd** — needed specifically for the **zero-line** read (crossed positive 8/05), which the histogram alone cannot express.
- **macdh** — needed for the **rate of change**: five sessions of decay from +4.451 to +1.331 is the single most actionable early-warning signal in this tape and is invisible in the MACD line alone. (I skipped `macds` because its value is fully recoverable as macd − macdh, and the histogram is the more informative presentation.)
- **rsi** — the only oscillator selected. It anchors where past tradeable lows occurred (31.02 on 7/23, 33.27 on 6/26) and shows 45.85 is mid-range, i.e. no mean-reversion edge. (StochRSI omitted as redundant with RSI.)
- **atr** — non-negotiable at 3.61% of price, spiking off its July base; it sizes the stops and position, and around it the 1×/2×ATR targets are derived.
- **vwma** — the discriminator between a "healthy pullback" and "distribution." It confirms that the August advance was on lighter volume than the declines, and that price has lost the volume-weighted recovery zone.
- Bollinger bands were **not** selected as series but are read from the verified snapshot for the envelope top (381.88) and lower band (314.57), since the ATR/VWMA/50SMA combination already covers volatility and mean-reversion positioning.

**What would make me upgrade to outright bullish:** a higher low above 340 followed by a close above 355 with MACD histogram re-expanding. That combination — trend reclaim + momentum re-acceleration + volume confirmation — is not present today.

**Position sizing discipline:** with ATR at 12.42 (3.61%), a 2% account-risk budget implies a position roughly 1/3 the size of what the same risk budget allowed at July's 10.3 ATR. Widen stops, cut size.
