## 1. Company snapshot
Alphabet Inc. (ticker **GOOGL**) is the parent of Google — Search, YouTube, Google Cloud, Android/Play, hardware, and the "Other Bets" portfolio (e.g., Waymo, Verily). Its business model spans advertising (still the bulk of revenue), cloud infrastructure/AI services, subscriptions/devices, and a large portfolio of equity investments in private companies. The financial signature this quarter is unmistakable: **accelerating core revenue and operating profit, an aggressive AI-infrastructure capex super-cycle, and a very large non-cash investment revaluation gain.**

### Key reads
- **The headline is flattered by a massive non-cash investment gain.** Non-operating income of $37.72B was overwhelmingly driven by a **$36.92B fair-value gain on equity securities** (up from $9.76B a year ago). This is corroborated on the balance sheet: non-marketable equity securities without readily determinable fair value jumped from **$64.1B (12/31/25) to $101.3B (3/31/26)**, with cumulative *upward* price adjustments rising from $44.5B to $80.8B. This is a mark-to-market revaluation of a large private holding (e.g., a strategic AI/space stake) — **real economic value, but non-cash and non-recurring.**
- **Underlying operating performance is genuinely strong.** Operating income rose **+29.7%**, outpacing revenue growth of +21.8%, i.e. real operating leverage. Operating margin expanded 2.2 pts to **36.1%** — a standout for a company plowing money into AI.
- **"Clean" earnings:** Stripping the after-tax equity gains from both periods (using ~19% tax), adjusted net income ≈ **$32.8B in Q1 2026 vs ~$26.5B in Q1 2025 → +~24% YoY**, adjusted diluted EPS ≈ **$2.68 vs ~$2.15**. So the *core* earnings engine is compounding at roughly mid-20s% — still excellent, just far below the reported +81%.
- **Margin squeeze higher in the P&L:** Gross margin improved but **R&D grew faster than revenue (+25.6% vs +21.8%)** and G&A/S&M both rose ~21–23%, capping op-margin expansion.

**Actionable insight:** Do not extrapolate the $5.11 EPS or 56.9% net margin — both are inflated by the private-mark revaluation. Model forward earnings off **operating income** and treat investment gains as a volatile, below-the-line swing factor.

### Key reads
- **Capex is exploding.** Trajectory: $32.3B (2023) → $52.5B (2024) → $91.4B (2025) → and **$35.7B in Q1 2026 alone (+107% YoY)**, an annualized run-rate north of ~$140B. This is the AI data-center/compute buildout.
- **Free cash flow is being compressed.** Despite OCF rising +26.7% YoY in Q1, FCF *fell* ~47% to $10.1B because capex more than doubled. Full-year FCF has been essentially flat for three years ($69.5B → $72.8B → $73.3B) *because capex tripled* — a critical divergence for valuation models built on FCF.
- **Alphabet is now borrowing to fund growth and returns.** Q1 2026 net debt issuance of $31.4B is enormous (total debt carrying value jumped $49.1B → $80.3B). This is a notable strategic shift — the company historically self-funded with a large cash pile.
- **Buybacks were paused in Q1 2026 ($0 vs $15.1B a year ago)**, while the dividend continued ($0.21/share, up from $0.20). Capital return has pivoted toward **funding AI capex + preserving flexibility**, supported by debt and minority-interest raises.

**Actionable insight:** The capex ramp is the single biggest swing factor for GOOGL's FCF and hence valuation. Watch management's capex guidance and any signs of FCF inflection. The combination of rising capex, paused buybacks, and new debt issuance signals a management team that expects high returns on AI infra — but it lowers near-term FCF yield and raises the bar for ROI execution.

## 5. Synthesis & actionable takeaways for traders

**Positives**
1. **Core momentum is excellent:** revenue +21.8%, operating income +29.7%, operating margin 36.1% (+2.2 pts). Clean-adj. earnings up ~24% YoY.
2. **Cloud/AI demand is visible and contracted:** $467.6B RPO backlog signals durable multi-year growth.
3. **Fortress balance sheet:** ~$47B net cash, trivial leverage, ample liquidity — the capex super-cycle is more than fundable.
4. **Dividend intact and growing** ($0.84 annualized run-rate), plus an untapped capacity to resume buybacks.

**Watch-items / risks**
1. **Earnings quality:** Reported net income ($62.6B) and EPS ($5.11) are heavily inflated by a **$36.9B non-cash private-investment gain**; do not anchor valuation on these. On a recurring basis, EPS is roughly half the headline.
2. **FCF compression:** Capex +107% YoY crushed Q1 FCF to ~$10B (−47%); full-year FCF has been flat for three years. FCF-based valuations need to bake in the capex drag before it inflects.
3. **Capital-allocation shift:** Buybacks paused ($0 vs $15.1B), new debt issued ($31.4B) — management is prioritizing AI investment. This is a bullish statement of confidence but raises "ROI-on-capex" execution risk.
4. **Tax rate creeping up** (17.3% → 19.2%), a mild EPS headwind.
5. **Marks are volatile:** The same private-stake revaluation that boosted Q1 could reverse in a weak funding/tech environment, injecting large GAAP swings.

**Net read:** GOOGL shows a **fundamentally strong, accelerating core business** entering the peak of an AI capex cycle. The quarter "beat" optically due to a one-time investment gain, but the underlying operating engine (+30% operating income) is the real story. For traders, the key debates are **(a) the ROI/payback on the ballooning capex and (b) the timing of an FCF inflection** — both of which will drive the multiple more than the GAAP EPS line.
