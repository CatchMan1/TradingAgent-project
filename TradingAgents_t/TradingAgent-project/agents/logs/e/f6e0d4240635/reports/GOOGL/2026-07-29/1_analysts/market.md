## 6. RSI — A Failed Oversold Print

Two readings of this, and both deserve weight:

RSI at **43.57** is now squarely mid-range and offers **no actionable edge in either direction**. The prior June low was **33.27 on 6/26**, so the July low of 31.02 was a marginally lower momentum low — again confirming the July break was structurally worse than June's.

Mechanically: a 10% squeeze resolved violently to the downside. Price **closed below the lower band on two consecutive sessions** — 7/23 (317.69 vs lower band 329.21) and 7/24 (319.74 vs 323.96) — a statistically stretched extension. It then **re-entered the band on 7/27** (326.56 vs 321.85) and has stayed inside since. **Band breach followed by re-entry is a well-known short-term mean-reversion trigger**, and it is the cleanest bullish setup in this chart right now.

But note where the bands are pointing: the **lower band is still falling hard** (336.82 on 7/22 → 329.21 → 323.96 → 321.85 → 319.82 → **318.35**), and the **mid-band (349.62) is now declining** (peaked ~358.5 in early July). The dynamic mean is now overhead resistance rather than support. The 7/29 close is **3.69% below the mid-band** and **5.77% above the lower band** — i.e., the stock has room to run either way inside a very wide envelope.

Notably, **the lower Bollinger band at 318.35 is almost exactly the 7/23–7/24 closing low zone (317.69–319.74)**. That confluence is the most defensible support level on the chart.

## 8. Volume — The Weakest Link in the Bull Case

This is where the bounce argument gets thin.

- The **breakdown** on 7/23 came on **69.4M** shares.
- The **rebound** has come on **31.8M (7/24), 28.5M (7/27), 29.5M (7/28), 27.5M (7/29)** — an average of ~29.3M, versus a ~28.8M 20-session average I derive from the daily rows above. **The rally is running on perfectly average volume.** There is no evidence of institutional accumulation; it looks like short covering and dip-buying.
- **VWMA is at 341.31, above the 336.71 close.** Price has been **below** the volume-weighted average since roughly **7/17** (close 346.77 vs VWMA 358.20). In VWMA terms, **the average dollar transacted over the recent period is above the current price — the marginal buyer is underwater.** VWMA has fallen from 358.74 (7/20) to 341.31 (7/29), confirming the distribution.

The one genuinely bullish volume datum is the **2026-06-26 bar: 114.7M shares** (by far the largest in this 165-row dataset) which closed at 337.39 after trading down to **330.20**, and was followed by an immediate reversal to **353.65 on 6/29**. That high-volume reversal low was *broken* on 7/23, which is why the July break carries more weight than June's.

**Interpretation:** volume confirms the down-move and fails to confirm the up-move. That asymmetry argues the bounce is counter-trend until proven otherwise.

**ATR (14) = 11.67**, or **3.47% of the close**. ATR behaved exactly as expected for this sequence: it compressed to **10.31 on 7/14** (the squeeze), spiked to **12.22 on 7/23** (the gap), and has settled at 11.67.

Practical implications:
- A **1.5× ATR stop** from 336.71 = **~319.2** — which lands almost precisely on the Bollinger lower band (318.35) and the 7/23–24 close cluster. Risk = ~17.5 points (**5.2%**).
- A **2× ATR stop** = **~313.4**, i.e. just below the 7/23 low of 314.90. This is the "give the trade room past the obvious stop-hunt level" option. Risk = ~23.3 points (**6.9%**).
- A **1× ATR upside objective** = **~348.4**, essentially the declining Bollinger mid-band at 349.62.

Given 3.47% daily ATR, **position size should be roughly 60–70% of what it would be at a 2% ATR name** for the same dollar risk. With a 319 stop and a 349 target, the reward:risk is approximately **0.7:1** — which is *not* attractive for a fresh long. That math is the core reason the recommendation below is not a buy.

## 12. Recommendation: **HOLD (neutral, risk-managed)** — Do Not Chase the Bounce

**The bull case** is real: price is above a rising 200-day SMA, the 50/200 alignment is still bullish, the 200-day undercut was reclaimed within two sessions, RSI refused to break 30, price re-entered the lower Bollinger band after a two-day breach, MACD histogram has improved three straight sessions, and GOOGL has four consecutive higher closes with higher highs and higher lows (7/23 low 314.90 → 7/29 low 331.62; 7/27 high 330.42 → 7/29 high 342.50).

**The bear case is currently stronger on evidence:** 27 sessions below the 50 SMA, 6.6% below that average and falling, price below VWMA, MACD at its most negative reading of the window and below signal, a −21% drawdown with unbroken lower highs, and — decisively — **the breakdown happened on 2.4× average volume while the recovery is happening on exactly average volume.**

The tiebreaker is **asymmetry**: with ATR at 3.47%, a long entered at 336.71 with a sensible stop at ~319 offers roughly 0.7:1 reward:risk to the 349.62 mid-band. That is a poor bet. Meanwhile the bounce has already delivered +5.99% from the 7/23 close in four sessions — the easy mean-reversion money has been made.

**For existing holders:** Hold, but define the line. A close below **318.35** (Bollinger lower band, coincident with the 7/23–24 closes) invalidates the re-entry thesis and argues for trimming. A close below **314.90** confirms trend continuation down and favors a materially reduced position. Do not average down above 325; wait for structure.

**For prospective buyers:** Do not buy at 336.71. Two valid triggers:
- **Trigger A (breakout confirmation):** a daily close above **342.09** (full gap fill) on volume **>40M**, with MACD histogram turning positive. That shifts the risk/reward materially.
- **Trigger B (retest with divergence):** a pullback into **318–325** that holds, with RSI making a higher low above ~40 and a bullish candle at the Bollinger lower band. That is the higher-quality entry, with a stop under 314.90.

**For traders already short:** The histogram improvement, the band re-entry, and the 200 SMA reclaim argue for tightening stops to protect gains near the 341–342 zone; pressing shorts into support at 318–325 has poor expectancy.

**Key risk event:** The 7/23 and 4/30 gaps both show the signature of information-driven repricing. Another such event can invalidate any technical read overnight — position sizes should assume gap risk well beyond ATR, since ATR understated the 7/23 move (an ~24-point gap versus an ~11-point ATR).
