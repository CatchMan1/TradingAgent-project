But converting one capex-heavy quarter into a ~0.9% FCF yield is an acknowledged worst-case construction, and $533M quarterly interest against $39.7B quarterly operating income is capital intensity, not insolvency.

The bear decisively wins execution, and I side with the neutral synthesis over the trader. The aggressive analyst is too casual on regulation. The New York data-center moratorium and 14 states considering restrictions are ambiguous, not cleanly bullish — permitting friction defers Alphabet's own AI monetization even as it raises barriers for others.
