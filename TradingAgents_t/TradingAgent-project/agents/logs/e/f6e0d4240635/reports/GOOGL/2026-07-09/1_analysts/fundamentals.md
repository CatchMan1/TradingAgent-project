**Key reads / actionable signals:**

3. **Capital-allocation pivot: buybacks paused, debt issued.** Q1 FY2026 buybacks dropped to **$0** (from $15.1B) while the company issued **~$31B of net new debt**. Alphabet has flipped from a net capital-returner to a net capital-raiser to fund the AI buildout — a notable strategic shift (and mildly dilutive to per-share returns near-term). Shares outstanding actually ticked **up** slightly (12,088M → 12,116M) since SBC dilution is no longer offset by repurchases.
4. **External funding for Other Bets.** Minority-shareholder proceeds of **$3.2B** in Q1 FY2026 (vs $0.4B) suggests outside capital being raised into a venture (Waymo is the most likely candidate), validating optionality in Other Bets while reducing Alphabet's own cash outlay.
