## 1. Executive Summary

Alphabet's Q2 2026 report shows a **two-speed fundamental picture** that traders must separate:

- **Core operations are accelerating and highly profitable.** H1 2026 revenue reached **$229.7B (+23.0% y/y)**, with Q2 revenue **$119.8B (+24.2%)** — faster than Q1's +21.8%. Operating income rose **+30.0%** to **$80.5B** in H1, with operating margin expanding ~190bp to **35.0%**.
- **Headline EPS is heavily distorted by non-cash investment gains.** **$135.8B** of H1 2026's **$216.2B** pretax income (≈63%) came from debt/equity securities gains; in Q2 alone, non-operating income was **$98.0B** versus operating income of **$40.8B**. Headline Q2 diluted EPS of **$9.11** compares with a **core (ex-non-operating) EPS of ~$2.68** (+26% y/y).
- **Free cash flow has collapsed.** H1 capex more than doubled to **$80.6B (35.1% of revenue)**, driving H1 FCF down to **$4.3B** from **$24.3B**, and **Q2 FCF turned negative (~–$5.9B)**. Capex incurred but unpaid jumped to **$29.1B**, implying continued heavy Q3 cash outflows.
- **Capital structure changed materially.** Alphabet issued **$30.5B of common stock**, **$19.1B of convertible preferred**, and **$56.2B of net debt** in H1, while **buybacks went to zero** (vs. $28.3B in H1 2025). Total debt nearly doubled to **$101.1B**; interest expense rose ~6x.

**Bottom line:** the operating business is performing better than at any point in recent history, but the quality of reported earnings, the capex/FCF trajectory, and the new dilution/debt funding model are the swing factors for the stock. Valuations based on headline EPS will be badly misleading.

## 2. Company Profile

**Alphabet Inc. (NASDAQ: GOOGL)** is the parent of Google and one of the world's largest internet and technology companies. Its business is organized around:

- **Google Services** — Google Search and other advertising, YouTube ads and subscriptions, Android, Chrome, Google Play, Maps, hardware (Pixel/Nest), and subscription products (YouTube Premium, Google One).
- **Google Cloud** — Google Cloud Platform infrastructure, data/analytics, and Google Workspace productivity software; the primary enterprise AI distribution channel.
- **Other Bets** — long-horizon ventures including Waymo (autonomous driving), Verily and Isomorphic Labs (life sciences/AI drug discovery), and Wing (drone delivery).

**Share structure:** Class A (GOOGL, voting), Class B (super-voting, insider-held), Class C (GOOG, non-voting). GOOGL is the instrument analyzed here.

### 3.2 The quality-of-earnings issue — read this before using the P/E

- **H1 2026 equity securities gains: $135.9B** (of which **$99.0B in Q2 alone**), versus **$11.0B** in H1 2025.
- These are largely **mark-to-market, non-cash revaluations** of Alphabet's investment portfolio. Supporting evidence: deferred tax liabilities jumped from **$0.9B to $22.8B** (+$21.9B), and "equity securities without readily determinable fair value" rose from **$64.1B to $124.3B**, with cumulative upward revaluations climbing from **$44.5B to $85.7B**.
- **Implication for traders:** a screen showing GOOGL at a single-digit P/E on trailing GAAP EPS is an artifact. On an annualized core basis, H1 2026 core EPS is **~$10.6**; on headline GAAP it is **~$28.5**. The gap is ~$18/share of investment marks that can reverse in a risk-off or down-round environment.

### 3.3 Operating momentum details

- **Revenue accelerated** from +21.8% in Q1 2026 ($109.9B) to +24.2% in Q2 2026 ($119.8B).
- **Gross margin +240bp in H1** — the strongest signal in the report; indicates AI-related compute costs are being more than offset by revenue mix/pricing.
- **Operating leverage:** opex grew 25.6% vs revenue +23.0%, so incremental margin remained positive but thinner than the gross-margin gain implies.
- **Stock-based compensation:** $15.2B in H1 (+32.2%), 6.6% of revenue vs 6.2% — dilution pressure is rising alongside cash comp.
- **Depreciation:** $13.6B in H1 (+43.2%), a direct consequence of the AI capex build; this will keep climbing and is a structural headwind to future margins.
- **Tax rate step-up to 19.1%** from 17.1% is a ~200bp EPS headwind and appears structural (mix/foreign minimum taxes), not one-off.

### 4.1 Liquidity and leverage
- **Net cash position remains enormous:** $242.5B cash + investments vs. $101.1B debt and $18.0B convertible preferred → **~$123B net cash**. Current assets of $343.5B provide a very comfortable current ratio (roughly 2.3–2.4x on an estimated current-liability base).
- **But the direction of travel is notable:** debt **+$52.0B in six months**, interest expense **$1.81B vs $0.30B** (+514%), and interest income of $2.81B means net interest income fell to **$1.0B** from $1.76B. Interest coverage is still a very healthy **~44x** operating income.
- **Deferred tax liabilities of $22.8B** are the accounting footprint of unrealized investment gains — a future tax drag if positions are monetized, and a reversal risk if marks fall.

### 4.2 Working capital and demand signals
- **Receivables +10.0% vs revenue +23.0%** → DSO improved; collection quality is strong. Receivables are ~54 days of H1 revenue (annualized), a healthy level.
- **Deferred revenue (contract liabilities) $10.1B, +17.4%** → forward bookings/Cloud commitments still growing, though lagging revenue growth.
- **Accounts payable +66%** and **capex incurred but unpaid of $29.1B** (vs $10.6B a year ago) confirm a supplier-financed infrastructure build; this is a leading indicator of continued large cash capex in Q3.
- **Legal accrual of $17.4B** is a material regulatory overhang (antitrust/remedies). The FY2025 10-K discloses **$3.0B of loss-contingency payments in Q3 2025**.

**Key observations:**

3. **The funding mix shifted from self-funding to external funding:**

## 9. Actionable Trading Insights

1. **Normalize earnings before doing any valuation work.** Use **core EPS ≈ $10.6 annualized** (H1 2026 core $5.31 × 2) or **NOPAT ≈ $130B** (annualized operating income of $160.9B taxed at 19.1%). Headline EPS of ~$28.5 annualized embeds ~$18/share of investment marks.
2. **Watch FCF, not EPS, as the primary signal.** If Q3 capex stays at a ~35%+ of revenue run-rate, FY2026 FCF will be dramatically below FY2025, and the equity story becomes "AI infrastructure investment cycle" rather than "cash-return compounder." The **$29.1B of accrued but unpaid capex** guarantees another large Q3 cash outflow.
3. **Monitor the funding/dilution path.** Total debt is $101B and rising, buybacks are paused, and convertible preferred now sits on the balance sheet. Any resumption of buybacks would be a strong positive signal; any further equity or preferred issuance is a negative signal on FCF durability.
4. **The investment portfolio is now a major earnings driver — treat it as a separate risk factor.** A ~$136B H1 gain implies a very large concentrated position that was revalued upward. A reversal of even a third of that would wipe out a full year of core earnings growth in reported EPS. Ask which asset drove the Q2 $99B gain before sizing a position.
5. **Operating momentum argues for constructive exposure on weakness; headline multiples argue for caution on strength.** The clean operating business grew operating income 30% with expanding margins — that is a premium-quality asset. But the stock is now a leveraged play on private-market AI valuations as well as on search/cloud.
6. **Valuation scaffolding (arithmetic, not a price target — current market price was not available from these tools):**
   - Annualized NOPAT ≈ $130B; net cash ≈ $123B; shares ≈ 12.3B.
   - At **20x** NOPAT: EV $2.60T + $0.12T = ~$2.72T → **~$221/share**
   - At **25x** NOPAT: EV $3.26T + $0.12T = ~$3.38T → **~$275/share**
   - At **30x** NOPAT: EV $3.91T + $0.12T = ~$4.03T → **~$328/share**
   - Apply a discount if you believe capex intensity structurally lowers Alphabet's through-cycle FCF conversion.
7. **Catalyst calendar:** Q3 2026 earnings (typically late October) — focus on capex guidance, Cloud/segment margins, buyback resumption, and whether the equity marks hold. No SEC filings were accepted in the seven days to 2026-09-17, so there is no fresh filing catalyst in the immediate window.

**Suggested framing:** *constructive on the operating franchise, neutral-to-cautious on reported EPS quality and FCF, and alert to dilution.* Position sizing should reflect that this is now two exposures in one ticker: a 35%-margin advertising/cloud business and a large, volatile, mark-to-market AI investment portfolio.
