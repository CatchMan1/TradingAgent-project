## 9. Nuances, Caveats and What Would Change the Thesis

1. **The 3-day rally is unconfirmed.** Closes of 343.50 → 346.47 → 347.68 on 23.8M → 25.6M → 18.7M shares is a *drifting* advance, not a demand-driven one. Today's low volume into the 50 SMA is the single biggest argument for patience.
2. **The MACD signal is real but small.** A zero-line cross at +0.13 on a $348 stock carries little statistical weight; the 2026-09-21–22 false start is a live precedent.
3. **The 50 SMA turn is only four sessions old.** One down-week will re-flatten it and re-narrow the 50/200 gap toward a cross.
4. **The Oct 1 outside day was absorbed.** The 2026-10-01 session (open 350.79, high 353.22, low 335.51, close 338.24, 33.3M volume — a ~2.1 ATR range) was fully recovered within three sessions, and price is now above the pre-drop close of 344.08. That is genuine evidence of demand and the strongest bullish datapoint in this report.
5. **Upside is capped by dated supply.** Until $364.17 is cleared, every rally is a countertrend bounce inside a May-to-present corrective structure.
6. **Event risk.** The squeeze is likely to resolve on a company-specific catalyst rather than drift. Alphabet's Q3 report has historically landed in late October — if that pattern holds, the window for a quiet range is short. *(This timing is an assumption, not derived from the provided tools.)*
7. **Data limitation.** The price history supplied begins 2026-03-01, so no 52-week high/low, and no pre-March context, can be established. Long-term claims are confined to the 200-day average and the March–May swing points.
