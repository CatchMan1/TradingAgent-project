## 6. Expected price impact and horizon

**Base case — near-term (0–3 trading sessions): modestly negative skew, high bounce risk.**
The 2026-09-23 close ($337.83, −3.8%) already discounts much of the bad news. Two forces now compete:
- *Downside:* momentum/CTA selling after a high-volume −3.8% day; the 10Y at ~5.11% keeps duration-heavy megacaps under pressure; Meta Connect follow-through coverage extends the "Muse threat" narrative for another 24–48h.
- *Upside:* the **Gemini 4 headline is a live, dated positive catalyst** and is exactly the kind of announcement that reverses a "Google is losing the model race" narrative. Analyst "bad news for Google" quotes (3b) are opinion, not data.

I would characterize the setup as **negative momentum into a positive catalyst window** — asymmetric to the upside if Gemini 4 is dated/benchmarked within days, but vulnerable if the tape keeps selling off on yields.

**Medium term (1–4 weeks): competitive-positioning referendum.**
The market is being asked to re-price Google Search economics against agentic interfaces (Muse, and by extension OpenAI/Anthropic). This is a **multiple** question, not an EPS question: no revenue data in the evidence shows any Search deterioration. Watch for (a) any Gemini 4 benchmark/pricing detail, (b) enterprise reaction to the Gemini security incident, (c) whether "Muse" coverage broadens from telecom/insurance into advertising.

**Longer term (quarters): hyperscaler capex and AI-financing scrutiny.**
The IMF + MIT + Ed Zitron cluster is the beginning of a **capex-ROI debate**, which historically compresses hyperscaler multiples before it touches earnings. Alphabet's Anthropic stake (§4b) is the natural hedge here — a marked-up public stake would let the market value AI exposure outside the capex line.

**Relative-performance call.** I expect GOOGL to remain a **high-beta expression of the AI-complex** near term: it likely underperforms on AI-de-rating days (as on 09-23) and outperforms sharply on Gemini-4-type product days. The stock is not, on this evidence, trading on its own fundamentals.
