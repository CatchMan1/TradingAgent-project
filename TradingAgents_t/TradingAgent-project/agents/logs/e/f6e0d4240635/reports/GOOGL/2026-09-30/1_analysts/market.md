## 3. Trend architecture

**Primary trend — bullish, but only modestly.**
The 200-day SMA has risen every session in the reviewed window: 326.91 (2026-08-03) → 338.60 (2026-09-30), a slope of roughly +0.10–0.17 points per session. Price at 344.08 is **+1.62% above it**. Notably, the only closes below the 200-day since July were:
- 2026-07-23 close 317.69 vs 200-day 323.56 (−1.8%)
- 2026-07-24 close 319.74 vs 200-day 323.90 (−1.3%)
- 2026-09-09 close 330.65 vs 200-day 336.40 (−1.7%)
- 2026-09-10 close 332.60 vs 200-day 336.62 (−1.2%)

Both undercuts were **reclaimed within 1–2 sessions** (2026-07-27 close 326.56; 2026-09-11 close 338.50). That is the single most constructive structural fact in the chart: the rising 200-day has repeatedly absorbed supply, producing failed breakdowns rather than a trend break.

**Intermediate trend — down/neutral.**
The 50-day SMA has declined monotonically from 358.43 (2026-08-03) to 343.93 (2026-09-30), −14.5 points. Price has printed **lower major highs**: 400.80 close (2026-05-08), 377.65 close (2026-08-04), 364.17 intraday (2026-09-22). The April 30 earnings gap (open 374.07 vs prior close 349.94, volume 72.04M) has been **entirely retraced** — price traded down to 314.90 on 2026-07-23. That round-trip is a medium-term bearish tell even though the 200-day holds.

Importantly, the 50-day is now **flattening fast**: 344.29 (09-25) → 344.21 (09-28) → 343.99 (09-29) → 343.93 (09-30), a slope of only ~−0.06/session versus ~−0.30/session in early September. It is transitioning from a falling resistance line into a horizontal pivot.

**Short-term trend — fading.**
The 10-day EMA bottomed at 337.63 (2026-09-10), rose to 346.68 (2026-09-22), and has now declined four straight sessions to 343.66. Price sits 0.42 above it. The short-term impulse is over; the question is whether it becomes a higher-low consolidation or a rollover.

**The spread that matters:** 50-day (343.93) minus 200-day (338.60) = **5.33 points (1.6%)**. On 2026-08-03 that spread was 31.5 points. At the current rate of convergence (~0.17 points/session) it would close in roughly 30 sessions — mid-November. Not a forecast, but a **strategic risk marker** that coincides uncannily with the triangle apex discussed below.

**10 EMA / 50 SMA / 200 SMA stack**

## 7. Scenarios and triggers

**Base case — continued coil (highest probability, ~55%).**
Range 336–356 into mid-October. Bollinger bandwidth stays under 8%, RSI oscillates 45–58, MACD hovers around zero. Action: no position at 344; sell strangles/condors or simply wait. Mean-reversion trades only at the edges.

**Bull resolution (~20%).**
- **Trigger:** daily close above 355.30 (2026-09-22 upper band) and then above 359.44 (2026-09-18 high), on volume >1.3× the 20-day average (roughly >34M).
- **Confirmation:** MACD back above zero; 10 EMA reclaims the 50 SMA; RSI >58.
- **Targets:** 364.17 (2026-09-22 high) → 377.65/380.52 (2026-08-04 close/high) → 384.80 (2026-04-30 close).
- **Invalidation:** a close back below 347 after the breakout.

**Bear resolution (~25%).**
- **Trigger:** daily close below 340.99 (2026-09-30 low), confirmed by a close below 338.60 (200-day) / 337.45 (2026-09-23 low).
- **Targets:** 330.65 (2026-09-09 close) → 330.35 (lower band) → 327.74 (2026-09-10 low) → 317.69/314.90 (2026-07-23).
- **Structural confirmation:** the 50-day crossing below the 200-day (spread only 5.33 points) — a mid-November risk if the drift persists.
- **Invalidation:** a reclaim of 344 on a closing basis.

**Bull trap / whipsaw (~10% or part of base).** A push through 352–355 that immediately reverses (as a repeat of 09-30) would trap breakout buyers and often produces an accelerated move to 330–334. This is precisely why the 09-30 close matters: the market has already shown it will sell that zone.
