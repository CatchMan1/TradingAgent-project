Scenario A — Continuation (my base case, ~55% weight). Requirements: hold above 373.51 and, more defensively, above 367.50; MACD crosses zero; volume holds above ~30M.

Scenario B — Rejection / lower-high failure (~30% weight). A stall at 380.52–386.76 with declining volume would create a new lower high only if it fails below the 402.62 May peak — but more importantly, a rejection at the May supply shelf after an 18.9% eight-day vertical move is a classic exhaustion signature.

  • Deeper failure target: 317.69/314.90 retest, which would make the 7/23 low a double bottom if held, or a fresh breakdown if not.

Scenario C — Choppy digestion (~15% weight). Most probable near-term path given 2-ATR extension: a 3–10 session range between 363 and 382, letting the 10-EMA (352.04 and rising ~$3–5/session) and 50-SMA (358.23) catch up. This is where patient swing traders get a low-risk long at the 10-EMA rather than at today's close.

Risk management, concretely:

  • ATR is 12.87 (3.41% of price). A 1-ATR stop is $12.87; a 2-ATR stop is $25.74. Anything tighter will be stopped by ordinary noise in this regime.
  • Cash volatility sizing: to risk 1% of equity per position with a 2-ATR stop, position size = 1% ÷ 25.74 per share ≈ 0.0389% of equity per $1 of share price — i.e. roughly half the share count you would use in a 1.5% ATR name.
  • Do not add to a position on a green day below 382.85. Both unfilled gaps below are legitimate downside magnets.

What would change my mind (bearish): a close below 358.23; a MACD histogram that rolls over before the MACD line crosses zero; or an RSI divergence (price > 380.52 with RSI < 62.56). What would confirm the bull case decisively: MACD zero-line cross + a close above 382.85 + a weekly close above 386.76 on expanding volume.

Event-risk caveat: the −7.1% gap on 2026-07-23 on 69.4M shares is inconsistent with normal technical drift and implies a discrete catalyst. Nothing in the price recovery removes that event risk; the technical structure is repaired, the fundamental question that caused the gap is not addressed by any indicator here.
