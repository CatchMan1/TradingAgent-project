## 2. Company Profile

**Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL)** — the parent of Google, operating the world's largest digital-advertising franchise (Search, YouTube), a scaled cloud/AI platform (Google Cloud, Workspace), a subscription/device/app ecosystem (Play, YouTube Premium, devices), and a venture-style portfolio of "Other Bets" (autonomy, life sciences, etc.). GOOGL is the Class A common line.

The single most important profile change visible in the 2026 filings: **Alphabet has become a capital-intensive, externally-financed AI infrastructure builder with a very large investment portfolio that now drives reported earnings.** That is a structural shift from the 2023–2024 "cash-machine + buyback" model, and it changes how the equity should be valued.

| **Total debt & equity securities gain** | **135,803** | vs. $11,411M in H1 2025 (11.9×) |

Corroborating balance-sheet evidence: *non-marketable* equity (no readily determinable fair value) rose **$64,094M → $124,259M** in six months, while cumulative upward price adjustments rose **$44,485M → $85,732M** and cumulative downward adjustments only $8,820M → $9,115M. The gap between +$113.6B of recognized gains and only +$41.2B of cumulative upward adjustments implies **~$72B of stakes migrated into marketable securities** (i.e., an observable-price event such as an IPO/secondary in a major portfolio holding during Q2 2026).

**Key readings:**

2. **But the direction is unmistakable:** debt went $12.0B → $49.1B → $101.1B in 18 months (8.4×). Interest expense confirms the cost: **H1 2026 $1,811M vs H1 2025 $295M (+514%)**, and Q2 alone was $1,278M — an annualized run-rate near $5B+, up from ~$0.6B in FY2025.

## 6. Cash Flow & Capital Allocation — The Story That Matters

### 6.1 Cash flow

| Dividends | 0 | 7,363 | 10,049 | 4,977 | 5,231 | 2,542 | 2,689 |

- **Operating cash flow is accelerating hard** (+32.8% y/y in H1 2026), and OCF margin is a superb 36.9% of revenue (vs 34.2%). The business is generating more cash than ever.
- **But capex more than doubled (+103%) and FCF collapsed 82% to $4.3B for the half.** Q2 2026 delivered **negative free cash flow of −$5.9B** — an extremely rare event for GOOGL and the central bear datapoint.
- **Forward capex visibility is worse than the cash number suggests:** "capital expenditures incurred but not yet paid" jumped to **$29,113M** (from $10,635M a year earlier, and $15,090M at FYE2025). Cash capex therefore *understates* the accrual-basis run-rate; Q3 2026 capex plausibly exceeds $45–50B. Expect further FCF pressure.

|---|---|---|

| Proceeds from minority shareholders | +3,758 | +400 |
| Buybacks | **0** | (28,306) |
| Dividends (+ $86M preferred) | (5,231) | (4,977) |
| **Net financing inflow** | **+86,320** | (26,033) |

- Alphabet raised **~$109.6B of external capital in six months** and simultaneously **stopped buying back stock entirely** (after $45.7B in FY2025 and $62.2B in FY2024). Share count **rose 1.18%** to 12.230B — the first sustained net dilution in years.
- The **$19.0B convertible preferred** is new, ranks ahead of GOOGL common, and carries an ~$0.1B annual preferred dividend; conversion would add future dilution.
- The company also spent **$22,051M on "other investments"** (vs $2,312M) — buying private stakes — and **$76,480M on marketable securities** (vs $39,870M), while adding **$8.1B of finite-lived intangibles** (gross $2,467M → $10,585M), indicating bolt-on M&A.

**Interpretation:** management is funding the largest capex cycle in the company's history partly by selling equity and preferred stock. This is the single most important signal of management's *scale* of AI ambition — and, simultaneously, evidence that even a $165B-OCF business cannot self-fund it without levering up.

## 10. Risks (ranked by evidence weight)

1. **Free-cash-flow compression / capex escalation (highest conviction).** Q2 2026 FCF −$5.9B; capex 37.5% of revenue; $29.1B of accrued-but-unpaid capex. If capex guidance rises again in Q3, FCF stays negative through 2026.
2. **Earnings-quality reversal risk.** $113.6B of H1 gains came from non-marketable stake remeasurement and $21.5B from marketable marks. Both are non-cash and reversible. A risk-off tape or a portfolio-company de-rating could turn 2026's record EPS into a comp nightmare in 2027. Q2's non-operating swing was $98.0B vs $2.7B a year ago — the entire y/y EPS delta of +$6.80 is essentially this line.
3. **Capital-structure/dilution regime change.** Buyback halt + $30.5B equity issuance + $19.0B convertible preferred + minority-interest raises ($3.8B) remove a decade-long bid under the stock and add senior claims.
4. **Rising tax rate + deferred tax build.** 13.9% → 19.1% effective rate; ~$22.8B net deferred tax liability (from $0.9B). Each 100bp of rate ≈ $1.6–2.0B/yr on core pre-tax earnings.
5. **Depreciation & SBC acceleration.** Depreciation +43% y/y and SBC +32% y/y are structural drags on future margins as AI assets enter service.
6. **Legal/regulatory overhang.** Current loss-contingency accruals $17.4B (from $6.3B at YE2024) — quantifiable and likely to grow; episodic charge risk.
7. **Asset intensity.** Total assets +55% while revenue +23% — capital efficiency is declining for the first time in the modern Alphabet era.

## 11. Actionable Trading Insights

**Bull case (evidence-backed)**
- Revenue growth accelerated for five straight quarters to **+24.2%** in Q2 2026 — demand, not accounting.
- **Core EPS +22% y/y** with **operating margin +188bp** *despite* a 43% depreciation increase. Operating leverage is real.
- Operating cash flow **+32.8%** to $84.9B in H1; cash conversion of 131% of core earnings.
- **~$139B net cash** and ~45× interest coverage — the AI spend is a choice, not a constraint.
- Deferred revenue +17.4% and $8.1B of new intangibles suggest monetizable backlog and capability build.

**Bear case (evidence-backed)**
- **Negative Q2 FCF (−$5.9B)** and an FCF collapse of 82% for the half; capex accruals point to more.
- Reported EPS is **2.7× core EPS**; the beat is investment marks, not operations.
- **Zero buyback, net share issuance, $19B preferred, debt 8.4× in 18 months** — the shareholder-return model has been suspended.
- Effective tax rate up 520bp since FY2023, and a $22.8B deferred tax liability awaiting realization.

**How to trade / position**
1. **Value GOOGL on core EPS (~$5.3/H1), not GAAP.** Ask what multiple you'd pay for ~$11 annualized core EPS growing >20% with 35% operating margins and $139B net cash. Apply your own multiple — price data is outside this toolset.
2. **Fade headline-EPS euphoria, buy operating momentum.** If the stock re-rated on the +178% GAAP EPS print, that multiple is not defensible. If it re-rated on +24% revenue and +30% operating income, it is.
3. **Key catalyst dates:** Q3 2026 report (~late Oct 2026). Watch, in order: (a) capex guidance and whether FCF turns positive, (b) whether buybacks resume, (c) depreciation trajectory, (d) any reversal of investment marks, (e) legal accrual changes.
4. **Asymmetry skew:** the downside from a mark reversal (potentially $30–80B of pre-tax charges in a sharp drawdown) is large but *non-cash*; the downside that actually matters is a second consecutive negative-FCF quarter combined with unchanged capex guidance, which would break the "self-funding compounder" narrative.
5. **Relative-value / hedge ideas:** if long GOOGL for the AI-capex-growth thesis, consider hedging the earnings-quality leg (e.g., short-dated protection around the Q3 print, when marks and comps are most in focus), or pair against a peer with less mark-to-market income distortion.
6. **Monitoring checklist for next 30 days:** Q3-2026 10-Q acceptance (~2026-10-30), any new debt issuance or preferred conversion terms, 8-K disclosures on portfolio-company liquidity events (the source of the $113.6B gain), and Form 4 insider filings (unavailable here — verify externally).
