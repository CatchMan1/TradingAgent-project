GOOGL is in a **corrective consolidation inside a still-intact long-term uptrend**. That combination — falling volatility, contracting range, neutral RSI, flat short-term averages — describes a **coiled base resolving toward a decision**, not a trend in motion. The decisive pivot is a very tight band at **$349.5–$351.6**, where four independent measures converge.

**Bias: neutral-to-constructive, pending a volume-confirmed breakout above ~$352 or a break of ~$338.5.** This is a "trade the resolution, don't predict it" setup.

### Scenario B — Bearish resolution
**Trigger:** Daily close **below $338.50** (breaks the ten-session floor of $338.57), ideally on volume > 35M.
- **Targets:** T1 **$333.4** (200 SMA — the line the bulls must defend) → T2 **$330.3** (2026-07-30 low) → T3 **$325.7** (Bollinger lower band) → T4 **$314.9** (2026-07-23 cycle low).

- **Structural warning:** a close below the 200 SMA would be the first since the uptrend began and would materially raise the probability of the 50/200 death cross now mathematically on track for late Q4 2026.

### Scenario C — Continued coil (base case probability weight)
Price oscillates **$338.5–$352** for another 2–4 weeks. Signature would be further ATR decay toward $8 and Bollinger bandwidth under 12%.
- **Play:** range-fade only, small size, mean-reversion entries near $340–342 (range low) with stops under $338, and lightening near $350–352. Do not chase. **Avoid positioning ahead of a squeeze you cannot confirm.**

With an annualized ~$0.87 that is a ~0.25% yield — immaterial to price, but it will show up as a price adjustment in raw series.

### Position-management guidance
- **Pre-breakout:** size at 50–75% of normal. Realized vol is at a 3-week low; a squeeze resolution can gap through stops.
- **Use limit orders inside the coil.** Market orders in a $9.33-ATR name with 20M-share days invite slippage.
- **Do not use the 200 SMA as an entry trigger alone** — at $333.38 it is 4.4% below spot and rising; it is a *context* level, not a signal level.
