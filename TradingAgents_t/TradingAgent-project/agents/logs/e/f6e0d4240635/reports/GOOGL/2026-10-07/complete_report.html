8. Risks and Counterarguments
  1. Earnings-quality risk / reversal risk. The $135.9B gain is marked through the income statement. If the underlying equity holding declines, the same accounting channel produces losses.   5. Dilution. 142M shares issued in H1 (+1.17%) plus $30.5B of stock consideration, SBC +32% YoY, and preferred conversion. Buybacks at $0 remove the offset.
  6. Regulatory/legal. $17.4B of accrued loss contingencies and rising accrued taxes remain live risks.
  7. Concentration of the beat. With operating margin only 34–36%, the reported 76% net margin is a red flag for anyone screening on trailing multiples.
