Since no portfolio context was provided, we cannot assume the caller is oversized or risk-intolerant. Sizing should be expressed in multiples of normal target weight, and 0.5–0.7x is a more balanced range than 0.4–0.5x for a de-risking plan that explicitly preserves optionality.

So my adjustment to the trader’s decision is this. Keep the SELL/underweight instruction for GOOGL, but treat it as capital preservation first and tactical distribution second. I would keep the target at roughly 0.5–0.7x normal target weight, trimming 30–50% of the existing position, with the lower end of that range for oversized or risk-sensitive accounts and the upper end for long-term holders who want residual exposure. No new money and no outright short sale.

A daily close above 347.40 on more than 27 million shares should pause further trimming and allow the residual to participate, because that would be the first real repair signal. A daily close above 351.40 on strong volume would invalidate the bearish tactical thesis for the residual, but I would not chase GOOGL back to full weight. Treat 351.40 as a closing invalidation line, not as a guaranteed stop, and size the position so that a gap through it does not force a bad decision.

It accepts the conservative analyst’s emphasis on position size, gap risk, and a time limit, but it rejects the idea that the majority must be dumped at the neutral mid-band. For GOOGL, the sustainable strategy is staged de-risking into strength, a smaller residual, no new money, and clear invalidation levels. That gives us protection if the 200-SMA breaks while preserving enough optionality if Gemini 4 or a broader tech rebound repairs the tape.
