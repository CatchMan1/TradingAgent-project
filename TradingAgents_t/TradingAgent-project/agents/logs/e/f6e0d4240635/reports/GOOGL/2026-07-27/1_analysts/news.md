3. **$205B vs $44.9B.** Two very different magnitudes appear across two headlines. I treat $205B as most likely the multi-year/annual envelope and $44.9B as the quarter, but this is inference, not stated fact.
4. **No primary document.** No press release, no 8-K/10-Q excerpt, no earnings-call transcript, no analyst-target-change wire, no price/volume data. Direction of the post-earnings move is stated qualitatively ("selloff") with no magnitude.
5. **Sampling bias.** 100 of 116 eligible records were withheld, and none of the supplied records falls in the Mon–Fri trading week. Intraweek analyst downgrades/upgrades and the actual first-day market reaction are unobserved.
6. **Macro overlay.** "A Fed rate hike is on the table" (Yahoo, 07-26 19:31 ET) is a headline-level macro claim from a market-preview piece; if true it is a valuation headwind for GOOGL independent of company news. I do not treat it as verified.
