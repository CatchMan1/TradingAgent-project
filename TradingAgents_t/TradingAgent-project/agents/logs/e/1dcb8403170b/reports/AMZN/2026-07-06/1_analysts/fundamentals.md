## 1. Company Profile & Structure
Amazon.com, Inc. operates across three reported operating segments (confirmed `NumberOfOperatingSegments = 3`):
- **North America** – retail, marketplace, advertising, Prime
- **International** – retail/marketplace/advertising ex‑US
- **AWS** – cloud computing, now the primary profit engine and the driver of the current capital‑expenditure super‑cycle

Business mix reflects Amazon's transformation from a low‑margin retailer into a higher‑margin cloud + advertising company. Operating margins have structurally expanded (see §3), powered by AWS, advertising (expenses $23.5B in FY2025, up from $21.4B FY2024 and $20.3B FY2023), and a maturing logistics network. The current story, however, is dominated by an **AI/data‑center capex buildout** and a related **large, non‑cash equity‑investment gain** (see §2–§3).

## 7. Actionable Insights for Traders

1. **Underwrite the operating trend, discount the headline EPS.** The +76.6% net‑income / +74.8% EPS growth is flattered by a ~$12.3B non‑cash equity mark‑up. The *real* signal is **+29.6% operating income and margin expansion to 13.1%** — that is durable and bullish. Expect volatility if the market "looks through" the gain or if a future quarter lacks such a mark‑up and EPS optically decelerates.

2. **FCF compression is the key swing factor.** TTM FCF is **negative (~−$2.5B)** and Q1 capex was a record $44.2B. If management guides capex higher again, expect FCF‑focused investors to pressure the stock; if AWS revenue re‑accelerates and capex growth plateaus, the capex‑to‑growth conversion becomes the bullish catalyst.

3. **Watch the debt trajectory.** A ~$53B single‑quarter LT‑debt raise (LT debt +78% QoQ) marks a strategic shift to debt‑funded AI buildout. Still investment‑grade‑comfortable (D/E ~0.31, ~$144B liquidity), but rising interest expense ($0.80B in Q1, +48% YoY) and continued large issuance would be a leverage risk to monitor.

4. **Margin quality is improving at the core.** Gross margin 51.8%, operating margin 13.1%, rising advertising and AWS mix, and declining SBC ($19.5B FY2025) all support a structurally higher‑margin story — the strongest fundamental leg to lean on.

5. **Acquisitions accelerating** (~$15.4B in Q1 2026, $19.3B TTM) — potential inorganic growth/AI positioning (consistent with the large private‑equity investment mark‑up), but also a cash draw to factor into FCF.

6. **Data gaps to close before trading:** (a) **insider activity — unavailable**; (b) full‑year FY2025 revenue was not directly tagged (Q1 alone implies a ~$726B annualized run‑rate); confirm via the 10‑K. Do not treat Q1×4 as actual revenue (Q4 is seasonally strongest).
