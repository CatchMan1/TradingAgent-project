## 1. Company Profile
Amazon.com, Inc. (AMZN) is a global technology and e-commerce conglomerate that reports **three operating segments** (confirmed by `NumberOfOperatingSegments = 3` in the latest 10-Q): **North America, International, and Amazon Web Services (AWS)**. The business spans online retail/marketplace, advertising, subscription services, logistics, and cloud computing / AI infrastructure (AWS). Its scale is enormous: Q1 FY2026 quarterly revenue of **$181.5B** and total assets of **$916.6B**.

The defining fundamental narrative of the current period is a **massive re-acceleration of capital investment (AI/data-center build)** funded increasingly by **debt issuance**, combined with **healthy revenue growth and operating-margin expansion**, plus a large **non-operating equity-securities revaluation gain** that is inflating headline net income.

## 6. Trader-Relevant Takeaways
1. **Top line & operating profitability are genuinely strong:** +16.6% revenue and 13.1% operating margin (a record-ish level) show the core retail+cloud+ads engine is firing on all cylinders. This is the bullish backbone.
2. **Headline EPS is flattered:** ~$12.3B of the quarter's pre-tax income came from non-operating equity revaluation. On operating earnings alone, the quarter is solid but less spectacular than $2.78 suggests. Watch for reversal risk in future quarters if private valuations mark down.
3. **FCF is the headline risk:** Negative TTM free cash flow, with capex exceeding OCF, means Amazon is in a debt-funded investment sprint. Watch the capex trajectory and any management commentary on capex guide for the pivot signal that would re-rate FCF.
4. **Balance-sheet shift to leverage:** ~$53B of new LT debt in one quarter. Credit metrics are still comfortable given $144B liquid assets, but the era of fortress balance sheet + buybacks is on pause.
5. **Valuation framing:** With GAAP earnings inflated by one-off gains and FCF temporarily negative, EV/EBITDA or operating-income-based multiples are more reliable than P/E or P/FCF for the next few quarters.
6. **Bears vs bulls:** Bear case = capex overrun + slowing FCF + rising leverage + earnings-quality noise. Bull case = accelerating revenue/margin expansion + AWS/AI monetization + still-nascent payoff from the enormous capex program.
