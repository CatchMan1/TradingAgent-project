And a 1.15–1.25x allocation into the Q2 print, with a prior 9.7% earnings-related gap-down in the dataset, is not moderate risk. The stop at $243.75 also assumes you get filled near it. A gap can blow through it.

AMZN’s operating trend is improving, the tape is constructive, and the Q2 print could resolve higher on AWS strength and Prime Day spend. If AMZN clears $256.50 on volume and then confirms after earnings, waiting for everything could force a chase. The correct response to uncertainty is smaller size and a coherent stop, not zero participation.

So my neutral adjustment is this: keep the direction as a BUY on AMZN, but moderate the implementation. Cap the total intended allocation at roughly 0.75 to 1.0x standard, not 1.15–1.25x. If the caller is already at or above that exposure, do not add; if underweight, build gradually toward that cap. Pre-Q2 deployment should be capped around 40–50% of the intended position, not 70%. Hold the remaining 50% for either a disciplined pullback toward $246.40–$248 after the event or post-Q2 confirmation that capex guidance and FCF are not deteriorating further.

The stop needs to be made internally consistent. Preferred approach: if you want to keep the $241.38/$238.81 dip ladder, use a decisive close below $238.81 as the structural invalidation and cut total size enough that dollar risk stays comparable to the original plan. If you insist on the $243.75 stop, then delete the sub-stop dip adds at $241.38 and $238.81; those are not executable under the same plan. I would rather use the structural stop with smaller size than a tight stop that fights its own ladder.

Halt adds if capex guidance steps materially higher while FCF is still negative, or on a decisive close below $238.81. That gives the aggressive analyst participation in the momentum, while respecting the conservative analyst’s valid event-risk and balance-sheet cautions. For AMZN, the most reliable strategy is not to chase full size into the upper band before Q2, and not to stand aside entirely. It is to stay constructive, size at or slightly below standard, cap pre-event exposure, and make the breakout confirmation earn the next tranche.
