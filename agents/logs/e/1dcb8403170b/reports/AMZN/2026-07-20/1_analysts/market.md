## 1. The primary regime: intact long-term uptrend, mid-cycle consolidation

**Price is +6.7% above the 200-day SMA (234.21), and that average is rising monotonically** — from 230.33 on 2026-05-21 to 234.21 on 2026-07-20, a gain of ~+1.7% in two months. A steadily rising 200-SMA with price above it is the cleanest available definition of a bullish primary trend.

**But the intermediate trend is still repairing.** The 50-day SMA has been declining without interruption since 2026-06-18 (257.10) and now sits at 251.633 — i.e. price (249.99) is **0.65% BELOW its 50-SMA**. That is the defining tension in this chart: structural bull trend, intermediate distribution/correction. The 50-SMA is still rolling over and will keep pressuring the 251–252 zone for several more sessions before it flattens.

**Long-term structure is still bullish but the cushion has thinned.** On the retrieved window (2026-05-21 onward) the 50-SMA crossed below nothing, and stands +7.4% above the 200-SMA — a wide "golden-cross" spread. However, because the 50-SMA is falling, that spread is compressing (from roughly +13% on 2026-05-21 implied by 240.79 vs 230.33, to +7.4% now). This is characteristic of a trend digesting a large advance, not of a trend reversal — yet.

**Important structural fact worth flagging:** the only clearly confirmed closes below the 200-SMA in the visible series were 2026-06-25 (close 227.01 vs 200-SMA 232.795), with 2026-06-22 an essentially flat/marginal miss (232.79 vs 232.84). Price reclaimed the 200-SMA on 2026-06-29 (close 240.14 vs 232.82) and has closed above it on every session since. That is a factual, tool-supported recovery sequence — not an inferred "bounce."

## 2. Short-term trend: a constructive base is building

The short-term structure has clearly turned up from the June low:

- **10-day EMA is rising sharply**: 236.70 (2026-06-26) → 247.60 (2026-07-20). Price closed above it on 7/15, 7/16, and again on 7/20, and is currently +0.97% above it.
- **Higher lows are traceable**: the June capitulation low was 225.55 (2026-06-25). Subsequent swing lows stepped up to 240.52 (7/8), 243.80 (7/14) and 243.59 (7/17).
- **Higher highs on the swing**: 256.48 (7/15 high) → 258.08 (7/16 high). The 7/17 pullback held 243.59.
- **10-EMA (247.60) is converging up toward the 50-SMA (251.63)** — a short-term average crossing back above a medium-term average would be the confirmation signal that the June–July correction is complete. It has not happened yet.

**Volume-weighted confirmation:** VWMA has bottomed and turned — 236.52 (2026-06-30) → 245.58 (2026-07-20), with the acceleration concentrated in the last five sessions (237.35 on 7/13 → 245.58, ~+3.5%). Because VWMA is volume-weighted, its rise means the *volume-weighted* average transaction price is being dragged upward, i.e. buyers are transacting at higher prices — an accumulation signature. Price sits +1.80% above VWMA, a healthy but not stretched premium.

## 3. Momentum: the most constructive part of the setup, with one caution flag

**MACD delivered a complete bull sequence in July:**
1. **Signal-line crossover (bullish)** on 2026-07-01, when the histogram flipped positive (+0.470) after being negative since at least early June (−3.885 on 6/10).
2. **Zero-line crossover (bullish)** on 2026-07-15, MACD turning positive (+0.260) for the first time in the visible series, from −0.654 on 7/14.
3. MACD has since extended to +0.824 with the signal line at −0.622 — both rising, MACD above signal.

**Caution:** the histogram peaked at 2.124 on 2026-07-15 and has narrowed for three consecutive sessions (1.946 → 1.576 → 1.446). Momentum is still positive but *decelerating*. That typically precedes either a controlled pullback into the 10-EMA/VWMA zone or a sideways digest — not necessarily a reversal.

**RSI at 54.04 is neutral-to-constructive.** Key observations:
- RSI bottomed at 33.63 (2026-06-25) and never reached classic oversold (<30) on this decline, i.e. the June selloff was orderly, not panic-driven.
- RSI has rebuilt an ascending sequence: 46.20 (6/29) → 52.71 (7/9) → 53.10 (7/14) → 60.14 (7/15).
- **There is a minor negative divergence:** price printed a *higher* high on 2026-07-16 (258.08 vs 256.48 on 7/15), while RSI *fell* from 60.14 to 54.19. Price closed that day at 249.89, i.e. well off the high — an intraday rejection rather than a confirmed breakout.
- Importantly, RSI at 54 leaves ~16 points of headroom to overbought. In a rising-trend regime this is the "room to run" zone, not a warning zone. RSI becomes a warning only on a break of ~45 or a failure at ~60.

## 4. Volatility: compression, then an upside resolution attempt

- **ATR(14) = 7.454**, equal to ~2.98% of price. ATR has contracted from its 8.591 peak on 2026-06-29 to 7.454 — realized volatility is cooling as the base forms.
- **Bollinger bandwidth** = (256.647 − 228.026) / 242.336 = **11.81%**, versus roughly 20%+ on 2026-06-22 ((276.85 − 226.14)/251.49 = 20.2%). That is a major contraction — a classic squeeze.
- The squeeze is currently resolving **upward**: the upper band has risen from 250.37 (7/8) to 256.647 while the lower band is flat-to-lower (228.00 area). Rising upper band + flat lower band = bandwidth expanding in the direction of the move.
- Price is **inside** the bands (2.66% below the upper band, 9.6% above the lower band) — no band-tag breakout yet. The upper band at **256.65** is the next mechanical hurdle and it lines up almost exactly with the 7/15–7/16 highs of 256.48–258.08. That confluence is the single most important level on the chart right now.

**For risk sizing:** 1× ATR ≈ 7.45 points; 1.5× ≈ 11.2; 2× ≈ 14.9. A stop placed just under the 7/17 swing low (243.59) from a 250 entry is ~6.4 points ≈ 0.86 ATR — tight, and at meaningful risk of noise-triggered exit given the 7.45 ATR.

- Interpretation: the +1.2% advance on 7/20 (247.23 → 249.99) occurred on light volume. The MACD zero-line cross on 7/15 came on 45.24M, which is only modestly above average. **This rally lacks volume conviction.**
- The constructive counter-argument is the rising VWMA (Section 2): cumulative volume is still accumulating at higher prices, even if the last session was thin.
- Validation test: a decisive break of 252.89–256.65 should ideally come with **>50M shares** to be trusted. A breakout on sub-35M volume is a fade candidate.

## 8. Why these eight indicators were selected

1. **close_200_sma** — establishes and grades the primary trend; rising slope plus price above = bull regime. Non-negotiable for context.
2. **close_50_sma** — captures the *intermediate* problem: a declining 50-SMA directly overhead at 251.63. This is the pivot level of the whole setup.
3. **close_10_ema** — the responsive entry instrument; identifies the short-term upturn and the first pullback support at 247.60.
4. **macd** — the cleanest evidence of regime change in July (signal cross 7/1, zero cross 7/15); trend-momentum, not price-relative.
5. **macdh** — separates *direction* from *acceleration*; it is the only tool here showing momentum decelerating while MACD is still rising (peak 2.124 on 7/15 → 1.446 on 7/20). Without it, the divergence would be invisible.
6. **rsi** — independent momentum scale for overbought/oversold and divergence; flags the 7/15→7/16 price-high/RSI-lower-high divergence and confirms ~16 points of headroom.
7. **atr** — converts all of the above into a risk budget (7.45 ≈ 3% of price) and sizes stops correctly in a post-squeeze regime.
8. **vwma** — volume-weighted truth serum. It is the only indicator here that tests whether the July advance has participation behind it; its 236.5 → 245.6 rise materially strengthens the bull case, and it doubles as support at 245.58.

*Deliberately excluded for redundancy: macds (subsumed by macd + macdh), boll/boll_lb (the middle and lower band add little beyond what 50-SMA/200-SMA/ATR already frame — the upper band is cited as context from the verified snapshot), and stochrsi-type double-oversold oscillators.*
*Note on the snapshot's `mfi` reading of 0.630132: the scale is ambiguous (0–1 normalized vs 0–100). If normalized, ~63 would read as moderately positive money flow without being overbought — a mild tailwind for the bull case. Because the scale cannot be verified from tool output, no conclusion is drawn from it.*

## 9. Summary verdict

**Bias: constructive/cautiously bullish, but the trade is a range trade until 252.89–256.65 falls on volume.** AMZN is in a confirmed long-term uptrend (+6.7% over a rising 200-SMA) that has completed most of a mid-cycle correction: MACD has crossed both its signal and the zero line, RSI has rebuilt from 33.6 to 54.0 with room to run, ATR and Bollinger bandwidth have contracted (a squeeze resolving with a rising upper band), and VWMA is climbing. Against that: price is still 0.65% below a *declining* 50-SMA, the MACD histogram has narrowed three sessions running, the 7/16 price high was met with a lower RSI (minor negative divergence), and 7/20's advance came on ~66% of typical volume. Net: **the path of least resistance is up, but confirmation requires a volume-backed daily close above 252.89.**
