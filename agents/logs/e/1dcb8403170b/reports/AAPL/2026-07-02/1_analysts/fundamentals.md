# AAPL — Fundamental Research Report
**Company:** Apple Inc. (AAPL) · **Analysis date:** 2026-07-02 · **Data source:** SEC filings point-in-time through 2026-07-02

## 1. Company Snapshot
Apple Inc. designs, manufactures and markets smartphones (iPhone), personal computers (Mac), tablets (iPad), wearables/home/accessories (Watch, AirPods, Vision), and a large and growing Services portfolio (App Store, iCloud, Music, advertising, Apple Pay, etc.). The company is characterized by an extremely strong brand, a tightly integrated hardware–software–services ecosystem, high margins, and enormous free cash flow that is returned to shareholders via buybacks and dividends.

**Key observations:**

- **Equity is compounding fast:** $73.7B → $88.2B → $106.5B over two quarters (+44% in six months), driven by retained earnings and buyback-driven share-count reduction.

- ⚠️ **Watch item — Intangibles jump:** Gross intangibles surged **+$12.8B** ($24,950M → $37,767M) while accumulated amortization barely moved (11,649M → 11,970M). This pattern strongly suggests a **business/asset acquisition (~$12–13B) completed in H1 FY2026** (technology/IP), rather than organic amortization. This is a notable capital-allocation development and should be verified in the 10-Q notes. It was largely *not* reflected in operating cash-outflow lines, hinting it may be a stock/asset transaction.

**Capital returns:** Share count fell from **14,773.3M** (FY25-end) to **14,667.7M** (Q2 FY2026) — roughly **105.6M shares (~0.7%) repurchased in six months**, on top of a robust dividend. As of 2026-04-17, shares outstanding were 14,687.4M.

## 6. Fundamental Scorecard & Actionable Insights

**Strengths (supportive for a constructive stance):**
1. **Growth re-acceleration:** ~16% YoY revenue growth in H1 FY2026 — a clear regime shift from Apple's historical single-digit pace.
2. **Record profitability:** Gross margin ~48.6–49.3%, operating margin ~34%.
3. **Best-in-class cash generation:** H1 OCF +53% YoY; free cash flow massively exceeds capital returns.
4. **Fortress balance sheet:** ~$62B net cash; improving liquidity (current ratio back above 1.0x); compounding equity.
5. **Aggressive shareholder returns:** ~$37B buybacks + ~$7.7B dividends in H1, shrinking share count and boosting per-share metrics.

**Risks / watch items (arguments for caution):**
1. **Rising R&D (+33%) and opex** — good for the future but a margin swing factor if revenue growth decelerates.
2. **Elevated effective tax rate (17.5% vs 15%)** — a ~250bp drag on net income/EPS conversion.
3. **Suspected ~$12–13B intangible/acquisition event** in H1 FY2026 — needs confirmation from filing notes; capital-allocation and integration risk to monitor.
4. **Buyback deceleration** — if sustained, reduces the EPS-per-share tailwind that historically flattered growth.
5. **Valuation unassessable here:** No price/valuation data in the dataset, so no P/E, EV/EBITDA or FCF-yield conclusion can be responsibly drawn from these tools — traders must overlay market-price data.

**Bottom line for traders:** From a pure fundamentals standpoint, AAPL's latest filings depict a company **re-accelerating growth while expanding margins and gushing free cash flow**, backed by a net-cash balance sheet — a distinctly positive fundamental backdrop. The main fundamental caveats are the sharp R&D ramp, a higher tax rate, and the notable intangible/possible-acquisition increase. Combine with price/valuation data before acting.
