Let me challenge the aggressive analyst first. You are leaning hard on stacked averages, expanding MACD, rising VWMA, and contracting volume on the pullback. The 25% increment is fine only if it brings total AMZN exposure to no more than the standard allocation, or if the caller consciously chooses to run above standard with a separate risk budget.
