AWS faces well-funded competition, and the AI race is likely to pressure pricing and require heavy investment. Retail is low-margin and consumer-sensitive. Advertising is high-margin but competitive. The more AMZN invests to defend the moat, the more pressure on free cash flow.

You also have no news or sentiment report in this run. That means no external confirmation of positive catalysts, no read on regulatory/antitrust headlines, and no macro/consumer signal. That cuts both ways — and it should reduce conviction, not increase it. A bull case built on “the franchise will re-rate” needs catalyst visibility. Right now, the data package doesn’t provide it.
