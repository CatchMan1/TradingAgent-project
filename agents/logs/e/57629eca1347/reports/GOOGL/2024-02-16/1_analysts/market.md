## 2. The Dominant Story: A Post-Earnings Gap That Has Not Been Recovered

The dataset contains two clearly different regimes:

**Regime A — orderly uptrend into late January.** Closes walked from 135.73 (2024-01-05) to 153.51 (2024-01-29), a +13.1% advance in 16 sessions on modest volume (21–34M). RSI reached **78.25 on 2024-01-29** (overbought), and price closed above the upper Bollinger band region (01-29 upper band 153.83, close 153.51 — riding the band, the classic "strong trend" RSI/band condition).

**Regime B — event shock and failed repair.** On **2024-01-31** GOOGL opened at 143.62 versus the prior close of 151.46 and closed 140.10 on **71.9M shares** (~2.6× the 21-session average of 32.9M). That left an **unfilled gap between 143.62 (01-31 open) and 151.19 (01-30 low)**. I will not assert *why* the gap happened (an earnings-type event is consistent with the timing/volume, but the tools do not state a cause).

The important technical fact is what happened afterwards: the rebound rally **stalled at 149.44 on 2024-02-09**, i.e. *inside* the 01-31 gap, and rolled over without closing it. In gap analysis that is a textbook **gap-fill failure** — sellers defending the gap. Since 02-09 the stock has printed five consecutive lower closes except one: 149.00 → 147.53 → 145.14 → 145.94 → 142.77 → **140.52** (−5.69% from the 02-09 close, −8.46% from the 01-29 close of 153.51).

## 3. Trend Structure: Three Timeframes, Three Answers

This is not a simple "broken chart." The structure is genuinely layered:

- **Long-term (200 SMA): intact uptrend.** The 200 SMA rises every session in the series (126.97 on 01-17 → 131.42 on 02-16) and price is **6.92% above it**. Nothing in the data says the primary bull trend is over.
- **Medium-term (50 SMA): freshly broken.** The 50 SMA is *still rising* (139.38 on 02-02 → 141.47 on 02-16), yet **2024-02-16 is the first close in the entire visible dataset below it** (140.52 vs 141.47). Every January close and every early-February close sat above the 50 SMA. This is a brand-new signal and it is *marginal* (−0.67%, less than a third of one ATR), which makes it fragile in both directions.
- **Short-term (10 EMA): clearly down.** The 10 EMA has turned over (146.19 on 02-12 → 144.52 on 02-16), price lost it on **2024-02-13** (close 145.14 vs EMA 146.00) and has not reclaimed it. The 20-day baseline (Bollinger mid 146.23) has also begun to flatten for the first time since 01-31, and price has closed below it for four straight sessions.

The average stack is still bullish — 20 SMA (146.23) > 50 SMA (141.47) > 200 SMA (131.42) — but the 20-vs-50 spread is compressing. If price cannot reclaim the mid-band, the 20 SMA will cross below the 50 SMA on the current trajectory, converting this from "pullback" to "medium-term deceleration."
