Base case (highest probability): range-bound chop 139–146 with a downward skew. The MACD histogram re-expansion, sub-50 RSI with no oversold cushion, declining VWMA above price, and light-volume bounce all point to continued base-building/erosion rather than a V-recovery. This is a "do less" regime for trend-following.

Bullish trigger — requires two confirmations, not one:

  1. A daily close above 143.90 (10 EMA) that also clears 145.98 (Bollinger middle) within the same week; and
  2. Volume ≥ ~38M (≈116% of the 20-day average) on the breakout bar. Then: initial target 149.00–149.44, secondary 153.51–153.78. Stop below 140.10 (≈1.2×ATR from a 145 entry would be tighter than the structure — prefer the structural stop at 140.10 and size accordingly). Nothing about the current tape justifies buying before those confirmations; a close between 141.70 and 143.90 is noise inside a flag.

Bearish trigger (the squeeze resolution I'd lean toward):

  1. A daily close below 139.49, ideally accompanied by volume > 35M; or
  2. A close below the 50 SMA (141.70) for a third consecutive session while the MACD line completes its zero-line cross. Then: target 137.72 (lower band), then 136.50 (post-gap low). A break of 136.50 opens the pre-earnings-to-post-earnings air pocket toward the 200 SMA zone at 131.60. Stop above 144.00 (~1.5×ATR), which sits above the 10 EMA and below the VWMA.

Invalidation of both: a decisive reclaim of 146.45 (the 2024-01-19 high, pre-gap top) would mark the gap as a completed shakeout and shift the framework back to trend-following long with the 50>200 structure reasserting.

What I would explicitly not do: short the 141 area into support without a break (the 2024-02-20 failed breakdown at 139.49 shows dip-buyers are present); buy the 141 area expecting a fast gap fill (light volume, negative histogram, no RSI divergence); or treat the +7.2% premium to the 200 SMA as a "buy the dip" signal on its own — that premium was ~16% on 2024-01-29 and has been compressing, which is mean-reversion against the longs, not for them.
