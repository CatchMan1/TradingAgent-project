## 2. Trend Structure — Two Speeds, Two Verdicts

**Long-term (200 SMA): unambiguously bullish.** The 200 SMA has risen every session in the window, from 127.59 (2024-01-22) to 131.787 (2024-02-21) — roughly +0.095/session, a steady, low-slope climb. Price at 142.55 sits 8.17% above it. There is no long-term breakdown risk anywhere in sight; the 200 SMA would only come into play on a ~7.5% further decline.

**Intermediate (50 SMA): bullish but being tested.** The 50 SMA rose from 135.37 (2024-01-12) to 141.809 (2024-02-21). Critically, the *rate* of advance has flattened: +0.40/day in mid-January versus roughly +0.11/day over the last three sessions (141.697 → 141.809). A flattening 50 SMA rising *into* a falling price is precisely where a trend gets decided. The 50 SMA is also 7.6% above the 200 SMA — a firmly bullish "golden-cross" configuration — but note the gap is narrowing for the first time since the January rally, because the fast average is decelerating while price falls.

**Short-term (10 EMA): bearish.** The 10 EMA peaked at 148.81 on 2024-01-30 and has fallen monotonically to 143.653. Price has closed below it since 2024-01-31. The 10 EMA is now *above* both price and the 50 SMA — a short-term downtrend stacked on top of an intermediate uptrend. Until GOOGL reclaims 143.65 on a closing basis, the path of least resistance is sideways-to-lower.

**The key tension:** price is $0.74 above the 50 SMA and $1.10 below the 10 EMA. The whole near-term setup collapses into a 3-point decision zone (141.81 – 143.65). A close outside that zone with volume is the highest-quality signal available on this chart.

## 3. What Caused the Damage — the 2024-01-31 Earnings Gap

This chart cannot be read without the January 31 event:

- **2024-01-30:** close 151.46.

- **2024-02-02:** a violent reversal bar — opened 139.26, printed the swing low of **136.50**, and closed 142.38 on 62.50M shares. That is a capitulation-and-reclaim candle and establishes the floor reference.
- **2024-02-09:** rebound high of 149.44 (close 149.00) — a *lower high* versus 153.78, and a partial fill of the gap.
- **2024-02-12 → 2024-02-16:** five-session fade from 147.53 to 140.52, including -2.17% on 2024-02-15 (37.59M shares).
- **2024-02-20 → 2024-02-21:** 141.12 → 142.55, a modest two-day bounce on *declining* volume (25.14M → 23.22M).

**Overhead supply map:** the unfilled gap between the 2024-01-31 high of **144.00** and the 2024-01-30 low of **151.19** is the single largest block of trapped supply. Price spent only two sessions (2024-02-08/09) inside it before being rejected. Everything between 144 and 151 is structurally heavy. GOOGL is currently *below the entire gap*, meaning the average buyer since January 31 is underwater — which is exactly why the 143.4–144.0 area should reject on first touch.

## 4. Momentum — Bearish, but Decelerating

**MACD has completed a full deterioration:**
- Histogram flipped negative on 2024-01-31 (from +0.824 to -0.077) and printed its worst reading of -0.9006 on 2024-02-20.
- The MACD line fell from +3.894 (2024-01-30) to **-0.0066 (2024-02-21)** — a fresh **bearish zero-line cross**, the first negative MACD reading in the 40-session window. This is confirmation that the *short-term* trend is down, not merely a pause.
- **However**, the histogram has now contracted for two consecutive sessions (-0.9006 → -0.8287), and the MACD line's decline is flattening (+0.1286 → -0.0066, a much smaller drop than the prior sessions' ~-0.32/day). Momentum is still negative but the *second derivative* has turned. This is the earliest, weakest form of a bottoming hint — it needs a histogram flip above zero to become actionable, and that has not happened.

**RSI is neutral and mildly constructive.** RSI peaked at 78.25 (2024-01-29) — genuinely overbought into the print — reset hard to **42.60 on 2024-02-16**, and has now recovered to **47.21**. Two observations matter:
1. RSI reset to 42.6 **without breaking 30**. In a genuine downtrend, the overbought reading gets followed by sub-30 readings. Here the oscillator reset and held above 40, consistent with a corrective pause rather than a trend reversal.
2. RSI (47.21) and price are both making higher lows off the 2024-02-16 trough, so there is no clean bearish divergence to trade against. Equally, there is no bullish divergence, since price's 2024-02-20 low (139.49) was *lower* than the 2024-02-16 low (140.14). Momentum is genuinely neutral — no edge from this indicator alone.

**Practical implication:** with bands at 11.5% and ATR at ~2.2% of price, position size must be reduced relative to a low-volatility regime. Two-ATR is ~6.2 points — a stop placed inside that will be noise-stopped.

## 6. Volume — the Weakest Part of the Bull Case

Participating volume on the recovery is deteriorating:
- Down sessions carried the weight: 2024-02-15 (-2.17%) on **37.59M**, 2024-02-16 (-1.57%) on **31.47M**.
- The bounce carried almost nothing: 2024-02-20 (+0.43%) on 25.14M, 2024-02-21 (+1.01%) on **23.22M** — the second-lightest session since 2024-02-08.

This is the textbook distribution/absorption signature: sellers active on weakness, buyers unenthusiastic on strength. The **VWMA at 143.425 is above the close**, quantified confirmation that the volume-weighted average entry price of the last ~20 sessions is *higher* than today's price — in other words, the recent cohort is underwater and constitutes overhead supply. VWMA has already rolled over (146.94 on 2024-01-30 → 143.425), and price has not reclaimed it. **A daily close above 143.425–143.653 would be the first objective evidence that this is demand, not a dead-cat bounce.**

## 7. Actionable Framework

**Bias: HOLD / neutral, with a bullish intermediate tilt and a bearish short-term tilt. Do not chase this bounce.**

*Why not buy here:* price is below the 10 EMA, below VWMA, below the Bollinger midline, MACD just crossed zero, and the bounce is on shrinking volume. There is no confirmation.

*Why not short here:* the 50 SMA and 200 SMA are both rising, price is above both, RSI reset without breaking 30, and the MACD histogram is contracting. Shorting into a rising 50 SMA in an 8%-above-200-SMA uptrend is a low-probability trade.

**Bullish trigger (add / initiate long):** a daily close **above 143.65** (10 EMA) that also clears the **144.00** January 31 gap high, ideally on >32M shares.
- Target 1: **145.76** (Bollinger midline)
- Target 2: **149.44** (2024-02-09 rebound high)
- Target 3: **151.19** (2024-01-30 low — full gap fill)
- Invalidation: close back below 141.81 (50 SMA)

**Bearish trigger:** a daily close **below 141.81** (50 SMA), confirmed by a break of the **139.49–140.14** lows (2024-02-20 and 2024-02-16). Note that 2024-02-20 already probed 139.49 intraday and was bought, so a *close* below 139.49 is the real signal.
- Target 1: **137.374** (lower Bollinger band)
- Target 2: **136.50** (2024-02-02 capitulation low)
- Invalidation: reclaim of 143.65

**Risk parameters (ATR-anchored, ATR = 3.0995):**
- Tight technical stop below a structural level: ~1.3×ATR ≈ 4.0 points.
- Volatility stop: 2×ATR ≈ 6.2 points (appropriate for a swing position given the 2.17% daily range).
- For a 1% account-risk position at a 6.2-point stop, size ≈ (0.01 × equity) / 6.2 shares per unit of equity.

**What to watch next:** (1) whether the MACD histogram flips positive — that is the cleanest early momentum turn; (2) whether volume expands on the *next up day*; (3) whether the 50 SMA at 141.81 holds on a closing basis. All three bullish, and the setup becomes a low-risk continuation entry. Two of three bearish (especially a close under 141.81), and the 137.37 / 136.50 zone becomes the next objective.
