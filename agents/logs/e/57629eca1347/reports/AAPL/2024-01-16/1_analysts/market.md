# AAPL Technical Assessment — 2024-01-16

## Executive view

AAPL is in a **short-term downtrend within a still-intact longer-term uptrend**. The 2024-01-16 close of **183.63** is below its 10-day EMA, 50-day SMA, and volume-weighted average, but remains above its rising 200-day SMA. Momentum remains negative, though some measures are less stretched than earlier in January. This is a corrective structure, not yet a confirmed long-term breakdown.

**Tactical bias: bearish-to-neutral.** The burden of proof remains on the bulls until AAPL reclaims roughly **186** and then **189**. A close below the **180.2–180.8** area would materially weaken the longer-term trend case.

All selected indicator values below agree with the verified market snapshot for 2024-01-16.

## 2. Why these eight indicators were selected

I selected indicators across trend, momentum, volume, and volatility to avoid relying on overlapping signals:

- **10-day EMA:** Captures the current short-term trend and immediate resistance.
- **50-day SMA:** Defines the medium-term trend.
- **200-day SMA:** Defines the strategic trend and major support reference.
- **MACD:** Measures the broader momentum shift and zero-line crossover.
- **MACD histogram:** Shows whether bearish momentum is accelerating or converging toward the signal line.
- **RSI:** Assesses whether momentum is overbought, oversold, or merely weak.
- **ATR:** Quantifies current volatility for risk sizing and stop placement.
- **VWMA:** Confirms whether price action is being supported or rejected by volume-weighted participation.

I excluded additional oscillators such as stochastic RSI because they would largely duplicate RSI. I also avoided adding multiple Bollinger-band and moving-average variants, which would consume indicator slots without adding distinct information.

## 3. Trend structure: long-term support, medium-term deterioration

### Short-term trend — bearish

The **10-day EMA is 185.97**, and AAPL’s close of 183.63 is below it. The 10-day EMA has fallen from **195.51 on 2023-12-19** to **185.97 on 2024-01-16**, reflecting a clear loss of short-term momentum.

The 10-day EMA also crossed below the 50-day SMA around **2024-01-08** and remains below it. This is a short-term bearish crossover, although it is not by itself a long-term sell signal.

### Medium-term trend — still rising, but price is below it

The **50-day SMA is 189.21**, leaving AAPL about **3.04% below** that average. The 50-day SMA is still rising, from 186.63 on 2023-12-29 to 189.21 on 2024-01-16, but price has been below it since the early-January decline.

That combination—a rising 50-day SMA with price below it—typically means the medium-term trend has not yet rolled over, but it is under pressure. If price remains below the average, the 50-day SMA itself may begin to flatten.

### Long-term trend — constructive but being tested

The **200-day SMA is 180.81**, and AAPL remains **about 1.56% above** it. The 200-day SMA is also rising, from 179.49 on 2023-12-29 to 180.81 on 2024-01-16.

The 50-day SMA remains above the 200-day SMA by approximately **8.41 points, or 4.65%**, preserving the broader bullish moving-average structure. However, the January 16 low of **180.93** came within **0.12 points** of the 200-day SMA. That is a meaningful proximity test, not proof of support. A closing break below the 200-day SMA would shift the strategic picture materially.

**Trend conclusion:** long-term bullish, medium-term corrective, short-term bearish.

- **MACD histogram:** -0.727  

The MACD line is below both zero and its signal line. AAPL’s MACD crossed below zero on **2024-01-04**, falling from +0.055 on January 3 to -0.702 on January 4, and has remained negative since.

The histogram has become less negative than its January 5 reading of -2.045, but this should not be interpreted as a bullish reversal. The narrower histogram primarily reflects the signal line catching down toward the MACD line, not a confirmed upward turn in momentum.

A genuine bullish momentum repair would require the MACD line to turn upward and eventually cross back above the signal line.

The **RSI is 38.46**, below the neutral 50 level but above the conventional 30 oversold threshold.

RSI fell to **28.79 on 2024-01-05**, rebounded to **43.06 on 2024-01-12**, and has since slipped back to 38.46. Because the January 16 close of 183.63 is higher than the January 5 close of 181.18, while RSI is also higher, this is a modest stabilization rather than a classic bullish divergence.

The practical implication is that momentum is weak enough to warrant caution, but not so oversold that a mechanical mean-reversion buy is supported by the indicator alone.

## 7. Actionable levels and scenarios

### Bearish continuation

A daily close below the **180.17–180.81** zone would be significant. That zone contains the January 5 low at 180.17 and the rising 200-day SMA at 180.81.

- A simple intraday dip below 180.81 is less meaningful than a close below it.
- A close below 180.17 would provide stronger evidence that the long-term trend structure is deteriorating.
- In that scenario, the next reference area would need to be reassessed from subsequent price action rather than assumed in advance.

### Neutral/base case

AAPL remains between strategic support near **180.8** and overhead resistance near **189.2**. In that range, the evidence favors patience rather than aggressive directional exposure: momentum is negative, but the long-term trend has not broken.

### Bullish repair

The first constructive signal would be a daily close back above the **185.97–185.98** area, where the 10-day EMA and VWMA converge.

A stronger repair would require:

1. A close above **186**,
2. A move through the January 2 gap area and the **188.44** January 2 high,
3. A close above the **189.21** 50-day SMA,
4. MACD turning upward, and
5. RSI recovering above 50.

Until those conditions occur, rallies toward 186–189 should be treated as potential resistance rather than automatic breakout opportunities.

---

## 8. Bottom line for traders

AAPL’s strategic uptrend remains technically alive because price is still above the rising 200-day SMA. But the tactical evidence is bearish: the stock is below the 10-day EMA, 50-day SMA, and VWMA; MACD is below zero and below its signal line; RSI is weak; and recent heavy volume has been concentrated on down days.

The most prudent interpretation is **defensive patience**:

- **Existing long exposure:** monitor the 180.2–180.8 area closely; a closing break would warrant a more defensive stance.
- **New long entries:** wait for a reclaim of 186 and preferably 189, with improving MACD and RSI, rather than anticipating a reversal solely from the January 16 intraday recovery.
- **Short exposure:** momentum supports the bearish case, but the strong close within the January 16 range and proximity to the 200-day SMA increase the risk of a countertrend bounce.
