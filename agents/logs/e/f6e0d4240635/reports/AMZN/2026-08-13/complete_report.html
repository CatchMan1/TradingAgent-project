Bull Analyst: # BULL CASE: AMZN — The Market Is Arguing With the Wrong Number

Alright, let me open this properly, because I think the bear case on AMZN writes itself in about thirty seconds — and then falls apart in about five minutes once you actually read the filing instead of the headline.

Here's my thesis in one line: AMZN just printed its fastest revenue growth in the visible dataset, accelerated sequentially, with 43% operating-income growth and genuine cost leverage — and the market is busy arguing about a non-cash accounting mark that has nothing to do with any of it.

Let me build that, then I'll take the bear's best shots head-on.

1. First, let's kill the "unexplained gap" mystery — because it isn't one

The technical report is honest that it can't explain the 7/31 regime shift: "+15.32% close-to-close, 129M shares, no fundamental data in the toolset." Fair. But the fundamental report hands us the answer on a plate:

  • Revenue +19.6% YoY — up from +16.6% in Q1. Acceleration on a ~$800B annualized base.

That is not a meme gap. That is the market discovering that the largest-scale business in the world is re-accelerating while expanding margins. And crucially — the gap has not been filled. The 7/31 low of 262.01 is still the floor. The bear is going to have to explain why a filing-driven repricing should be given back.

2. The growth story is the hardest thing in this dataset to fake
