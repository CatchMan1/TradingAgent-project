Four-tenths of a turn of operating cash flow. AMZN could repay its entire net debt position with about five months of operating cash flow. The bear is describing a leverage problem that arithmetic doesn't support.

Negative FCF during an investment ramp is what AMZN has done in every major buildout cycle. The bear's argument requires that this time the returns don't show up.
