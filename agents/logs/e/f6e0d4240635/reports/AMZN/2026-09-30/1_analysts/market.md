**Base case — coil resolves upward (bullish, but trigger-dependent).**
Needs a **daily close above 252.44** on volume ≥ ~40M, then confirmation above **256.12** (50-SMA). Targets: **259.49–260.81**, then **267.56** (2026-08-28 high). Note the coil apex (converging wedge plus descending 20-SMA) lands in roughly the **first half of October**, so this is a near-term decision point, not a Q4 thesis.

**Bear case — support gives way.**
A close below **244.30** opens **243.88** (lower band), then the **239.82–241.60** confluence. If that zone fails on expanding volume/ATR, the post-2026-07-31 structure is invalidated and 235.50 becomes the reference. **Higher-probability neutral: range trade 244–252.** With ATR at 5.65, a 244–252 band is only ~1.4 ATR wide — too tight for a high-quality swing trade in the middle. Fade only at the edges, or wait for the break.

**Risk parameters (ATR-based, ATR = 5.654):**
- 1×ATR = 5.65 pts (2.27%); 1.5×ATR = 8.48; 2×ATR = 11.31.
- Long from the 246–247 lows with a structural stop below **240.50** (under the 200-SMA and gap floor) risks ~5.5–6.5 pts for a 258–260 target — roughly **2.2:1 to 2.5:1** reward-to-risk.

**Event risk:** this series contains two single-session gaps of **179.2M shares (2026-02-06)** and **129.1M shares (2026-07-31)** that dwarf normal volume. A catalyst of that magnitude can resolve the coil in one session in either direction and will invalidate any stop placed inside the 239.8–260.8 band. Reduce size ahead of any known catalyst rather than after.

## 7. Bottom line

AMZN is **not broken, but it is not working**. The primary uptrend is intact (price +3.3% above a rising 200-SMA; 50>200), yet the intermediate trend has clearly stalled — 12.3% off the 2026-08-03 close, below a now-rolling-over 50-SMA, below VWMA, with MACD negative since 2026-09-09. Simultaneously, volatility has collapsed (bandwidth 6.71% from 29.58%, ATR 2.27% from 3.50%), RSI has bottomed at 39.45 without breaking 30, and the histogram is making higher lows while price made a lower low. That combination — decelerating downside momentum plus an extreme squeeze — favors **patience over prediction**: let the 252.44 / 244.30 boundaries declare themselves. The asymmetry is modestly better to the upside given the primary trend and the 239.8–241.6 confluence beneath, but the honest read is a **neutral coil with a bullish tilt only above 252.44 and a genuine failure below 244.30**.
