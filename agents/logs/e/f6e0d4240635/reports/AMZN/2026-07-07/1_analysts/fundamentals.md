## 1. Company Profile
Amazon operates three reportable operating segments (confirmed: `NumberOfOperatingSegments` = 3):
- **North America** — online & physical retail, third‑party marketplace, logistics.
- **International** — retail/marketplace outside NA.
- **AWS (Amazon Web Services)** — cloud compute, storage, database, and AI/ML services — the primary profit engine and now the primary capex destination.

Adjacent high‑margin businesses: **advertising** (ad serving), subscription services (Prime), and a large **investment portfolio** of non‑marketable equity stakes (the revaluation of these drove a one‑time gain this quarter — see §4). Amazon does **not** pay a dividend and does **not** run a meaningful buyback; growth is reinvested.

## 4. The Anthropic / Equity‑Stake Gain — Critical for Interpretation
The surge in "other non‑operating income" ($15,647M) and "other noncurrent assets" is driven by **revaluation of non‑marketable equity securities**:
- `EquitySecuritiesWithoutReadilyDeterminableFairValue` jumped **$16.2B → $48.1B** (+$31.9B).
- `EquitySecuritiesWithoutReadilyDeterminableFairValueUpwardPriceAdjustment` = **$12,328M** booked in Q1 FY2026 income (vs. just $37M a year earlier).

**This is a large unrealized, mark‑to‑market gain on a private AI investment stake** (consistent with Amazon's Anthropic position). It is **non‑cash and non‑recurring**. Traders should:
- Strip it out when judging earnings power (adj. EPS ~$1.92, not $2.78).
- Recognize it inflated book value and net income — not a sustainable earnings driver, but it *does* signal substantial latent value sitting in Amazon's investment portfolio.

## 9. Bottom Line for Traders
- **Operationally strong:** Revenue accelerated to +16.6% and operating income +30% with margin expansion — the core retail + AWS + ads machine is performing well.
- **Earnings quality caveat:** Headline net income/EPS are inflated by a ~$12.3B pre‑tax unrealized Anthropic‑stake gain; adjusted EPS ~$1.92 is the cleaner read.
- **Capital‑intensity risk is the dominant theme:** Amazon is spending >$150B/yr on capex, funding it with a $53B debt raise, pushing TTM free cash flow **negative**. This is a bold AI bet — bullish if returns materialize, but it compresses FCF and raises leverage/execution risk near term.
- **Balance sheet remains a fortress** (~$144B liquid assets) and can absorb the build‑out, but debt is climbing fast and interest expense is rising.
- **Positioning:** The setup favors investors tolerant of a capex/FCF trough betting on AI‑driven growth, with awareness that headline profits are one‑time‑boosted this quarter and FCF will remain under pressure.
