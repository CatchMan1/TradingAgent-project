## 1. Executive Summary

AMZN's Q2 FY2026 results show **powerful operating momentum**: revenue accelerated to +19.6% YoY, operating margin expanded ~226 bps to 13.7%, and operating income rose 43%. However, **headline EPS is heavily distorted by a non-cash, mark-to-model gain** on non-marketable equity securities. Excluding that item, core EPS growth is a still-strong but far more modest ~+29%.

At the same time, AMZN is in the middle of an **unprecedented capital-expenditure cycle**: H1 2026 capex of $98.4B (+72% YoY), TTM capex of $173.0B, and **TTM free cash flow has turned negative (−$11.6B)** for the first time in the retrieved history. This is being funded with debt — total debt plus finance leases rose ~81% in six months to ~$146.4B — and long-dated contractual obligations stand at **$650.0B**.

**Bottom line for traders:** the operating business is inflecting upward faster than revenue, but the *quality* of reported earnings and the *cash-flow/leverage* trajectory are deteriorating. Headline EPS beats should be discounted; forward attention will center on capex guidance, AWS/AI monetization, and FCF.

## 9. Risks & Things to Watch

**Bearish / risk factors**
1. **Earnings quality risk.** ~$48.4B after-tax of H1 2026 net income came from non-cash revaluation of a $122.3B illiquid, mark-to-model equity portfolio. A 20% markdown would be ~$24.5B pre-tax (~$2.25/share) and flow straight through EPS. Expect high quarterly EPS volatility unrelated to operations.
2. **Negative FCF and rising leverage.** TTM FCF −$11.6B; debt +80.5% in six months. Continued capex at ~$200B+/yr would require more debt or a slowdown in buybacks (none evident) — both re-rating risks.
3. **Depreciation cliff.** D&A +31–32% YoY and capex/D&A at ~2.2x (TTM capex $173B vs D&A $75B). Future D&A growth will mechanically compress margins unless revenue/mix offsets it.
4. **Fixed-cost commitments.** $650B contractual obligations, $427.5B beyond year 4. If AI demand normalizes, operating leverage reverses sharply.
5. **Interest expense +155% YoY** and rising; net interest income has flipped to roughly neutral.
6. **Tax normalization.** Cash taxes were only $6.6B TTM on $135B net income; deferred tax expense of $30.5B in H1 2026 signals future cash tax risk.
7. **Working capital / receivables.** AR +30.1% since year-end vs H1 revenue +18.2% — monitor DSO (~40 days) for collections or mix issues.

**Bullish / supportive factors**
1. **Revenue acceleration** (+16.6% → +19.6%) with gross margin expansion (+84 bps H1).
2. **Operating leverage is genuine**: opex ratio down 181 bps in Q2; marketing and G&A both deleveraged as % of sales.
3. **SBC discipline**: SBC down 7.6% YoY in Q2 despite 19.6% revenue growth (3.0% of revenue vs 3.9%).
4. **Fortress liquidity**: $176.1B cash + securities; net cash positive ex-leases; interest coverage >20x.
5. **Hidden asset value**: $122.3B non-marketable equity portfolio (~$11.2/share pre-tax) is not reflected in operating earnings and could support sum-of-the-parts valuation.
6. **Supplier float** (~140 AP days) funds a meaningful portion of working capital.
7. **Inventory flat YoY while revenue +18%** — best-in-class turns, low obsolescence risk.

## 10. Actionable Insights for Traders

1. **Do not trade the headline EPS.** Q2 $5.75 and H1 $8.53 are inflated by ~$3.59 and ~$4.44 respectively of non-cash equity marks. Use **core EPS of ~$2.16 (Q2)** and **~$4.09 (H1)**, or **~$7.98 core TTM EPS** for valuation. Headline TTM ($12.42) overstates core by ~56%.
2. **The operating story is a genuine upside surprise.** Revenue accelerated and operating margin expanded >200 bps with cost discipline. If Q3 FY2026 confirms ~19–20% revenue growth and mid-13% operating margin, the core earnings trajectory supports a higher multiple on *core* EPS.
3. **The FCF debate is the swing factor.** TTM FCF is negative and capex is running at ~26–27% of revenue. Any commentary suggesting capex peaks in FY2026 would be a strong positive catalyst; any upward revision to capex or obligations is a negative catalyst. Position sizing should reflect this binary.
4. **Watch the equity marks as a volatility source, not a value driver.** A reversal of even part of the $62.8B H1 revaluation could produce a large headline EPS miss despite unchanged operations. Consider that headline consensus may be anchored on inflated non-operating gains.
5. **Leverage trajectory warrants monitoring.** Debt +$65B in six months with $78.1B of obligations due in the next twelve months. Liquidity covers it, but the direction of travel matters for credit spreads and buyback capacity.
6. **Key catalysts to track:** Q3 FY2026 print (late Oct 2026), capex guidance, AWS/AI revenue disclosure, any update to the $650B obligation schedule, debt issuance/refinancing, and mark-to-market updates on the $122.3B equity portfolio.
7. **Valuation cannot be computed from the toolset provided** (no price/market-cap data). Apply the core TTM EPS of ~$7.98 and forward core estimates to your own price data; using headline EPS will systematically understate the P/E by roughly one-third.
