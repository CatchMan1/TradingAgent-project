**Recommendation: Underweight**

**Rationale**

The bear case wins on evidence quality and risk/reward, not on a claim that AMZN’s operating business is broken. The bull is right that Q2 was operationally strong: operating income +43.2%, operating margin 13.7%, operating cash flow +39.6%, and best-in-class inventory turns. But the bull’s leap from “strong quarter” to “buy now” is not supported by the disclosures in the debate.

The bear’s decisive points are:

- **Revenue acceleration is not proven organic.** H1 2026 acquisitions were $39.8B versus $1.7B a year earlier, including $24.4B in Q2 alone. The prompt contains no acquired-revenue contribution or organic growth disclosure. The bull concedes this “should be reconciled,” which is not enough to underwrite an Overweight.
- **Earnings quality is weaker than the bull’s core-EPS framing.** The $50.5B non-cash mark is correctly conceded, but the bear goes further: the 3.3% cash tax rate and deferred-tax dynamic are tied to the same accounting event. The ~$2.16 core EPS is therefore not clean. The $122.3B mark-to-model asset is also a two-way P&L risk, not a free option.
- **The FCF/debt trajectory is the real change.** TTM FCF is –$11.6B, Q2 capex was $54.2B (+68.4%), long-term debt rose from $68.8B to $133.0B, and net cash has moved to roughly $67B net debt including leases. The bull’s 0.8x gross debt/EBITDA and 20.9x interest coverage describe the level, not the direction; interest expense is +154.7% y/y.
- **AWS remains unverified.** There is no segment revenue or operating income data in the provided materials. The bull’s inference that “AWS did not collapse” from consolidated margin is not sufficient for AMZN’s most important swing factor.
- **The technical report supports the bear’s timing, not the bull’s.** The report’s own long entry at current levels is described as poor at 0.53:1 R:R, while selling strength into 242–247 is described as structurally superior at 3–4:1. AMZN is below the 50SMA, VWMA, Bollinger mid, and July pivot; the base case caps near 242.

This is not a Sell call because AMZN has $176.1B of liquidity, strong operating cash generation, and no near-term solvency issue. But the current price offers poor reward/risk, and the bull’s core claims—organic acceleration, clean core EPS, disciplined capex, and confirmed AWS strength—remain unproven. That supports trimming exposure rather than adding.

- **Standard allocation:** Reduce AMZN to **50–75% of normal target weight**. Trim **25–50% of any existing position** now and use any rally into **242.41–247.00** to complete the reduction.

- **Tactical/hedged accounts:** If mandate permits, sell strength into **242.41–247.00** with a stop above **248.50**, targeting **234.75 → 226.80 → 226.13**. Size any tactical short at no more than **0.5–1.0% account risk**; this is a hedge/trim overlay, not a core short.
- **Rebuild/upgrade criteria:** Move back toward Hold/Overweight only on either:
  - two consecutive daily closes above **242.41** on >60M shares with MACD histogram turning positive; or
  - Q3 evidence of **organic revenue growth ≥18%**, operating margin ≥13.5%, capex ≤$52B, TTM FCF moving toward breakeven, long-term debt flat/declining, and AWS segment reacceleration.

The debate does not provide organic revenue or AWS segment data; those gaps are central to any upgrade.
