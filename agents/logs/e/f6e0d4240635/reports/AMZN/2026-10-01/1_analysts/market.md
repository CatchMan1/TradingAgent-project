## 2. Trend structure: long-term bullish, intermediate corrective

### Long-term trend remains intact

The **200-day SMA is $241.22**, and it has risen from **$233.03 on July 6, 2026** to **$241.22 on October 1**. A rising 200-day average is a constructive long-term backdrop. AMZN’s close is **2.91% above** that average.

In addition, the **50-day SMA at $256.19 remains above the 200-day SMA by about $14.97**, or roughly **6.2%**. There is no death-cross setup in the current data. The primary long-term trend structure is therefore still positive.

### Intermediate trend has weakened

The **50-day SMA** has stopped rising meaningfully. It was **$256.29 on September 23** and **$256.19 on October 1**—essentially flat over that stretch, after climbing from **$247.01 on August 3**. More importantly, AMZN closed below the 50-day SMA on **September 22** at $254.98 and has remained below it since.

That is a change in character from the strong July–August advance. The September 21 close of **$258.45** was above the then-50-day SMA of **$256.10**, but the follow-through failed immediately. That failed hold is evidence that the intermediate trend is corrective rather than impulsive.

### Short-term trend is still down

The **10-day EMA is $249.66**, and price is **0.57% below it**. The 10-day EMA has fallen from **$267.16 on August 11** to **$249.66 on October 1**, though the pace of decline has slowed: it was $250.17 on September 29, $249.98 on September 30, and $249.66 on October 1.

That is stabilization, not a reversal. The 10-day EMA is still sloping down, and price has not reclaimed it.

### Price pattern: compression between lower highs and higher lows

Since the August peak, AMZN has produced a series of lower swing highs:

- **$287.20** intraday on August 3
- **$280.14** on August 10
- **$267.56** on August 28

At the same time, the recent lows have edged higher:

That combination—lower highs and slightly higher lows—is a short-term contracting range. ATR has also compressed. Such conditions often precede a directional resolution, but the direction is not predetermined. Given that price is below the 50-day SMA and MACD is negative, the downside resolution deserves respect until proven otherwise.

In a strong uptrend, RSI near 40–45 can mark a reset; in a correction, it can also precede further weakness. The indicator therefore supports a **wait-and-see** stance rather than a mean-reversion buy.

## 4. Volatility: compressed, so breakout risk is rising

ATR is **$5.66**, equal to about **2.28% of the close**. That is down substantially from **$9.98 on August 5** and **$6.69 on September 4**. The latest session’s range was $5.71, almost exactly one ATR, so the day was volatile in absolute terms but normal relative to the recent regime.

The key implication is that volatility has compressed after the huge late-July/early-August expansion. A move through either end of the current range could produce a larger-than-usual follow-through move, but false breaks are also common when ATR is falling.

For risk management:

- **1 ATR** ≈ $5.66
- **1.5 ATR** ≈ $8.49
- **2 ATR** ≈ $11.32

A structural stop below $244.30 from the current close would risk about **$3.93**, or roughly **0.7 ATR**. That is tighter than a pure 1.5-ATR stop and therefore more prone to noise. Position sizing should account for that trade-off.

## 6. Actionable plan

### For existing long-term holders

The long-term trend is still intact while AMZN holds above the **200-day SMA at $241.22**. A cautious approach is to maintain exposure but use **$244.30** as an early warning level and **$241.22** as the strategic invalidation line.

A daily close below $244.30 would break the recent higher-low structure. A close below $241.22 would materially weaken the long-term technical case and argue for reducing trend exposure until the stock rebuilds above its intermediate averages.

### For new long positions

Avoid buying in the middle of the range at $248.23. Two more favorable setups exist:

1. **Confirmation breakout:** Wait for a daily close above **$250.77** and preferably above the October 1 high of **$251.83**, with volume expanding above the recent ~35 million-share average and the MACD gap turning positive. The next resistance is the 50-day SMA at **$256.19**.
2. **Support stabilization:** Watch the **$244.30–$245.60** area for a reversal pattern with improving volume. A stop below $244.30 or below $241.22 would define risk, depending on the trader’s time horizon.

The first setup carries less uncertainty but a less attractive entry price. The second offers better risk/reward but requires evidence of buying interest that is not yet present.

### For short-term tactical traders

While AMZN remains below **$250.77**, rallies toward $250–$252 are vulnerable to rejection. A failed retest of that zone, followed by a close back below the 10-day EMA, would keep the short-term bias bearish, with **$246.12** and then **$244.30** as reference levels.

A confirmed daily close below $244.30 would shift focus to the **200-day SMA at $241.22**. However, shorting directly into $244–$245 support without a confirmed breakdown is a lower-quality setup because the long-term trend remains upward.
