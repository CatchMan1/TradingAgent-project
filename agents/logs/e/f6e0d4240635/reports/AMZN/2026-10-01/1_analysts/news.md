**Horizon:** Positive sentiment immediately; capacity benefits mostly 2030+.

**Uncertainty:** PPA economics undisclosed; 190 MW is new capacity, and its materiality to AMZN’s total power needs is not stated; licensing, construction and regulatory execution risk.

1. **Strong AI demand versus financing concerns.** Anthropic’s revenue growth and Micron’s AI-driven beat support demand. But Anthropic’s ability to fund a >$100 billion AMZN commitment, Oracle’s cash-flow worry and the “AI bubble” commentary all point to rising skepticism about AI spending returns.
2. **Company-specific positives versus macro negatives.** The Constellation PPA, Synopsys deal and Anthropic commitment are constructive for AMZN. Consumer-confidence weakness and retail price competition are negatives, though their AMZN-specific magnitude is unquantified.
3. **Headline versus substance.** “Amazon backs nuclear plant expansion” could sound like an equity investment; the evidence describes a **20-year PPA** supporting infrastructure investment. Similarly, the “>$100 billion promised to Amazon” is a reported commitment, not recognized revenue.
4. **Timing.** The PPA and Anthropic items were published after the Sep 30 close; the Synopsys item was before the close. Next-session reaction may be uneven.
5. **Evidence limits.** No full articles, no deal pricing, no AMZN management commentary and no price/valuation data. I cannot verify whether the positive news was already priced.
