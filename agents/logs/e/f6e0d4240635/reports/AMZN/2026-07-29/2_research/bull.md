- **Operating cash flow: $148.5B TTM, +30% versus FY2024's $115.9B.** The cash-generation engine is accelerating.

The bear is looking at the subtraction sign and treating it as a verdict on the business. I'm looking at *what the money bought*: **$569.3B in contractual obligations**, with **$57.1B due within the next twelve months.**

The bear called this "fixed-cost rigidity." I call it a **contracted revenue backlog**.
