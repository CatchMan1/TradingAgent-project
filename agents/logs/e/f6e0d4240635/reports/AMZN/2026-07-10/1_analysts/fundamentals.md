## 1. Company Profile & Identity

AMZN (Amazon.com, Inc.) is a global technology and e-commerce conglomerate organized around **three operating segments** (confirmed by the 10-Q: `NumberOfOperatingSegments = 3`):

- **North America** — online and physical retail, third-party marketplace, advertising, Prime subscriptions
- **International** — the same retail/advertising business outside North America
- **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** — cloud computing, storage, database, AI/ML infrastructure and services

AMZN's fundamental model is a high-volume, low-margin retail/logistics engine that funds high-margin, high-growth profit pools: **AWS (cloud + AI) and advertising**. Advertising expense (a cost line, distinct from advertising *revenue*) rose to **$23.5B in FY2025** from $21.4B (FY2024) and $20.3B (FY2023), underscoring continued brand/marketing investment. The company is currently in a **massive AI/data-center capital-expenditure supercycle**, which is the single most important driver of the current financial picture.

## 3. Income-Statement Analysis & Earnings Quality

- **Revenue growth is re-accelerating**: +16.6% YoY, a strong pace for a company of this size, led by AWS/AI and advertising.
- **Margin expansion is genuine and operating-driven**: gross margin +120 bps and operating margin +130 bps. Gross profit grew 19.5% vs revenue 16.6%, showing operating leverage.
- **D&A is surging**: quarterly D&A rose to **$18.95B in Q1 FY2026** vs $14.26B a year earlier, and TTM D&A jumped to **$70.4B** (from $55.4B in the prior TTM). This reflects the exploding asset base from capex — a headwind to near-term margins but a marker of capacity build-out.
- **⚠️ Earnings-quality red flag — non-operating gains dominate the beat:** Other non-operating income exploded to **$15.65B** vs $2.75B. The driver is a **$12.33B upward price adjustment on equity securities without readily determinable fair value** (Q1 FY2026). The carrying value of these private equity holdings roughly **tripled from $16.2B (Dec-2025) to $48.1B (Mar-2026)** — almost certainly a mark-up on a strategic AI/private-tech stake. This is real economic value but is **non-cash, mark-to-market, and highly volatile**.
  - **Core (operating-only) net income** is closer to **~$18–19B** once the ~$12B mark-up is stripped out. So roughly **40% of the Q1 headline EPS of $2.78 is non-core.** Traders should not extrapolate this quarter's EPS run-rate.
- **Interest expense nearly doubling** (+48% YoY) reflects the new debt issuance (see §4).

**Interpretation:**

This is the defining trade-off facing AMZN investors today: near-term FCF compression in exchange for AI/cloud capacity that should drive future growth.
- **Financing flipped positive** in FY2025 (+$9.7B) after years of cash return/debt paydown, and Q1 FY2026 saw a **record $53.4B long-term debt raise**. AMZN is now funding growth with debt.
- Capex incurred but not yet paid jumped from $16.8B (2024) to **$27.0B (2025)**, signaling the capex pipeline remains heavy into future quarters.

## 9. Actionable Insights for Traders

1. **Focus on operating metrics, discount headline EPS.** Q1 FY2026 EPS of $2.78 is inflated by ~$12B of non-cash private-equity revaluation. The "core" earnings power is closer to **~$18–19B net income** on **13.1% operating margin** — that margin expansion (from 11.8%) is the durable, bullish signal.
2. **The capex/FCF story is the swing factor.** TTM free cash flow turned **negative (−$2.5B)** for the first time in this dataset as capex ($151B TTM) overtook operating cash flow ($148.5B). Bulls will treat this as a down payment on an AI/cloud payoff; bears will flag FCF compression and rising leverage. Watch the capex trajectory and any guidance on AI capacity monetization.
3. **Leverage is rising — monitor it.** Total debt roughly doubled to ~$122B in one quarter and interest expense is surging. Current liquidity (~$144B) and a 1.18 current ratio comfortably cover near-term needs, but a sustained debt-funded capex ramp is a watch item for credit and equity risk.
4. **Revenue re-acceleration (+16.6%) + margin expansion is a powerful combination** if it persists — this is the strongest operating data point in the report and supports the growth narrative.
5. **Treat the private-equity revaluation as volatile value, not recurring earnings.** Mark-to-market swings (both up and down) will distort reported net income quarter to quarter; separate operating income from investment gains.
6. **No insider data available** — do not rely on ownership signals from this analysis.
