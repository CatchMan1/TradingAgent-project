1. Executive Summary

Amazon's Q2 2026 filing shows a company in the middle of an extraordinary capital-deployment cycle funded increasingly with debt, while core operating momentum is simultaneously accelerating. Three things dominate the fundamental picture:

  1. Core operations are inflecting higher. Q2 2026 revenue reached $200.6B (+19.6% YoY) and operating income $27.5B (+43.2% YoY), lifting operating margin to 13.7% (+226bp) — despite depreciation rising 31% YoY. Incremental operating margin was ~25%.

The gap is being closed with debt: long-term debt rose +93% in six months, from $68.8B to $133.0B, plus $13.5B of finance leases.

Bottom line for traders: the operating business is performing better than at any point in its history on margin, and that is the durable signal. But the headline EPS, the balance-sheet expansion, and the free-cash-flow profile are all being driven by items that are either non-cash (equity marks) or forward-dated (AI/data-center capex).
