## 2. The most relevant indicators for this specific setup (8 selected)

I chose indicators that answer four different questions without duplicating each other: *trend direction* (moving averages), *momentum trajectory* (MACD + RSI), *volatility envelope / extension* (Bollinger bands), and *whether volume validates price* (VWMA).

**4. macd (8.712 vs signal 7.113)** — Momentum confirmation. The MACD line crossed above its signal around 2026-07-01/02 (histogram flipped from -1.251 on 07-01 to +0.261 on 07-02) and crossed above zero between 07-02 (-0.686) and 07-06 (+0.879). **5. rsi (62.54)** — Overbought/oversold and, more importantly, *momentum divergence control*. **8. vwma ($319.48)** — The volume cross-check, and the most valuable signal in this report. VWMA jumped from 300.72 (07-16) to 310.27 (07-17) — a **3.2% one-session move in a volume-weighted average** — which can only happen if enormous volume traded at much higher prices. It then kept stepping up (314.02 on 07-20, 317.27 on 07-21, 319.48 on 07-22). Price sits **2.0% above VWMA**: buyers are in control, and the average *cost basis of recent volume* is chasing price upward rather than price having run away from the volume base.

**Why this set is non-redundant:** three MAs at distinct time constants (10/50/200) give trend *layering*; MACD gives momentum *direction and cross timing*; RSI gives *overbought state* (a different question than direction); the Bollinger pair gives *extension in standard-deviation terms*; VWMA gives *participation quality*. I deliberately avoided stochastics (redundant with RSI), macds/macdh as standalone selections (the histogram decline is read off the macd/signal spread), and the Bollinger midline (the 50-SMA/10-EMA already cover the "mean" role).

## 3. Nuance: what the data actually says about the last five sessions

This is where a naive read ("uptrend, buy") would be wrong in its details. The tape has **changed character** since the 2026-07-17 high:

| 2026-07-16 | 333.26 | 62.97M | 71.43 | 3.229 | New record close, heavy volume |
| 2026-07-17 | 333.74 | 63.41M | 71.65 | 3.337 | Peak close; intraday high 334.99; hist peak |
| 2026-07-20 | 326.59 | 53.47M | 63.87 | 2.724 | -2.14% reversal day, low 323.68 |

| 2026-07-22 | 325.89 | 38.76M | 62.54 | 1.599 | Range 5.66 (< ATR 8.02), lowest volume of last 5 |

Three things stand out:

1. **Momentum is decelerating, not reversing.** MACD histogram has fallen from 3.337 (07-17) to 1.599 (07-22), a **-52% decay** in four sessions, and the MACD line itself rolled from 8.927 (07-21) to 8.712. RSI dropped 9.1 points from its 71.65 peak. Yet price is only **-2.35%** off the record close. That is distribution *of momentum*, not *of shares* — but it does raise the probability that the next 1–3 weeks are sideways-to-lower rather than an immediate breakout continuation.

2. **The pullback is happening on shrinking volume — a constructive tell.** Volume fell from 63.41M (07-17) to 38.76M (07-22), three consecutive declines. Supply is not aggressive. A distribution top usually shows *rising* volume on down days; here 07-20's -2.14% came on 53.47M, below the 63.41M of the prior up day.

3. **The 10-EMA is holding as the short-term floor.** The 07-21 low of 322.22 came within $0.01 of that day's 10-EMA (322.21), and price closed above it; 07-22's low of 323.34 also held above the current 10-EMA of 322.88. Three consecutive closes (327.74, 325.89) above a rising 10-EMA is the definition of an intact short-term trend. A decisive close below 322.88 would be the first objective evidence of trend degradation and would shift the tactical bias to neutral.

RSI at 62.54 leaves room before the 70 zone is re-entered.

**Tactical plan (swing horizon):**
- **Constructive trigger:** a close above **$329.00** (today's high) followed by a close above **$333.74** re-establishes the breakout. Confirmation would be volume back above the 07-17 figure of ~63M and MACD hist turning up from 1.599.
- **Preferred entry zone for trend followers:** hold/add while price stays above the **$322.22–$322.88** confluence. Scaling in on a tag of the 10-EMA rather than chasing 07-17 highs is the higher-expectancy approach given the -52% histogram decay.
- **Second-chance entry:** **$314.86–$317.31** shelf — a controlled pullback to the pre-breakout base with VWMA still rising would be healthy, not bearish.
- **Invalidation (short-term):** a daily close below **$322.88** with expanding volume → reduce tactical longs; bias neutral between there and the Bolly midline.
- **Invalidation (medium-term):** a daily close below **$304.89** (50-SMA) → the 10>50>200 stack is broken; stand aside.
- **Stops:** roughly **1.0–1.5× ATR = $8–12** below entry. For a long initiated near $326, that implies a stop in the **$314–$318** region — conveniently aligned with the structural shelf, i.e., the stop is placed where the chart says "I'm wrong," not at an arbitrary distance.
- **Position sizing:** with ATR at 2.46% of price and band width at 23% of the midline, this is a **high-volatility regime**; size positions ~25–35% smaller than you would in the June range-bound tape (ATR $6.79–$7.00 on 2026-06-18/06-24) to keep dollar risk constant.

**What would flip me cautious:** (1) a break of $322.88 on rising volume; (2) MACD line crossing back below its signal (needs a further ~1.6-point decline in the spread); (3) price failing at $333.74 a second time on *declining* volume; (4) VWMA flattening or rolling over while price churns — that would mean the high-volume 07-15/07-17 buyers are no longer adding.

**What would make me more aggressive:** a quick reclaim of $333.74 accompanied by volume ≥ 70M and MACD hist re-expanding above 2.5, which would convert the current pause into a classic bull-flag continuation.
