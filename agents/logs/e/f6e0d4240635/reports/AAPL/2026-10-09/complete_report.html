9. Signals, Red Flags, and What to Monitor
Green flags (evidence-backed)
  1. Gross margin above 50% in Q3. This is the headline. A 357bp YoY expansion on 16.4% revenue growth is very hard to achieve in hardware and implies genuine pricing power and/or a favorable mix.
  2. Revenue growth of 16.2% for nine months — an order of magnitude above the ~5–8% Apple historically delivers. Whatever is driving it (new form factor, refreshed cycle, pricing), it is durable across three consecutive quarters.
  3. R&D +32.5% fully absorbed by gross margin. Apple has room to keep investing.
  4. Working capital flipped from −$17.7B to +$0.5B, a $18.2B swing that drove the OCF surge.
  5. Net cash $62.2B, zero new LT debt issued, $14.4B repaid. Downside protection is unusually strong.
  6. SBC intensity falling (2.89% vs 3.09% of revenue) — earnings quality improving.
  7. AR down 21% YoY while revenue rose 16% — no channel stuffing at the receivables line.
  8. Finished-goods inventory down 4.1% YoY — consistent with genuine sell-through, not pull-forward.
Red/amber flags
  1. Tax rate normalization to ~17.6%. Structural, not one-off. Costs ~$2.7B per nine months vs the old rate. Every forward EPS model needs this.
  2. Inventory +94% YoY, raw materials +260%. Bullish if it precedes a launch; a write-down risk if demand disappoints. Monitor Q4 inventory turnover and any reserve build.
  3. ~$13B intangible addition with no explanatory footnote in the data. Likely an acquisition; verify in the 10-Q. Adds ~$3.3B/yr of amortization.
  4. Buyback decelerated 12%. EPS growth is becoming more operationally dependent — good for quality, but it removes a mechanical tailwind if operating growth slows.
  5. Amortization of intangibles + higher tax + R&D growth are three simultaneous drags that gross margin is currently absorbing. A 200bp gross-margin reversal would compress operating margin by roughly that much.
  6. Capex fell 28% to 1.87% of revenue. Fine for an asset-light model, but it means Apple is not building owned AI infrastructure — its AI capacity depends on partners and opex. Watch whether R&D growth is sustained without a capex ramp; if capex starts rising sharply, margin guidance changes.
  7. Insider activity unknown. No data. Do not treat absence of insider selling as a positive signal.
