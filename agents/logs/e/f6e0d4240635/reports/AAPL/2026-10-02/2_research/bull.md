The market keeps asking whether AAPL can monetize health and wearables. The news flow around Apple Watch heart tracking, a Health app redesign, “health age,” and Readiness scores is early, but it supports the bigger point: AAPL has a massive, sticky installed base and can layer software and health features into hardware refresh cycles. That is exactly the kind of ecosystem leverage that drives services and accessory revenue over time.

The bear may say, “That’s not quantified.” Fair. But the core business already is quantified: **Q3 gross margin of 50.1%, up 357 bps YoY**, and **9M gross margin of 49.1%**. AAPL is not relying on a speculative new product to justify the bull case. The existing franchise is throwing off cash and expanding margins.

### 5. The bear’s inventory and margin concerns are valid to watch — not thesis killers

Here is where I’ll give the bear some ground, because a good bull case should not pretend risks don’t exist.

**Inventory is up 94%** from FY2025 year-end. But look at the composition:

- Raw materials: **+259.9%**
- Finished goods: **-4.1%**
- Inventory days on TTM COGS: roughly **17 days**

That is not a demand warning yet. That is a raw-material pre-build, consistent with a product-cycle ramp. If finished goods start rising while revenue decelerates, I’ll get cautious. But today, the data says preparation, not obsolescence.

**Opex grew 22.9% in Q3, faster than revenue.** True. But Q3 operating margin still expanded **263 bps** to 32.6%, and incremental operating margin was roughly **49%**. R&D rose **32.3%**, which is an investment cycle, not a bloated cost structure. The bear can call it a margin risk; I’d call it a company funding future growth while still expanding profitability.

**Gross margin may normalize.** Sure. But Q3 gross margin was 50.1%, 9M was 49.1%, and the incremental gross-profit conversion is excellent. If gross margin reverts to the high-40s, the stock may need to reset expectations — but that is a “watch Q4” issue, not a reason to sell a fortress-balance-sheet franchise at a consolidating technical level.
