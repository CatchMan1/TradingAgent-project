1. Executive Summary

The fundamental case is the core of this. AAPL is not growing in line with a mature hardware company. Through nine months of FY2026, revenue grew 16.15% to $364.4B, gross profit rose 21.7%, operating income rose 21.7%, net income rose 20.0%, and diluted EPS rose 22.4% to $6.88.
