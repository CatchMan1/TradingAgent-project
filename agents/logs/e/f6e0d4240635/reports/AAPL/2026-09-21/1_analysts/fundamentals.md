## 1. Bottom Line

AAPL's most recent reported fundamentals show a **broad-based, accelerating growth and margin cycle** that is materially stronger than the prior year:

- **9M FY26 revenue $364.4B, +16.2% YoY** — roughly 2.5x the growth rate implied by FY25's trajectory.
- **Gross margin 49.1% for 9M, and 50.06% in Q3 FY26** (+357 bps YoY) — the strongest gross margin in the dataset.
- **Q3 diluted EPS $2.02, +28.7% YoY**, with growth accelerating each quarter of FY26 (+18.3% → +21.8% → +28.7%).
- **Free cash flow $110.2B for 9M (+52.5% YoY)**; net cash position more than doubled to **$62.2B**.
- Two items require due diligence before the Q4 print: a **+94% inventory build (raw materials +260%)** and an **unexplained +$12.1B increase in net intangible assets alongside a +$13.5B rise in other non-current liabilities**.

**Important timing note:** there have been **no new SEC filings in the past week**, and AAPL is in its pre-earnings quiet period. FY26 Q4 closes ~2026-09-26; results are expected **late October 2026**. This report is therefore a stock-take of Q3 FY26 fundamentals, not a reaction to fresh news.

## 7. Working-Capital Deep Dive — The Two Anomalies

### (a) Inventory +94% — the single most important forward signal
- Inventory rose from $5.72B to $11.09B; **raw materials +$5.52B (+260%)**, while **finished goods fell 4.1%**.
- Inventory days (inventory ÷ 9M COGS, annualized) rose to **~16 days from ~9 days**.
- **Interpretation A (bullish):** a deliberate component/raw-material pre-build ahead of a major product ramp — consistent with the Q3 print's 50% gross margin (favorable component costs locked in) and with tariff/supply-chain buffering.
- **Interpretation B (bearish):** a demand miss forming, with components bought but not converting to sell-through.
- **The tie-breaker is the Q4 print (late Oct):** finished goods should rise and then revenue should convert at ≥49.5% gross margin. If raw materials keep climbing while finished goods stay flat and revenue decelerates, treat it as a red flag.

### (b) Intangibles +$12.1B net / other non-current liabilities +$13.5B
- Gross intangibles rose **$13.27B** while accumulated amortization rose only **$1.15B**, so net intangibles nearly doubled to $25.4B (6.6% of assets).
- Simultaneously, other non-current liabilities rose **$13.53B (+32.6%)**.
- The vendor cash-flow extract shows only **$1.78B** of "other investing" outflow and **no separately disclosed business-acquisition line**, so this combination is **not fully explained by the data provided**.
- **Most likely explanations:** a sizable IP/technology licensing arrangement, an acquisition with deferred/stock consideration, or a long-dated tax/regulatory accrual.
- **Action:** this is the #1 question for management on the Q4 call. A $12–13B technology acquisition would be a strategic catalyst (AI capability) but also an integration/capital-allocation risk.

### (c) Receivables — a genuine positive
- AR fell 21% while revenue rose 16%; DSO (AR ÷ quarterly revenue × 91) fell to **~26 days from ~38.5 days** YoY.
- Nontrade receivables also fell 17%. This is a large, high-quality cash contributor and supports the 115% CFO/net-income conversion.

## 12. Actionable Insights for Traders

1. **Bias: constructive into the late-October FY26 Q4 print.** The estimate-revision setup is favorable: Q3 beat the growth trajectory of both prior quarters on revenue, gross profit, pre-tax income and EPS. Three consecutive quarters of *acceleration* is the strongest fundamental signal in this dataset.
2. **Set explicit confirmation thresholds for Q4:** gross margin **≥49.5%** (Q3: 50.06%), operating expense growth **≤20%** (Q3: +22.9%), effective tax rate **≤18%**, and **finished-goods inventory rising while raw materials stabilize**. Missing two or more would invalidate the margin thesis.
3. **Do not extrapolate the +94% inventory build in either direction yet.** It is the single highest-variance item. Position sizing should assume an information event on inventory at the Q4 call.
4. **Discount headline EPS growth for tax.** The 219 bps effective-rate increase is structural, not transient. Underlying operating EPS growth is ~25–26%; this makes the underlying business look *better*, not worse, but it means FY27 consensus needs the higher tax rate baked in.
5. **Price in a rising R&D trajectory.** +32.5% R&D growth is the main FY27 margin risk. As long as gross margin holds ≥49%, it is self-funding. If gross margin mean-reverts toward 47%, R&D growth becomes an EPS problem.
6. **Watch for M&A.** $62B net cash, $110B 9M FCF, a 12% slower buyback, zero new debt issuance, +$12.1B intangibles and +$13.5B other non-current liabilities together point to a large pending or completed non-cash transaction. An AI-related acquisition would be a positive strategic catalyst with integration risk; the market's reaction will depend heavily on price and financing.
7. **Balance sheet removes the tail risk.** Current ratio 1.0x, debt/equity 0.78x, net cash $62B. Downside fundamental risk is earnings-driven, not solvency-driven.
8. **Timing/seasonality:** FY26 Q4 closes ~2026-09-26 and typically captures only ~1–2 weeks of the new iPhone cycle. **The December-quarter guide matters more than the September quarter's reported revenue.** Position for the guide, not the print.
9. **Nothing new this week.** No filings, no insider data. Do not attribute unrelated price action to fundamentals; the next hard information is the late-October release plus the FY26 10-K.
