The bear's "AI bubble" argument (Dalio, Ives) is a macro opinion about the sector. It is not a company-specific finding. Meanwhile the company-specific evidence — accelerating revenue, expanding gross margin, external TPU sales, 22-year nuclear power — all points the other way.

The market is treating the investment as a cost; it should be treating it as a down payment on the next leg of growth.
