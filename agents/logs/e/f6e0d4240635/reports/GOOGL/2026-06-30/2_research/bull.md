The 200-SMA has climbed every session into June 30: 313.85 → 314.42 → 315.01. That is not a distribution top. That is a growth franchise shaking out.

Gross margin rose to **62.4%**. That is best-in-class scale and still-accelerating growth. Yes, headline EPS of $5.11 was inflated by roughly **$36.9B of non-cash equity-securities gains** — I’m not going to defend that as operating earnings. But strip it out, and core net income was still around **$32.6B, up roughly 24% YoY**. The bull case does not require the inflated GAAP number. The bull case is: revenue +22%, operating income +30%, margins expanding, operating cash flow +26.7% to $45.8B, and a fortress balance sheet.

That is heavy, but it is funding data-center and compute capacity into an AI demand cycle that the news flow says is still early. Google Cloud is a credible AI leverage point, and the broader AI buildout narrative remains supportive. If GOOGL is spending this aggressively, it is because management sees demand worth chasing — not because it has run out of growth.

**Positive market indicators are lining up.** GOOGL just debuted in the Dow, replacing Verizon, and the stock popped roughly **5% intraday** on June 29. The Dow is price-weighted, so a high-priced GOOGL commands meaningful index weight and forces passive funds to buy. Could part of that flow fade? Sure. Index inclusion is short-term icing. Meanwhile, the tape is risk-on for tech: Nasdaq +2.1%, Dow above 52,000, AI/cloud tailwinds, and yet the Fear & Greed Index was still just **26.5** — “Fear.” That tells me sentiment is cautious, not euphoric.
