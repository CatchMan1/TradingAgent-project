  • Trigger B (retest with divergence): a pullback into 318–325 that holds, with RSI making a higher low above ~40 and a bullish candle at the Bollinger lower band. That is the higher-quality entry, with a stop under 314.90.

For traders already short: The histogram improvement, the band re-entry, and the 200 SMA reclaim argue for tightening stops to protect gains near the 341–342 zone; pressing shorts into support at 318–325 has poor expectancy.

Key risk event: The 7/23 and 4/30 gaps both show the signature of information-driven repricing. Another such event can invalidate any technical read overnight — position sizes should assume gap risk well beyond ATR, since ATR understated the 7/23 move (an ~24-point gap versus an ~11-point ATR).
