## 1. "The P&L is validating the capex" — no, the P&L is *leading* the capex, and the lead is closing

You cite +160bps y/y operating margin. I'll raise you **−210bps sequentially**, and I'll tell you why it matters more.

Q1'26: revenue $109.9B, op income $39.7B, **36.1% margin.**
Q2'26: revenue $119.8B, op income $40.8B, **34.0% margin.**

Opex went from $28.9B to $33.1B — **+14.3% in one quarter** — against 9.0% revenue growth. That is the first hard datapoint in this entire file showing the AI cost ramp hitting the income statement, and it arrived in the same quarter you're holding up as proof that it isn't. SBC is +32.2% y/y at 6.6% of revenue, growing materially faster than the top line. Depreciation is already +43.2% y/y — *before* the $80.6B of H1 capex has meaningfully turned on. Capex/depreciation at 5.9x means D&A must roughly **triple** over the next 8–12 quarters even if capex freezes tomorrow. On H1 revenue, a move from 5.9% to 15% of revenue in D&A is **~$21B/year of incremental operating cost — roughly 400bps of margin, or ~$6 of your $10.75 core EPS.**

So your 24% revenue growth and your margin expansion are not the same trade. You are capitalizing a margin trajectory that the next four quarters are structurally biased against. When you say "the P&L is already validating the investment," what the P&L actually says is: *the investment is just now starting to bill us.*

## 3. "Reported EPS is noisy but the marks are real hidden value" — the marks are already in the book, and the risk is symmetric

You call the $124.3B portfolio "hidden asset value" and "a call option." Look at the carrying value itself: **cumulative upward price adjustments went from $44.5B to $85.7B in six months.** That is not a hidden asset trading at a discount — that is an asset that has been marked *up* 93% and is now carried at $124.3B on the balance sheet, having flowed $113.6B of "other" remeasurement gains through the income statement. The easy mark-ups are done. From here the same line item is a **levered short position on private AI sentiment** with no operating offset. A 30% drawdown in that complex is a ~$37B pre-tax hit — roughly 1.4 quarters of your entire core operating income, gone, with zero change in the actual business.

And note what those marks do to your return metrics: the report derives **~26% annualized core ROE on an equity base that just grew ~$200B, largely from revaluation.** That's not improving returns on capital. That's the denominator inflating while the numerator is a mark.

These are illiquid. You cannot monetize $124B of private AI equity at carrying value in a down market. Which means the "hidden asset" supports the book value and the reported EPS, but it does not support the *cash flow*, and cash flow is what's broken.
